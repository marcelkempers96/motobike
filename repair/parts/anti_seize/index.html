Anti-seize compound

Also called: copper grease, never-seez type, anti-seize paste

Find this part →

Anti-seize is a paste that stops threads and shafts rusting or welding together. It makes the next job easier.

When to use it

  • Shafts and bolts that are outdoors in the weather: axles, wheel nuts and exposed bolts.
  • Spark plug threads in an aluminium head, if the plug maker allows it. Check the manual or the plug box: some makers say not to.

What to know

  • Use a thin coat only. Anti-seize acts as a lubricant, so a torque value may need to be lower: follow the product label and manual.
  • Keep it off brake discs, pads, tyres and clutch plates.
  • Use it on a clean part.
