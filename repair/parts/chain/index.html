Problems it can cause

  • Handlebar is not straight when the wheel is straightDo not ride
  • Bike feels held back, as if something is draggingRide with care
  • Brake drags and the wheel does not spin freelyDo not ride
  • Chain is dry, rusty or has stiff linksRide with care
  • Chain jumps teeth or comes off the sprocketsStop now
  • Chain is noisy, clattering or slappingRide with care
  • Chain rubs on the guard, slider or swingarmRide with care
  • Chain has snappedStop now
  • Chain needs adjusting very oftenRide with care
  • Chain is tight in some places and slack in othersRide with care
  • Loud clunk when selecting first gearOK to ride
  • Clunk or snatch from the rear wheel when opening and closing the throttleRide with care
  • Bike judders or jerks when pulling awayRide with care
  • Motor gets very hot or power drops after a long climbRide with care
  • Grinding, clicking or growling from the motorRide with care
  • Rear wheel is locked or very hard to pushDo not ride
  • Whining noise in one or more gearsRide with care
  • New or increased engine vibrationRide with care
  • Oil leaking behind the front sprocketRide with care
  • Struggles to climb hills or carry a passengerRide with care
  • Bike pulls to one sideRide with care
  • Clunk from the rear suspensionDo not ride
  • Engine revs rise but the bike does not speed upRide with care
  • Oil leaking from a rear shockRide with care
  • Vibration at certain speedsRide with care
  • Sprocket teeth are hooked, sharp or brokenRide with care
  • Sudden loss of power while ridingDo not ride
  • Surges or jerks at steady cruising speedRide with care
  • Swingarm moves sideways or has playDo not ride
  • Bike weaves at high speedDo not ride
  • Rumbling or grinding from a wheelRide with care
