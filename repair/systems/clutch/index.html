Repair / Clutch

System

Clutch

Problems, repairs and parts for the clutch.

Problems

  • Semi-automatic clutch (step-through) slips or engages roughlyRide with care
  • Clutch drags: the bike creeps or lurches when in gear with the lever pulledRide with care
  • Clutch grabs or judders when pulling awayRide with care
  • Clutch lever has no resistance or the cable has snappedDo not ride
  • Rattle or whir from the clutch that changes when the lever is pulledRide with care
  • Clutch slips: revs rise but the bike does not pullRide with care
  • Hard to find neutralOK to ride
  • Clutch lever is very heavy to pullRide with care
