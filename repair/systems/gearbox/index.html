Gearbox and gear change

Problems, repairs and parts for the gearbox and gear change.

Problems

  • Loud clunk when selecting first gearOK to ride
  • Finds false neutrals between gearsRide with care
  • Gear lever is loose, bent or slipping on its shaftRide with care
  • Gear lever does not spring backRide with care
  • Whining noise in one or more gearsRide with care
  • Gears are hard to selectRide with care
  • Jumps out of gear under loadRide with care
  • Neutral light does not work or stays on in gearOK to ride
  • Stuck in one gearDo not ride
