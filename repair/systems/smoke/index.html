Problems

OK to rideRide with careDo not rideStop now

  • Smoke or oil mist from the crankcase breatherRide with care
  • Exhaust blocked or choked with carbon (two-stroke)Ride with care
  • Exhaust pipe glows red or the catalytic converter overheatsStop now
  • Ticking or puffing from an exhaust leakRide with care
  • Exhaust has become much louderRide with care
  • Popping or banging from the exhaust when slowing downRide with care
  • Rattling from the exhaust or heat shieldRide with care
  • Oil dripping from the exhaust (two-stroke)Ride with care
  • White smoke or steam from the exhaustRide with care
  • Engine still smokes after a rebuild with new ringsRide with care
