Controls and cables

Problems, repairs and parts for the controls and cables.

Problems

  • A control cable is frayed or stiffDo not ride
  • Choke lever or cable does not workRide with care
  • Brake or clutch lever is loose, bent or brokenDo not ride
  • Foot pedal or footpeg is stiff, bent or looseRide with care
  • Speedometer cable is noisy or brokenOK to ride
  • Throttle has too much free playRide with care
  • Throttle sticks or does not snap shutStop now
