Problems

OK to rideRide with careDo not rideStop now
  • Bike feels held back, as if something is draggingRide with care
  • Uses more fuel than normalOK to ride
  • Engine feels weak and down on powerRide with care
  • Will not reach normal top speedOK to ride
  • Pinging or rattling sound when accelerating (pinking)Ride with care
  • Struggles to climb hills or carry a passengerRide with care
  • Power fades as the engine gets hotRide with care
  • Engine revs rise but the bike does not speed upRide with care
  • Sudden loss of power while ridingDo not ride
