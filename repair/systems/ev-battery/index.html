Electric battery and charging

Problems, repairs and parts for the electric battery and charging.

Problems

  • Small 12 V battery is flatDo not ride
  • Battery shuts off suddenly (BMS protection)Do not ride
  • Battery percentage jumps or drops suddenlyRide with care
  • Charging stops before the battery is fullOK to ride
  • Charger cable or plug is damagedOK to ride
  • Charger shows a fault light or stays offOK to ride
  • Poor range or slow charging in the coldOK to ride
  • Battery pack is dropped, dented, swollen or wetStop now
  • Battery pack is hot, hissing or smellsStop now
  • Charge plug or socket gets hotDo not ride
  • Range has droppedOK to ride
  • Swapped battery is not recognisedDo not ride
  • Battery will not chargeDo not ride
