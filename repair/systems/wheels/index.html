Wheels and tyres

Problems, repairs and parts for the wheels and tyres.

Problems

  • Air leaking where the tyre meets the rimRide with care
  • Spokes are loose or brokenRide with care
  • Flat tyre on a wheel with an inner tubeDo not ride
  • Flat tyre on a tubeless wheelDo not ride
  • Rim is dented or crackedDo not ride
  • Tyre slowly loses airRide with care
  • Vibration at certain speedsRide with care
  • Tyre has a bulge or cutStop now
  • Tyre worn flat in the centreOK to ride
  • Tyre is cracked or oldRide with care
  • Tyre worn into a scalloped or cupped patternRide with care
  • Air leaking from the tyre valveRide with care
  • Rumbling or grinding from a wheelRide with care
  • Wheel moves sideways on the axleDo not ride
  • Wheel is buckled or wobblesDo not ride
  • Wheel hub or brake drum is very hot after ridingDo not ride
  • New tube goes flat soon after fitting a tyreDo not ride
