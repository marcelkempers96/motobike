Repair / Steering

System

Steering

Problems, repairs and parts for the steering.

Problems

  • Handlebar is not straight when the wheel is straightDo not ride
  • Clunk from the front when brakingDo not ride
  • Handlebars shake or wobbleStop now
  • Steering feels heavyRide with care
  • Steering is notchy or catches in the centreRide with care
  • Bike pulls to one sideRide with care
  • Bike weaves at high speedDo not ride
