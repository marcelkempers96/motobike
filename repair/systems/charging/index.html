Battery and charging

Problems, repairs and parts for the battery and charging.

Problems

  • Battery terminals are corrodedRide with care
  • Battery goes flat after the bike is parkedRide with care
  • Battery is boiling, swollen or leakingStop now
  • Lights go dim at idleRide with care
  • A new battery goes flat quicklyRide with care
  • Battery is not charging while ridingRide with care
  • Battery overcharges and bulbs blowDo not ride
  • Something drains the battery with the key offRide with care
  • Regulator is very hot or the connector has meltedDo not ride
  • Burnt smell from the generator or a dark stator connectorDo not ride
