Oil and leaks

Problems, repairs and parts for the oil and leaks.

Problems

  • Oil is milky or creamyDo not ride
  • Oil turns black very soon after a changeOK to ride
  • Oil leaking from an engine side coverRide with care
  • Oil leaking from the drain plugDo not ride
  • Oil leaking from the cylinder head or base gasketRide with care
  • Oil leaking behind the front sprocketRide with care
  • Oil leaking under the bikeRide with care
  • Oil level drops but there is no visible leakRide with care
  • Too much oil in the engineRide with care
  • Oil smells of petrol or the level risesDo not ride
  • Oil pressure or oil level light is onStop now
  • Two-stroke oil tank level is not going downDo not ride
