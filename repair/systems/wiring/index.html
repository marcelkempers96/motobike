Problems

  • Problems after fitting accessories or an alarmRide with care
  • Burning smell from the wiringStop now
  • Fuse keeps blowingDo not ride
  • Ignition switch is stiff or cuts powerDo not ride
  • Electrical faults come and goRide with care
  • Nothing happens when the key is turned onDo not ride
  • Handlebar switch does not work properlyRide with care
  • Electrical faults after rain or washingRide with care
