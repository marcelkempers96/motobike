Frame, stands and bodywork

Problems, repairs and parts for the frame, stands and bodywork.

Problems

  • Rattling from panels, mudguards or the seatOK to ride
  • Centre stand is hard to use or scrapesOK to ride
  • The bike has been dropped or crashedDo not ride
  • Crack in the frame or a bracketDo not ride
  • Mirror is loose or will not stay in placeRide with care
  • Seat is loose or the seat lock does not workRide with care
  • Side stand spring is weak or the stand is looseRide with care
