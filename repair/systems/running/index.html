Idle and running

Problems, repairs and parts for the idle and running.

Problems

  • Bogs down or chokes at full throttleRide with care
  • Cuts out when riding over bumpsRide with care
  • Cuts out randomly, then restartsRide with care
  • Misfires or dies in heavy rainRide with care
  • Engine dies when the side stand moves or the bike is put in gearRide with care
  • Flat spot in the middle of the rev rangeOK to ride
  • Revs hang or drop slowly after closing the throttleRide with care
  • Hesitates or stumbles when opening the throttleRide with care
  • Idle speed is too highRide with care
  • Idle speed rises and falls on its ownRide with care
  • Misfires or stutters when accelerating hard or climbingRide with care
  • Rough, lumpy or uneven idleRide with care
  • Engine runs hot and lean, pops and pinksRide with care
  • Engine runs rich, smells of fuel and smokes blackRide with care
  • Engine stalls at idle or at junctionsRide with care
  • Engine stalls once it is fully warmRide with care
  • Surges or jerks at steady cruising speedRide with care
  • Misfire: ignition or fuel?Ride with care
  • Stumbles or dies when the throttle is snapped open (CV carburettor)Ride with care
  • Two-stroke is hard to start or weak at low revs (reed valve fault)Ride with care
  • Two-stroke is weak at one end of the rev range (power valve)Ride with care
  • Runs rich or lean after changing the air filter or airboxRide with care
