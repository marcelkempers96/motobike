Fuel system

Problems, repairs and parts for the fuel system.

Problems

  • No fuel reaching the carburettorDo not ride
  • Fuel pours out of the carburettor overflow pipeDo not ride
  • Choke is stuck on or will not workRide with care
  • Unstable idle after cleaning or battery disconnection (fuel-injected)Ride with care
  • Engine warning light (check engine, MIL) is on or flashingRide with care
  • Fuel cap is stuck, leaks or will not lockRide with care
  • Fuel gauge reads wrong or does not moveOK to ride
  • Fuel is leaking or drippingStop now
  • Fuel pump whines loudly or runs all the timeRide with care
  • No buzz from the fuel pump when the key is turned onDo not ride
  • Strong smell of fuel around the bikeDo not ride
  • Engine stops and only runs on reserve or after a restRide with care
  • Fuel injector suspected blocked or leakingRide with care
  • Rust or flakes inside the fuel tankRide with care
  • Old or stale fuel in the tankRide with care
  • Engine stops after a few kilometres and the cap hisses when openedRide with care
  • Water or dirt in the fuelDo not ride
