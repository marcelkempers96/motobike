Yamaha MT-125: repairs, problems and specs

The MT-125 is the learner-legal 125 cc entry in Yamaha's MT naked range, first listed for 2014 to 2015. It uses a liquid-cooled single with variable valve actuation and a 6-speed gearbox, and later versions gained a TFT display and ABS.
