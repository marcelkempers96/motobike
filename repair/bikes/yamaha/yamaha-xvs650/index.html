Yamaha XVS650: repairs, problems and specs

The XVS650 is the Yamaha DragStar or V-Star 650 cruiser, produced between 1997 and 2017 around an air-cooled 649 cc 70-degree V-twin. The Custom has a very low seat at about 695 mm.
