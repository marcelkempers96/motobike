Royal Enfield Himalayan 411: repairs, problems and specs

The Himalayan 411 is Royal Enfield's air-cooled 411 cc adventure bike, built from 2016 and replaced by the Himalayan 450. It makes about 24 bhp and 32 Nm, with a 15 litre tank and 800 mm seat.
