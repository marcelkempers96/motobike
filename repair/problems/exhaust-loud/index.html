Smoke and exhaust

Exhaust has become much louder

Ride with careAlso called: noisy exhaust, blown silencer, loud muffler, broken baffle

The silencer (muffler) quietens the exhaust with chambers, perforated tubes and sometimes sound-absorbing packing. A sudden or gradual rise in noise usually means a leaking joint, a hole rusted through, a cracked weld, a baffle that has broken loose inside the silencer, or packing that has burned away. Road bikes must meet noise rules; the K-Light was approved to UNECE Regulation 41, and a loud exhaust can fail a roadside or annual check.

Is it safe to ride?

Yes with care for a short time. Stop if the pipe is hanging loose, touching the tyre or chain, or if you smell exhaust fumes at a standstill.

Quick checks first

  • Look along the pipe for holes, cracks, soot streaks and loose clamps.
  • Shake the cold silencer: does something rattle inside?
  • Has the bike been dropped or hit a kerb?
  • Has anyone removed the baffle or end cap?

Diagnose it step by step

1Engine cold. Look at the whole exhaust from the head to the outlet. Is there a visible hole, crack or broken weld?

  • Yes: the pipe or silencer needs welding or replacement. A temporary exhaust bandage can get you home. Do not patch the catalytic converter section.
  • No: go to step 2.

2Start the engine outside. Is the extra noise a sharp tick or puff at a joint?

  • Yes: go to Ticking or puffing from an exhaust leak and Find and fix an exhaust leak.
  • No, it is a general roar or a deep blare: go to step 3.

3Shake the cold silencer and tap it. Is there a rattle inside?

  • Yes: an internal baffle has broken. The silencer usually cannot be repaired by hand; replace it with one approved for your bike.
  • No: go to step 4.

4Does your silencer have a removable end cap or baffle? Is it missing or loose?

  • Missing or loose: refit or replace it. Riding without it is often illegal and can upset the mixture.
  • Present: go to step 5.

5Is the silencer a packed type (common on two-strokes and sport bikes), and does a puff of fibre or a sharp exhaust note come from it?

  • Yes: the packing has burned away. A workshop repacks it; two-strokes often need Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust and port at the same time.
  • No: go to step 6.

6. Check the mounts with Check exhaust mounts and heat shields. A loose silencer can open its joint under vibration.
