Controls and cables

Handlebar grip is loose or twists on the bar

Ride with careAlso called: grip slipping, grip turns on handlebar, loose rubber grip, grip coming off

A grip should not move on the bar or on the throttle tube. A grip that twists or slides makes the bike hard to control. On the right side it can also make the throttle feel vague or slow, because the grip turns before the throttle tube does.

Is it safe to ride?

Only slowly, to a place where you can fix it. If the right grip turns on the throttle tube, do not ride: you may not be able to close the throttle quickly.

Quick checks first

  • Twist each grip hard while holding the bar. Which one moves?
  • On the right, does the grip turn while the throttle tube stays still?
  • Is there oil, fuel or polish on the grip or bar?
  • Is the bar-end weight loose or missing?

Diagnose it step by step

1Is the left grip loose on the bar?

  • Yes: clean the bar and refit the grip with tape or glue (Remove and fit handlebar grips).
  • No: go to step 2.

2Does the right grip turn on the throttle tube?

  • Yes: refit or replace the grip on the tube (Remove and fit handlebar grips). Check the throttle snaps shut afterwards.
  • No: go to step 3.

3Does the whole throttle tube move along the bar, or rock?

  • Yes: the housing is loose, the tube is worn, or the bar end is missing. Tighten the housing and refit the bar end. Replace a worn tube (Strip, clean and lubricate a twist grip).
  • No: go to step 4.

4Does the throttle feel loose but the grip is firm?

  • Yes: this is free play, not a loose grip. Go to Throttle has too much free play.

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Oil or fuel under the grip Grip slides easily, feels greasy Clean and refit (grips)
Worn or stretched grip Grip loose even when clean New grips
Throttle tube worn or loose housing Tube rocks or slides on the bar Strip the grip
Bar-end weight missing Grip creeps outwards Refit the bar end
