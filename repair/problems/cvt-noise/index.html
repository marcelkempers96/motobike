Scooter automatic drive (CVT)

Noise from the scooter drive case

Ride with careAlso called: scooter drive rattle, CVT squeal, scooter belt noise, variator rattle

The long cover on the left of an automatic scooter holds the belt, the front pulley with its rollers (variator) and the clutch. Behind it at the wheel is a small gear case. Different noises point to different parts: a rattle that changes with engine speed often comes from rollers or worn clutch springs; a squeal from a slipping belt; a whine or growl that follows road speed from the gear case or wheel bearings; a knock or clatter that suddenly appears can mean something loose inside. For limits and settings, follow your scooter's manual.

Is it safe to ride?

Ride with care if the noise is a steady rattle or squeal. Stop riding if a new knock, clatter or grinding appears suddenly: a loose variator nut or broken part can wreck the case.

Quick checks first

  • Put the scooter on its centre stand. Does the noise happen at idle with the rear wheel still, or only when the wheel turns?
  • Push the scooter with the engine off: is there a whine or grind from the rear?
  • Check the gear oil level if your scooter has a level or dipstick.

Diagnose it step by step

1Engine off, push the scooter. Do you hear a whine, grind or rumble?

  • Yes: the noise follows road speed, not engine speed. Check the gear oil (Change scooter final drive oil) and look for metal on the drain bolt. Check the wheel bearings (Check wheel bearings) and for a dragging brake (Brake drags).
  • No: go to step 2.

2Did a loud knock, clatter or grinding start suddenly?

  • Yes: stop. Open the case (engine cold) and look for a loose nut, broken roller, broken fan or belt pieces. If parts are broken or loose, go to Check the scooter clutch and bell.
  • No: go to step 3.

3Is it a squeal, mainly when pulling away or accelerating?

  • Yes: the belt is slipping or glazed. Inspect and replace the scooter drive belt; see Scooter revs but accelerates slowly.
  • No: go to step 4.

4Is it a rattle that rises and falls with engine speed, worst at low speed or when the throttle is eased?

  • Yes: worn or flat-spotted rollers, or worn ramp sliders. Check variator rollers and sliders.
  • No: go to step 5.

5Is it a rattle at idle that stops when you open the throttle?

  • Yes: worn clutch shoe springs or a worn bell often rattle at idle. Check the scooter clutch and bell.
  • No: have an experienced mechanic listen with the case open and the engine running briefly, keeping well clear of moving parts.

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Low or old gear oil, worn gear case bearing Whine when pushed, engine off Change scooter final drive oil
Slipping or glazed belt Squeal on take-off Inspect and replace the scooter drive belt
Worn rollers or sliders Rattle with engine speed Check variator rollers and sliders
Worn clutch springs or bell Rattle at idle Check the scooter clutch and bell
Wheel bearing Rumble, play at the wheel Check wheel bearings
