Power and fuel use

Struggles to climb hills or carry a passenger

Ride with careAlso called: weak on hills, slows down with a passenger, labours uphill

On flat roads the bike seems fine, but on a hill, with a passenger or with cargo, it slows down, needs a lower gear than before, or cannot keep going. A hill or a heavy load asks for the most pulling force. Weak points that hide on the flat show up here: a slipping clutch, a soft rear tyre, a chain problem, or an engine that is already weak.

Is it safe to ride?

Yes, with care. Do not overload the bike; carry only what it is designed for, and shift down early on hills.

Quick checks first

  • Is the rear tyre pressure correct for the load? Many bikes need more pressure with a passenger.
  • Is the load within the bike's limit?
  • On a hill, do the revs rise without the bike going faster?
  • Has anyone changed the sprockets?
  • Is there a rattling sound (pinking) as you climb?

Diagnose it step by step

1On a hill in a middle gear, do the revs rise while the speed stays the same or drops?

  • Yes, manual clutch: see Clutch slips. Check clutch lever free play first (K-Light 125: 10 to 15 mm).
  • Yes, scooter: see Scooter revs but accelerates slowly.
  • Yes, step-through semi-automatic: see Semi-automatic clutch slips.
  • No: go to step 2.

2Does the engine misfire, jerk or rattle as you climb?

  • Misfire or jerking: Misfires when accelerating hard or climbing.
  • Light metallic rattle: Pinging or rattling when accelerating. Shift down and stop labouring the engine.
  • No: go to step 3.

3Are tyre pressure, chain slack and wheel spin correct?

  • Tyres: Check tyre pressure for the load.
  • Chain: Check and adjust chain slack. A chain that is too tight wastes power and wears the gearbox bearing.
  • Wheels that do not spin freely: Bike feels held back.
  • All fine: go to step 4.

4. Have the sprockets been changed? Count the teeth on the front and rear sprockets and compare with your manual (K-Light 125 rear: 41 teeth). A bigger front or smaller rear sprocket makes the bike faster on the flat but weaker on hills.

  • Changed: refit the standard sizes, with a new chain if worn.
  • Standard: go to step 5.

5Does the power fade only on long climbs, as the engine gets hot?

  • Yes: see Power fades as the engine gets hot.
  • No, it is weak in general: go to Engine feels weak and down on power. Check the air filter, spark plug and compression.

Use a road test on the same hill before and after each fix.
