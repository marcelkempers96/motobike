Brakes

Drum brake is weak

Do not rideAlso called: rear brake not working, drum brake poor, brake pedal goes down but little braking

A drum brake that needs a lot of force or travel and still slows the bike poorly. In a drum brake, a cam spreads two shoes against the inside of the drum. Weak braking comes from: shoes needing adjustment, worn or glazed shoes, oil or grease on the linings, water in the drum, a stiff cable or cam, or a cam that has turned so far it no longer pushes well.

Is it safe to ride?

No, until it is adjusted or serviced. A weak rear drum means you are relying on the front alone.

Quick checks first

  • Measure free play at the lever or pedal and compare with your manual.
  • Look at the wear indicator pointer on the brake arm with the brake applied: is it past the end of the mark?
  • Has the bike just been through deep water or washed? Brake gently a few times to dry it.
  • Did you just adjust the chain? Moving the rear wheel changes the brake rod length.

Diagnose it step by step

1Is the free play more than your manual allows?

  • Yes: Adjust a drum brake, then test.
  • No: go to step 2.

2After adjusting, is the wear pointer past its mark or the adjuster at the end of its thread?

  • Yes: the shoes are worn out. Service a drum brake.
  • No: go to step 3.

3With the brake applied, is the brake arm past a right angle to the rod or cable?

  • Yes: the cam has lost leverage; worn shoes or drum. Service a drum brake.
  • No: go to step 4.

4Is the cable or lever stiff, or slow to return?

  • Yes: much of your effort is lost in friction. Lubricate control cables; replace a frayed cable. See A control cable is frayed or stiff.
  • No: go to step 5.

5Is there oil or grease near the hub (leaking hub seal, chain lube, over-greased cam)?

  • Yes: oily shoes must be replaced, and the leak fixed. Service a drum brake.
  • No: go to step 6.

6When serviced, are the shoes glazed or the drum glazed, cracked or worn beyond its maximum diameter?

  • Glazed only: clean with fine abrasive paper and refit.
  • Worn shoes: replace as a pair.
  • Cracked or oversize drum: replace the hub.

7Does it get weak only on long hills?

  • Yes: see Brakes get weaker when hot.
