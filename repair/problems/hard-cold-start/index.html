Starting

Hard to start when the engine is cold

Ride with careAlso called: cold start problem, won't start in the morning, needs many kicks when cold, slow to start cold

The engine starts easily once warm, but on a cold morning it takes many tries. A cold engine needs a richer mixture because fuel does not evaporate well on cold metal. A carburettor bike gets this from the choke. A fuel-injected bike gets it from the ECU, which reads an engine temperature sensor. A cold engine also has thicker oil and a battery that gives less power. So the usual causes are the choke system, the battery, a weak spark, a small blocked jet, or a wrong temperature reading.

Is it safe to ride?

Yes once it is running and warmed up. Let it idle briefly before riding off.

Quick checks first

  • Carburettor: are you using the choke? Does the choke lever move freely and return?
  • Fuel-injected: K-Light 125 manual says not to touch the throttle when starting (PDF page 277). The ECU raises idle by itself on a cold engine.
  • Does the starter turn the engine at normal speed?
  • Has the bike stood for weeks with old fuel?
  • A cold engine does not vaporise fuel well. Liquid fuel does not burn, so the choke gives a very rich mixture so that part of it vaporises.
  • Fuel with a high alcohol share vaporises poorly in the cold. See Problems after using ethanol blend fuel.

Diagnose it step by step

1Does the engine crank at normal speed when cold?

  • No: cold weakens the battery and thickens the oil. Go to Engine turns over slowly.
  • Yes: go to step 2.

2Carburettor: does the choke work (lever moves the plunger or flap, and the engine runs faster with it on once started)?

  • No: Check the choke or starter valve and Choke is stuck on or will not work.
  • Yes, or fuel-injected: go to step 3.

3Is the spark plug clean, dry and correctly gapped?

  • No: Remove, check, gap and fit a spark plug. A sooty plug fires poorly when cold.
  • Yes: go to step 4.

4Is the fuel fresh (no sour or varnish smell, not left for months)?

  • No: see Old or stale fuel in the tank.
  • Yes: go to step 5.

5Carburettor: does it start on the choke but stall when you push the choke off?

  • Yes: the pilot (idle) jet is partly blocked. Clean the carburettor and see Starts only with the choke on or the throttle held open.
  • No: go to step 6.

6Fuel-injected: is there a fault code for the temperature sensor, or does the idle not rise when cold?

  • Yes: Check the engine sensors (TPS, MAP, temperature). K-Light 125: the ECU raises idle below 68 °C engine temperature; the head temperature sensor reads 1.9 to 2.1 kΩ at 25 °C.
  • No: go to step 7.

7Are the valve clearances and compression correct?

  • Have them checked: Check and adjust valve clearance, Test engine compression. Valves set too tight may not close fully, which loses compression and makes cold starting hard.

Cause How to confirm Fix
Choke not used or not working Starts with choke working Check the choke or starter valve
