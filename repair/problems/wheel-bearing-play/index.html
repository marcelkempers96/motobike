Wheels and tyres

Wheel moves sideways on the axle

Do not rideAlso called: wheel play, wobbly wheel bearing, wheel knocks side to side

When you lift the wheel and rock it at the top and bottom, it knocks or moves. You may also feel the bike weave, or hear a clunk over bumps. The usual cause is a worn wheel bearing, but a loose axle nut, a missing spacer, steering head play or swingarm play feel similar.

Is it safe to ride?

No. A wheel with side play can let the brake disc hit the caliper, make the bike weave, and in the worst case the bearing can collapse. Find and fix the cause before riding.

Quick checks first

  • Is the axle nut tight and its split pin in place?
  • Was the wheel recently removed? (Spacers left out or swapped.)
  • Does the play feel like a sharp knock or a soft movement?

Diagnose it step by step

1Check the axle nut is tightened to your bike's value. Was it loose?

  • Yes: tighten it (K-Light 125 front: 75 to 88 N·m; rear: confirm the value, the manual conflicts). Fit a new split pin. Check again. If the play has gone, check the spacers were fitted correctly.
  • No: go to step 2.

2Lift the wheel, rock it top and bottom, and watch closely where the movement is. Does the wheel move on the axle, or does the whole fork or swingarm move?

  • Wheel moves on the axle: go to step 3.
  • Front fork and wheel move together: steering head play. Check the steering head bearings.
  • Swingarm moves with the wheel: Swingarm moves sideways or has play.

3Remove the wheel (front, rear). Turn and push each bearing inner race with a finger. Rough, notchy, or loose?

  • Yes: Replace wheel bearings, both in the hub.
  • No: go to step 4.

4Is the centre spacer between the bearings present, and are the outer spacers in the right places?

  • No: fit them correctly. A missing centre spacer lets the axle crush the bearings.
  • Yes: go to step 5.

5Does the bearing move in the hub (outer race loose in its bore)?

  • Yes: the hub bore is worn. A workshop must repair or replace the hub.
  • No: check the axle for wear or bend, and recheck with the wheel refitted.

6Electric bike with a hub motor?

  • Yes: motor bearing play needs its own check: Check hub motor bearings and play.

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Loose axle nut Nut turns under torque wrench Tighten to value, new split pin
Worn wheel bearing Rough or loose inner race Replace wheel bearings
Missing or wrong spacer Spacer absent or swapped Refit correctly
Steering head play Fork moves with wheel Steering bearings
Swingarm play Swingarm moves with wheel Check the swingarm pivot
Worn hub bore Bearing loose in hub Workshop hub repair
