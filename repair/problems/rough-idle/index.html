Idle and running

Rough, lumpy or uneven idle

Ride with careAlso called: lumpy idle, shaking at idle, misfire at idle, uneven tickover

At idle the engine shakes, misses beats or sounds uneven, though it may run well on the move. At idle each fault shows clearly because the engine is turning slowly with little fuel. A tired spark plug, wrong idle mixture, an air leak or wrong valve clearance are the usual causes. On two-strokes, an oily plug is common.

Is it safe to ride?

Yes, with care. If it also misfires on the move or loses power, read Misfires when accelerating hard.

Quick checks first

  • Is the engine fully warm? Many engines are rough for the first minute.
  • Is the plug cap pushed fully onto the plug?
  • When was the plug last changed?
  • Is the air filter clean?
  • Is there a ticking noise from the top of the engine?

Diagnose it step by step

1Remove the spark plug. Is it black and sooty, wet with oil or fuel, or white?

  • Black and dry: rich idle. See Spark plug is black and sooty.
  • Wet with oil: see Spark plug is wet with oil.
  • White or blistered: lean. Go to step 4.
  • Light grey or tan, but the electrode is worn or the gap is wrong: service or replace the plug (K-Light 125: gap 0.7 ± 0.1 mm). Use Read a spark plug for help.

2With a new plug fitted, is the spark strong and blue in a spark tester?

  • No: Check for a spark safely and go to Weak, thin or orange spark.
  • Yes: go to step 3.

3Is the air filter clean?

  • No: clean or replace it.
  • Yes: go to step 4.

4Does the idle change when you spray water on the intake joints?

  • Yes: Find an intake air leak.
  • No: carburettor bikes go to step 5; fuel-injected go to step 6.

5Carburettor: does the pilot screw smooth the idle?

  • Yes: adjust idle speed and mixture.
  • No effect: clean the carburettor. Go to step 7 if still rough.

6Fuel-injected: is a code stored?

  • Yes: Read engine fault codes.
  • No: clean the throttle body. If still rough, the injector may be dirty: Fuel injector suspected blocked or leaking.

7Still rough with good spark and fuel?

  • Check valve clearance on a cold engine (K-Light 125: 0.05 to 0.07 mm, inlet and exhaust). Then a compression test. Low reading: Low compression.

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Worn, fouled or wrongly gapped plug Plug condition and gap Spark plug service
Loose plug cap or weak coil Weak spark in tester Spark test
Dirty air filter Filter clogged Air filter
Air leak Water spray changes idle Intake leak test
Idle mixture wrong or pilot jet blocked Pilot screw no effect Clean carburettor
Dirty throttle body or injector Deposits, codes Throttle body
Wrong valve clearance, low compression Feeler gauge, gauge reading Valve clearance
Hard or cracked air tube between airbox and carburettor Visible cracks, gap at clamp Replace the air tube
One starter plunger stuck on a multi-carburettor set One cylinder runs rich and rough Starter plunger check
