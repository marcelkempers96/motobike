Wheels and tyres

Tyre has a bulge or cut

Stop nowAlso called: bubble on tyre, lump in sidewall, cut tyre, egg on tyre

A bulge (a raised lump on the sidewall or tread) means the cords inside the tyre are broken, usually from hitting a pothole or kerb. Air pressure is pushing out through the weak spot. A cut that reaches the cords is just as weak. Either can burst without warning.

Is it safe to ride?

No. Stop now. A bulged tyre can blow out, and a front blowout often causes a crash. Do not add more air. Push the bike or arrange transport.

Quick checks first

  • Stop safely and look at the whole tyre, both sides.
  • Is there a lump, or a cut with threads visible?
  • Look at the rim next to the bulge: dented or cracked?
  • With a tube tyre, a lump can also be the tube pushing through a cut.

Diagnose it step by step

1Is there a raised lump that you can feel, on the sidewall or tread?

  • Yes: the tyre is finished. Do not ride. Replace a tyre. Go to step 4.
  • No: go to step 2.

2Is there a cut? Open it gently with a fingernail. Can you see cords or threads?

  • Yes: replace the tyre. Cuts to the cords cannot be plugged or patched safely.
  • No, only a shallow surface cut in the tread: ride gently to have it checked, and check the tyre before each ride (Inspect tyres for wear and damage).

3Is the "bulge" really a wavy, uneven bead line next to the rim?

  • Yes: the tyre is not seated on the rim. Deflate and have it reseated (Air leaking where the tyre meets the rim).
  • No: treat it as a bulge (step 1).

4When the wheel is off, check the rim at the place of the impact. Dented, cracked or bent?

  • Yes: go to Rim is dented or cracked. A new tyre on a damaged rim can fail too.
  • No: check runout before fitting the new tyre (Check a wheel for runout).

5Did the impact also hit the fork or the steering?

  • Yes, or unsure: after the new tyre is fitted, check the bars are straight and the bike runs straight (The bike has been dropped or crashed).

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Pothole or kerb impact, broken cords Lump on sidewall or tread Replace a tyre
Cut through to the cords Threads visible in cut Replace a tyre
Riding under-inflated, sidewall damage Low readings, bulge low on sidewall Replace, keep pressure correct
Bead not seated Uneven bead line, no lump Reseat bead
