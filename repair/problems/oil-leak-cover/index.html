Oil and leaks

Oil leaking from an engine side cover

Ride with careAlso called: clutch cover leak, generator cover leak, side case leak, crankcase cover gasket

The left cover usually covers the generator and the right cover the clutch, oil pump and often a centrifugal oil filter. Oil can leak at the gasket between cover and crankcase, round the shafts that pass through the cover (kickstart, gear lever, clutch lever) or through a crack from a fall. Covers are aluminium; loose screws and a gasket that has aged or been pinched are the common causes. Overfilled oil and a blocked breather raise the pressure inside and make small weak spots leak.

Is it safe to ride?

Yes with care for a seep. Do not ride if oil is running, the cover is cracked, or oil reaches the tyre or brake.

Quick checks first

  • Has the bike been dropped on that side? Look for scrapes and cracks.
  • Is the oil level above the upper mark?
  • Is the leak at the cover edge or round a shaft?
  • Was the cover recently removed for a repair?

Diagnose it step by step

1Clean and find the exact point with Trace an oil leak. Is it from a crack in the cover?

  • Yes: the cover needs replacing or welding by a workshop. A crack from a fall can grow fast. See The bike has been dropped or crashed.
  • No: go to step 2.

2Is it round a shaft (kickstart, gear lever, clutch arm)?

  • Yes: it is the shaft seal, not the gasket. Ask a workshop to replace the seal; the gear lever seal can usually be done without opening the case. Check the lever is not bent and pushing the shaft sideways.
  • No, along the cover edge: go to step 3.

3Is the oil level above the upper mark, or is the breather blocked?

  • Yes: correct the level with Check the engine oil level and clear the breather with Check the crankcase breather. Clean and recheck.
  • No: go to step 4.

4Are any cover screws loose? Check each by hand with the correct tool.

  • Yes: snug loose screws gently to the manual torque (K-Light 125: crankcase cover bolts 9.8 to 11 N·m). Do not overtighten; aluminium threads strip. Clean and recheck after a ride.
  • No, all tight: go to step 5.

5Was the cover off recently?

  • Yes: the gasket may be pinched or the wrong one. Return to the person who did the work.
  • No: the gasket has aged. Ask for Replace an engine cover gasket.
