Controls and cables

Foot pedal or footpeg is stiff, bent or loose

Ride with careAlso called: brake pedal sticks, bent footpeg, loose footrest, pedal does not return

A footpeg twists or wobbles under your foot, a folding peg will not fold or spring back, or the rear brake pedal is stiff, bent, or slow to return. A brake pedal that does not return fully keeps the rear brake dragging and the brake light on. This page covers footpegs and the rear brake pedal; gear and kickstart levers have their own pages.

Is it safe to ride?

With care for a loose footpeg once tightened. Do not ride if the brake pedal does not return, is bent so it is hard to use, or the peg bracket is cracked.

Quick checks first

  • Press and release the brake pedal: does it return fully to its stop?
  • Try to twist each footpeg.
  • Look at the brake light while pressing and releasing the pedal.
  • Look for cracks at the peg and pedal brackets.

Diagnose it step by step

1Is it the brake pedal, a footpeg, the gear lever or the kickstart?

  • Gear lever: Gear lever does not spring back or Gear lever is loose, bent or slipping.
  • Kickstart: Kickstarter will not move or does not spring back.
  • Brake pedal: go to step 2.
  • Footpeg: go to step 5.

2Is the brake pedal bent or cracked?

  • Yes: fit a new pedal. Do not straighten it. Then go to step 4.
  • No: go to step 3.

3Disconnect the brake rod or master cylinder pushrod. Does the pedal now move freely and return with its spring?

  • No: the pivot is dry or worn, or the return spring is weak. Clean, grease, and fit a new spring if needed (Service footpegs and foot pedals).
  • Yes: the stiffness is in the brake. Drum: a dry cam (Service a drum brake). Disc: Brake drags.

4Set pedal height and free play. Does the brake light go off when the pedal is released?

  • Set: Adjust the rear brake pedal or Adjust a drum brake. K-Light 125 free play: 20 to 30 mm.
  • Light stays on: Test and adjust the brake light switches.

5Footpeg: does the whole peg bracket move?

  • Yes: tighten the mounting bolts to the bike's value (K-Light 125 footrest bolt: 45 to 50 N·m). If it loosens again, look for a stripped thread or a crack (Crack in the frame or a bracket).
  • No: go to step 6.

6Folding peg stiff or floppy?

  • Yes: clean and grease the hinge pin, fit a new spring or split pin (Service footpegs and foot pedals). Replace a bent peg.

Causes, most likely first

Cause How to confirm Fix
Dry or worn pedal pivot Pedal stiff with rod disconnected Pedal service
Weak return spring Pedal returns slowly New spring
Loose peg bolts Bracket moves Fastener check
Bent pedal or peg after a fall Visible bend Replace part
Brake sticking Pedal free alone, stiff with brake Brake drag
