Oil and leaks

Oil pressure or oil level light is on

Stop nowAlso called: oil light on, oil pressure warning, oil can light, low oil warning

Some bikes have an oil pressure light: it comes on when the oil pump is not making enough pressure. Others have an oil level light (the K-Light manual mentions an oil sensor alarm for level) or, on two-strokes, a light for the oil tank. All three should light for a moment at key-on as a bulb test and go out once the engine runs. A light that stays on or comes on while riding is a warning to stop. There is no single pressure value for all bikes; testing pressure needs the model manual and a gauge.

Is it safe to ride?

No. Stop the engine as soon as it is safe. Running without oil pressure can destroy the engine in minutes.

Quick checks first

  • Stop and switch off.
  • Check the oil level (four-stroke) or the oil tank (two-stroke).
  • Look under the bike for a big oil loss.
  • Does the light come on at key-on and go out after starting?

Diagnose it step by step

1Is the oil level below the lower mark, or the two-stroke tank empty?

  • Yes: top up with the correct oil using Check the engine oil level. Look for a leak. If the light goes out at once after starting and there is no noise, ride gently and find out why the level was low. Two-stroke: go to Two-stroke oil tank level is not going down if the tank was full but the light was on.
  • No, the level is correct: go to step 2.

2Is there a new knock, rattle or rumble?

  • Yes: do not restart. Go to Deep knocking from the bottom of the engine.
  • No: go to step 3.

3Does the light flicker only at hot idle and go out above idle?

  • Yes: thin, old or wrong oil, or a worn pump. Change oil and clean the strainer with Change the engine oil and clean the strainer. If it continues, workshop.
  • No, it stays on at all revs: go to step 4.

4Is the wire on the oil pressure or level switch disconnected, broken or rubbing on the frame?

  • A wire touching earth turns the light on: repair it with Find a wiring fault.
  • Wire good: go to step 5.

5Workshop: the oil pressure must be measured with a gauge in place of the switch, following the model manual.

  • Pressure low: blocked strainer or centrifugal filter, worn oil pump, or blocked passage. The K-Light manual lists no or low pressure and a blocked oil passage as causes of seizure. See Assess bottom end and internal engine damage.
  • Pressure correct: the switch is faulty; replace it.

What happens if you keep riding: the bearings and cam surfaces lose their oil film and wear or seize within minutes.
