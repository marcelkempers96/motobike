Foam Air Cleaner

![005_center_font_size_4_b_foam_2.jpg](<005_center_font_size_4_b_foam_2.jpg>)

Air cleaners filter dirt from the air so it does not go into the engine. A Foam air cleaner can be cleaned and reused.
