Paper Air Cleaner

![005_center_font_size_4_b_paper_2.jpg](<005_center_font_size_4_b_paper_2.jpg>)

Air cleaners filter dirt from the air so it does not go into the engine. A Paper air cleaner cannot be reused and must be replaced.
