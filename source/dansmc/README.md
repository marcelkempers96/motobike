# Dan's Motorcycle Repair Course: reference material

Source: dansmc.com (Dan's online motorcycle repair course), as uploaded on 4 November 2026.
Kept here as reference only: `source/` is not deployed. Text, photos, diagrams and PDFs remain the
copyright of their owners. Moto Zero pages paraphrase the facts and procedures in our own words and
do not republish this material without permission.

Layout: `<section>/<lesson number>/` holds each lesson's page (`.md`, `.html`) and its images.
`_index/` holds the manifests (`manifest_all.csv`: every image with its page, caption and surrounding text).

Uploaded so far: lessons 001 to 025 (basics, tools, manuals) and 086 to 151 (electrical, engine,
frame, brakes, wheels, suspension). Lessons 026 to 085 and 152 to 157 were not in the upload.
