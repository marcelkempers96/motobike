# Lubricate or replace the throttle cable

**Home mechanic (basic tools)** · Time: 30 to 60 min (estimate) (estimate)


A throttle that is stiff, slow to close or sticks part-open is dangerous. The cause is usually a dry or frayed cable, a dirty grip tube on the handlebar, or a cable routed with a tight bend. This job cleans and lubricates the grip and cable, or fits a new cable.

## Safety
- Do not ride with a throttle that does not snap shut.
- Engine off and cold. You may need to lift or remove the fuel tank to reach the cable: work outside, no flames, catch any fuel.
- Some cables have a plastic lining and should not be oiled; check the maker's advice.

## You need
- Screwdrivers for the grip housing, spanners for the adjusters.
- Cable oil and a cable luber clamp, or a small plastic bag taped round the cable end as a funnel.
- A new cable of the same part number if the inner is frayed, kinked, or rusted, or the outer is crushed or cracked.

## Steps: grip and lubrication
1. Slacken the grip-end adjuster fully.
2. Open the throttle housing on the handlebar (usually two screws). Unhook the cable end from the grip tube.
3. Slide the grip tube off the bar. Clean the bar and the inside of the tube. Remove burrs. Check the bar-end weight is not pressing on the grip, and that grip glue is not on the tube.
4. Put a thin film of grease on the bar where the tube turns. Look at the cable end: broken strands near the nipple mean replace the cable.
5. Fit the luber or bag funnel on the top of the cable. Feed oil until it comes out of the bottom end. Work the inner cable in and out.
6. Refit the cable end, the tube and the housing. Make sure the housing does not pinch the tube.
7. Set free play: [Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust).

## Steps: replace the cable
1. Take photos of the cable route from grip to carburettor or throttle body, including every guide and clip.
2. Tape the new cable alongside the old one and follow the same route before you remove the old one, so the route stays correct.
3. Disconnect the old cable at both ends. At a slide carburettor, the cable end is in the slide under the top cap; keep the slide, needle and spring together and the right way up.
4. Connect the new cable at both ends. Lubricate it unless it is lined.
5. Secure it in the original guides. Keep it away from the hot engine and exhaust and away from tight bends.
6. Set free play.

## Check your work
- Grip snaps shut from full open with bars straight, full left and full right.
- Engine at idle: no rise in idle at either lock.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Snaps shut but idle still high: [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle) or [Clean the throttle body](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-body-clean).
- Sticky slide inside the carburettor: [Clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean).
