# Inspect the frame after a crash

**Workshop job (assessment and hand-off)** · Time: home assessment 30 to 45 min; workshop measurement 1 to 2 h (estimate) (estimate)


After a crash the obvious damage (a broken lever, scraped panel) is easy to see. The dangerous damage often is not: a twisted fork, a bent frame or a hairline crack at a weld can make the bike pull, weave or break later. This page is a careful home assessment followed by a workshop hand-off for measuring and repair.

## Safety
- Check yourself and any passenger first. A rider with a head knock should not ride.
- Turn the engine off. Lift the bike with help, bending your knees. Look for fuel, oil, brake fluid and battery acid leaks.
- Electric bike: if the battery pack is dented, swollen, wet inside, hot or smells, keep away from it and follow [Handle a damaged battery pack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ev-damaged-pack).
- Never weld on a frame with the fuel tank fitted or near the battery. Frame welding and straightening are specialist jobs.

## Home assessment (bike stationary)
1. Look at the frame closely with a torch: steering head, engine mount brackets, swingarm pivot, footpeg and stand brackets, rear sub-frame, every weld. Cracked or wrinkled paint at a joint can hide a crack.
2. Stand in front and look down: is the handlebar square to the wheel? See [Handlebar is not straight when the wheel is straight](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bars-misaligned).
3. Look at the forks from the front: legs parallel, mudguard centred. See [Fork legs are twisted or uneven](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fork-twisted) and [Align the front wheel, forks and handlebar](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/front-end-align).
4. Turn the bars lock to lock. Smooth, and the same angle each side. Unequal lock suggests a bent stem or damaged stops. Check [steering head bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/steering-bearing-check).
5. Wheel alignment: run a string line or two straight edges along both tyres at axle height. Both tyres should touch the line the same way on each side. A difference means bent parts or wrong chain adjuster settings.
6. Spin both wheels: check runout ([Check a wheel for runout](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-runout-check)) and brake discs.
7. Controls: levers, pedals, footpegs, gear lever, throttle snap-back, cables not pinched, switches and lights working.
8. Engine cases: cracks, oil leaks. Exhaust mounts. Swingarm straight.

## What the workshop should do
- Measure frame alignment on a jig or with laser tools: steering head, swingarm pivot and engine mounts.
- Measure axle bend (**K-Light 125 limit: 0.2 mm**) and rim runout (**K-Light 125 limit: 2.0 mm**).
- Crack-test suspect welds with dye penetrant.
- Replace bent fork tubes, triple clamps, handlebar, levers and pedals rather than straighten them.
- Decide whether the frame can be straightened by a frame specialist or must be replaced. Aluminium frames are usually replaced.

## What to ask for
- A written list of damage and measured values, for insurance and for you.
- A test ride report after repair: the bike must run straight hands-light at low speed and stop straight.

## Do not attempt at home
- Heating or hammering a frame, fork or wheel straight.
- Riding a bike that pulls to one side, has unequal steering lock or a cracked frame.
