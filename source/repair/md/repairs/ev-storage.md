# Store an electric motorcycle

**Rider job (no special tools)** · Time: 30 min to prepare; checks every month (estimate) (estimate)


If an electric motorcycle stands for weeks without care, the traction battery slowly loses charge and the small 12 V battery (if fitted) can go flat. A lithium battery left almost empty for a long time can be damaged and may refuse to charge. Good storage keeps the battery at a safe level and temperature. Follow your own manual first; the values below are one maker's advice, as an example.

## Safety
- Store and charge on a hard, non-flammable floor, away from exits and sleeping areas.
- Do not store a battery that is damaged, swollen or was in water. See [Handle a damaged battery pack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ev-damaged-pack).

## Steps
1. Clean and dry the bike. Do not use a pressure washer on the motor, controller or battery area.
2. Choose a place out of direct sun and rain. Indoor storage is best; outdoors, use a breathable, weather-resistant cover (Zero). Zero Motorcycles advises storing at **10 to 35 °C**.
3. Battery charge: follow your manual. As an example, Zero says to leave the bike plugged in so the BMS can manage the charge, and that its BMS lowers the battery to about **80%** after 30 days. If the bike is not plugged in and drops **below 30%**, Zero says to charge it.
4. Check the charge every month: Zero says to turn the key every **30 days** to check SoC.
5. 12 V battery: if your bike has one, keep it on a suitable maintenance charger, or disconnect it as the manual says. Some EVs cannot wake up with a flat 12 V battery even when the main battery is full (SolarQuotes). See [Check the 12 V auxiliary system](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ev-aux-check).
6. Tyres: pump to the correct pressure. Use the centre stand if you have one.
7. Swap-battery bikes: tell the operator if you will not ride for a long time. Gogoro, for example, asks riders to swap at least once every 30 days and to contact the network to suspend the plan if they will not ride for that long. Do not keep an operator's battery at home unless they say you may.
8. Before riding again: charge fully. Zero advises charging for **24 hours** before the first ride so the cells can balance. Then check brakes, tyres, lights and do a short test ride: [Pre-ride check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/pre-ride-check).

## Check your work
- The SoC stays at the level your manual recommends during storage.
- The bike wakes up and charges normally after storage.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Bike will not wake: [Small 12 V battery is flat](/moto-zero/repair/problems/ev-aux-battery-flat).
- Battery will not charge after storage: [Battery will not charge](/moto-zero/repair/problems/ev-wont-charge).
