# Pre-ride check

**Rider job (no special tools)** · Time: 2 min daily; 10 to 15 min weekly (estimate) (estimate)


A short check before riding finds most problems that cause roadside breakdowns and crashes: low tyres, weak brakes, a sticking throttle, dead lights, a slack chain. Do the **daily** check before the first ride of the day. Do the **weekly** check once a week, and before a long trip or a heavy load.

## Stop and do not ride if
- A brake lever or pedal goes soft or reaches the bar or footrest.
- The throttle does not snap shut by itself.
- A tyre has a bulge, a deep cut or is flat.
- Fuel is leaking, or there is a strong fuel smell.
- A wheel wobbles or has side play.
- An electric bike shows a battery warning, or the pack is hot, swollen or smells.

## Daily (about 2 minutes)
1. **Tyres:** look at both. Squeeze or kick them: both should feel equally firm. Look for nails and cuts.
2. **Brakes:** pull the front lever and press the pedal. Firm, with the free play your bike should have. Push the bike with each brake on: the wheel should not turn.
3. **Throttle:** twist and release with the bars straight and at full lock. It must snap shut.
4. **Lights and horn:** key on. Headlight, tail light, brake light (front lever and rear pedal), indicators both sides, horn. Warning lights come on and then go out as your manual says.
5. **Leaks:** look under the bike for fresh drips of fuel, oil or brake fluid.
6. **Stand:** side stand springs fully up.
7. **Electric bikes:** charge level, charge port cover closed, swap battery locked in ([Check swap battery contacts and latch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ev-swap-contact-check)), "ready" shows normally.

## Weekly (10 to 15 minutes)
1. Tyre pressures with a gauge: [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check). Tread and damage: [Inspect tyres](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-inspect).
2. Chain slack and lubrication: [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust), [Clean and lubricate the chain](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-clean-lube).
3. Engine oil level: [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check). Two-stroke oil tank level. Coolant level on liquid-cooled bikes: [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check).
4. Brake pads or drum wear indicator and fluid level: [Check brake pad wear](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-check), [Check brake fluid level and condition](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-fluid-check).
5. Cable free play: [throttle](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust), [clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust). Look for frayed cable ends.
6. Wheels: spin each, check for wobble and loose spokes ([Check and tighten spokes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spoke-tension)).
7. Mirrors, levers, footpegs, number plate, panels tight ([Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check)).
8. Battery terminals tight and clean ([Clean and tighten battery terminals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-terminal-clean)).
