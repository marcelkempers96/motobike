# Read a spark plug

**Home mechanic (basic tools)** · Time: 10 to 15 min, plus a short ride (estimate)


The colour and deposits on the plug's firing end show how the engine has been burning its fuel. MAVCC puts plug inspection first in any tune-up because the old plug gives clues. Reading a plug is a screening check, not proof: confirm the cause with a test before you replace a part.

## Safety
- Remove the plug from a cold or warm engine, not a hot one.
- Key OFF.

## Steps
1. For a fair reading, ride the bike for a few minutes at steady speed, then switch off with the key (do not let it idle long). Long idling and short trips make any plug look sooty.
2. Let it cool a little, then remove the plug: [Remove, check, gap and fit a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-service).
3. Look at the insulator nose (the white ceramic around the centre electrode), the electrodes and the metal shell. Use a torch.
4. Compare with the table below.

| What you see | What it usually means | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Light grey, tan or light brown, dry | Normal mixture and correct heat range | Gap it and refit |
| Dry, black, soft soot | Rich mixture: choke left on, blocked air filter, high float level, leaking injector; or a plug too "cold"; or a weak spark | [Spark plug is black and sooty](/moto-zero/repair/problems/plug-black-sooty) |
| Wet, black and oily, smells of oil | Oil reaching the cylinder: worn rings, valve stem seals, overfilled oil; on a two-stroke, too much oil | [Spark plug is wet with oil](/moto-zero/repair/problems/plug-oily) |
| Wet and smells of petrol | Flooded, or no spark | [Engine is flooded and smells of fuel](/moto-zero/repair/problems/flooded-engine) |
| Chalk white, blistered, or tiny metal beads | Overheating: lean mixture, air leak, wrong heat range, timing too far advanced | [Spark plug is white or blistered](/moto-zero/repair/problems/plug-white). Do not ride hard |
| Electrodes rounded or worn thin | Old plug | Replace |
| Cracked insulator | Damaged plug, often from dropping it or wrong tools | Replace; check for broken pieces in the engine with a workshop |

5. Two-stroke plugs are often darker brown than four-stroke plugs because of the oil in the fuel. Compare with a known good bike of the same model if you can.
6. On fuel-injected bikes, a black plug together with an engine warning light suggests a sensor fault. See [Read engine fault codes (fuel injection)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-code-read).

## Check your work
- After fixing the cause, fit a clean plug, ride again and re-read it. The colour should move toward light grey or tan.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Black plug and black smoke: [Black smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/black-smoke).
- Oily plug and blue smoke: [Blue or grey smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke).
