# Handlebar and grips

The handlebar is the steel tube you steer with, clamped to the top fork yoke, often through rubber-mounted risers. It carries the grips, the throttle twist grip, the levers, the switch housings and the mirrors. Bar-end weights at each end reduce vibration.

## What it does
The bar turns the forks. The grips give you a firm hold; the right grip is the throttle and must turn freely and snap back. Falls bend bars and tear grips; loose clamps let the bar rotate in its mounts, which is dangerous.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Bar not straight when the wheel points straight: [Handlebar not straight](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bars-misaligned).
- Throttle sticks or does not snap shut: [Throttle sticks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/throttle-sticks). Stop riding until fixed.
- Bar moves in its clamp, or the grip turns on the bar.
- After a fall: [The bike has been dropped or crashed](/moto-zero/repair/problems/crash-check).
- Vibration through bars: [Vibration at certain speeds](/moto-zero/repair/problems/speed-vibration).

## When to replace it
Replace a bent or cracked bar; do not straighten steel bars that have been bent a lot, because they weaken. Replace grips that are torn, loose or slippery. Replace riser rubbers if cracked.

## What to match when buying
- Bar diameter (22 mm, also called 7/8 inch, is common), width, rise and bend shape. Check that cables and hoses still reach at full lock.
- Some bars have holes or dowels for switch housings; match those.
- Grips: right grip inner diameter fits the throttle tube, left fits the bar. Many are sold as a pair.

## Fitting notes
- Tighten clamp bolts evenly, front bolts first if the manual says so, to the value for your bike.
- After fitting, turn bars lock to lock and check the throttle snaps shut in every position.
- Align: [Align the front wheel, forks and handlebar](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/front-end-align).
- See [Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check).
- Grips can be fitted over a layer or two of masking tape instead of glue, so they come off again without damage: [Remove and fit handlebar grips](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/grip-replace).
