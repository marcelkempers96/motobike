# Instruments

The instruments are the panel in front of the handlebar: speedometer, odometer, often a fuel gauge, rev counter and warning lamps for neutral, high beam, indicators, oil and the engine (MIL). Older bikes use a mechanical speedometer driven by a cable; newer ones use an electronic unit fed by a [speed sensor](/moto-zero/repair/parts/speed_sensor).

## What it does
The instruments tell you speed, distance, fuel and faults. Electronic clusters need a steady 12 V supply, an earth and signals from sensors and switches. Water getting in, vibration, a weak battery or a poor plug make them reset, flicker or go dark. Mechanical speedometers fail when the cable or drive gear wears.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Instruments or dashboard are dead](/moto-zero/repair/problems/dash-dead).
- [Speedometer or odometer does not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/speedo-dead).
- [Warning lights flicker or come on wrongly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/warning-lights-flicker).
- [Fuel gauge reads wrong](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-gauge-wrong).
- Fogged glass or water inside.

## When to replace it
When it has power and earth at its plug and the sensor signals are good, but still does not work; or the case is broken and lets water in. Some parts (bulbs, glass) can be replaced alone.

## What to match when buying
- Match make, model and year, plug and wiring. Speedometers are calibrated for wheel size and sensor; a wrong one reads wrong.
- In many countries changing the odometer reading is illegal. Keep a record of the old reading when fitting a new unit.
- Mechanical clusters: match the cable fitting.

## Fitting notes
- Home job: [Check power to the instruments](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/dash-power-check), then [Check the speed sensor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/speed-sensor-check) or [Replace the speedometer cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/speedo-cable-replace).
- Key off before unplugging. Fit the rubber mounts so vibration does not reach the unit.
