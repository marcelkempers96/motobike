# 12 V battery

The 12 V battery is a small box, usually under the seat or a side cover, with a red positive (+) and a black negative (-) terminal. Most commuter bikes now use a sealed lead-acid (AGM, "maintenance-free") battery; older ones use a flooded battery with filler caps; some use lithium. Electric motorcycles also have a small 12 V auxiliary battery, separate from the traction pack.

## What it does
The battery powers the starter and, on most bikes, the ignition, lights and horn. The [regulator rectifier](/moto-zero/repair/parts/regulator) recharges it while the engine runs. A lead-acid battery left flat grows hard sulphate on its plates and loses capacity. Heat, overcharging and deep discharge shorten its life.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Engine turns over slowly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/slow-cranking) or [starter relay clicks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/starter-clicks).
- [Battery goes flat after parking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-flat) or [a new battery dies quickly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/new-battery-dies).
- [Battery is boiling, swollen or leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-swollen): stop using it.
- [Corroded terminals](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-corrosion).

## When to replace it
When it will not hold charge after a full slow charge, or is swollen, cracked or leaking. A good voltage at rest is not proof of capacity; a battery can read well and still sag under load. Check charging too, so the new battery is not killed: [Test the charging system](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/charging-system-test).

## What to match when buying
- Match the **code**, voltage, capacity (Ah), size, terminal position and type. In a code like YTX7A-BS: Y is the maker (Yuasa), T means sealed AGM construction, X is the product family, and BS means terminals and packaging, with acid supplied in a separate pack. Other makers' batteries with the same letters after the first are made to fit the same place.
- Do not fit a flooded battery where a sealed one was fitted, as the K-Light manual warns. Lithium needs a compatible charging system and charger.
- Check the date code; an old battery that sat uncharged for months may already be weak.

## Fitting notes
- Remove negative first and fit positive first. Acid burns skin and eyes; charging gives off explosive gas, so keep sparks away.
- Home jobs: [Test and charge a 12 V battery](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-test-charge), [Replace a 12 V battery](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-replace), [Clean and tighten battery terminals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-terminal-clean).
- Never jump start from a running car: [Jump start a motorcycle safely](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/jump-start).
