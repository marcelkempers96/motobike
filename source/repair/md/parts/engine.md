# Engine (complete)

On a commuter bike the engine, clutch and gearbox share one crankcase under the tank. The cylinder points up or forwards, with the spark plug in the head.

## What it does
The engine burns petrol and air above a piston. The pressure pushes the piston down, and the connecting rod turns the crankshaft. A four-stroke fires once every two turns of the crank; a two-stroke fires every turn. An engine needs four things at the right time: fuel, air, a spark and compression. Most engine faults are one of these missing. See every part move in [Under the Engine](/engine/).

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Will not start: [Engine turns over but will not start](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cranks-no-start).
- Loses power: [Sudden loss of power while riding](/moto-zero/repair/problems/sudden-power-loss) and [Low power](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-power).
- Smoke: [Blue smoke](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke), [black smoke](/moto-zero/repair/problems/black-smoke), [white smoke](/moto-zero/repair/problems/white-smoke).
- Knocking, rattling or overheating: see [Engine noise and internals](/moto-zero/repair/systems/engine).

## When to replace it
An engine is rarely replaced whole. First rule out the cheap causes: fuel, air filter, spark plug, valve clearance and oil level. A [compression test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test) tells you whether the piston, rings and valves seal. Internal wear (rings, bearings, crankshaft) is a workshop job: [Engine internal assessment](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment).

## What to match when buying
- A used or exchange engine must match the frame mounts, the gearbox and final drive, the wiring (CDI or ECU, starter) and the exhaust and carburettor or throttle body.
- Ask for the engine number and check it matches the papers where your country requires it.

## Fitting notes
- Check the mounting bolts now and then: [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check).
- Change the oil on time: [Change the engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service). Low or old oil is the most common cause of engine damage.
