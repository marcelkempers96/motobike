# Throttle cable

The throttle cable runs from the twist grip on the right handlebar to the carburettor slide or the throttle body on a fuel-injected bike. Some bikes have two cables: one to open and one to close.

## What it does
Turning the grip pulls the cable and opens the throttle. A spring at the carburettor or throttle body pulls it shut when you let go. A little slack at the grip (free play) makes sure the throttle closes fully at idle and when you turn the bars. A dry, kinked or frayed cable can stick open, which is very dangerous.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Grip stays open or closes slowly: [Throttle sticks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/throttle-sticks). Stop riding until fixed.
- Too much slack before the engine responds: [Throttle has too much free play](/moto-zero/repair/problems/throttle-free-play).
- Idle rises when you turn the bars: [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle).
- Stiff or gritty grip: [Cable frayed or stiff](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cable-frayed).

## When to replace it
Replace a cable with any broken strands, a kinked outer or one that stays stiff after cleaning and oiling.
- Most throttle cables fail at the grip end, where the wire bends each time the grip turns. Look there first.
- On a twin cable throttle, replace the return cable too; do not run the bike on the opening cable alone ([Replace a twin throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-twin-cable-replace)).

## What to match when buying
- Make, model and year. Length, end fittings, adjuster position and whether it is a single or a pull and return pair.
- Carburettor and fuel-injected versions of the same model often use different cables.
- Compare with the old cable before fitting. Cheap cables with rough inner wire stick in the outer.

## Fitting notes
- Route the cable exactly as the old one. Turn the bars lock to lock with the engine idling: the idle must not change.
- Check the grip snaps shut in every bar position before riding.
- See [Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust) and [Lubricate or replace the throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-service).
