# Combined braking unit (CBS)

A combined braking system (CBS) links the rear brake control to part of the front brake, so one control brakes both wheels. On hydraulic systems there is a CBS valve or secondary master cylinder near the frame, with its own small reservoir and extra pipes. On scooters with drum brakes, CBS is often a mechanical equaliser bar between two cables.

## What it does
When the rider presses the rear brake, the CBS unit sends some pressure (or pull) to the front brake, often to one piston of a three-piston caliper. This helps new riders who use only the rear brake. The unit has seals and a plunger that can leak inside, and mechanical linkages can seize or go out of adjustment.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Rear brake no longer applies the front, or brakes feel unbalanced: [CBS does not work as it should](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cbs-fault).
- Soft pedal: [Spongy brake](/moto-zero/repair/problems/spongy-brake), [Rear brake pedal travels too far](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-pedal-travel).
- Front or rear drags after using the pedal: [Brake drag](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).
- Fluid on the CBS unit or pipes: [Brake fluid leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-fluid-leak).

## When to replace it
A unit that leaks inside or outside is replaced or rebuilt by a workshop using the exact model procedure. Do not bypass or remove CBS.

## What to match when buying
- The exact part number for your bike: CBS units are model-specific.
- Seal kits, plunger assemblies and pipes must be for the same unit.
- Mechanical scooter systems need the correct equaliser and cables.

## Fitting notes
- The bleed order for CBS is not the same as for a normal brake. A normal bleed will leave air in the CBS circuit. Workshop job.
- See [Check a combined braking system (CBS)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cbs-check).
