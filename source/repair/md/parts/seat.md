# Seat and seat lock

The seat is a padded cover on a plastic or steel base. It is held by bolts, by hooks at the front and a key lock at the back, or by a hinge and latch on scooters. Under it are often the battery, fuses, tools and on electric bikes the battery pack.

## What it does
The seat carries the rider and passenger and protects the parts under it from rain. A worn lock or broken hook lets the seat move, which is dangerous at speed. A torn cover lets water into the foam, so it stays wet and rots the base.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Seat moves, lock will not catch, or key will not turn: [Seat is loose or the seat lock does not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/seat-loose).
- Rattle from the seat: [Rattling from panels](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bodywork-rattle).
- Water under the seat causing electrical faults: [Electrical faults after rain](/moto-zero/repair/problems/wet-connectors).

## When to replace it
Replace a cracked base, broken hooks or a lock that will not hold. Recover a torn cover before the foam gets soaked.

## What to match when buying
- Make, model and year. Hook and bolt positions must match the frame.
- Locks come with keys; some bikes use one key for ignition, seat and tank. A single lock may need a new key set.
- Covers: buy for your model so they fit the base.

## Fitting notes
- Lightly oil the lock and latch.
- Push down after fitting and pull up hard to check it is locked.
- See [Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check) and [Find and fix rattles](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rattle-fix).
- Seat fixings vary: tool box, bolts at the rear, lever or key latch, push button or hinge. If the seat will not come off, see [Remove the seat when you cannot find how](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/seat-remove).
- Where a rod or cable links both latch sides, check that both release. They can stretch or break.
