# Stator and flywheel

The stator and flywheel make up the bike's generator, under the left engine cover. The stator is a ring of copper coils bolted to the engine. The flywheel (rotor) is a heavy cup with magnets inside, fixed to the end of the crankshaft, that spins around the stator. On many bikes the [ignition pick-up](/moto-zero/repair/parts/pickup) sits with the stator, and the [starter clutch](/moto-zero/repair/parts/starter_clutch) is fixed to the flywheel.

## What it does
As the magnets spin past the coils they make AC electricity. The [regulator rectifier](/moto-zero/repair/parts/regulator) turns this into DC to charge the battery and run the lights. The coils get hot in oil; heat and age break down the varnish on the wire, and a coil shorts or goes open. Loose or wet plugs between the stator and regulator burn.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Battery not charging](/moto-zero/repair/problems/not-charging) or [lights dim at idle](/moto-zero/repair/problems/dim-lights-idle).
- [Burnt smell from the generator or a dark stator connector](/moto-zero/repair/problems/stator-burnt).
- [No spark](/moto-zero/repair/problems/no-spark) on bikes where the ignition is fed from a stator coil.
- Dark, burnt coils when the cover is off.

## When to replace it
When a coil reads open, shorted to earth, or outside the resistance range, after checking the plug and wiring. A cracked or loose flywheel, or lost magnets, need a new flywheel. Follow your bike's manual.

## What to match when buying
- Match the part number, or make, model and engine. Count coils and wires; single-phase and three-phase stators are not swappable.
- Check whether the pick-up comes with the stator.
- Rewound stators are common and can be good, but ask about wire grade and a warranty.
- The rotor that turns around the coils is a separate part: [Flywheel (magnet rotor)](/moto-zero/repair/parts/flywheel).

## Fitting notes
- Home job to test: [Test the stator](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stator-test). Removing the flywheel needs a puller and holder: workshop job.
- Clean the crank and flywheel tapers dry, fit the key, and keep metal bits off the magnets.
- Drain or catch the oil behind the cover, and use a new cover gasket.
