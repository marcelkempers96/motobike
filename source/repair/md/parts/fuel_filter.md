# Fuel filter

A fuel filter stops dirt and rust reaching the carburettor or injector. On carburettor bikes it is usually a strainer on the [fuel tap](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_tap) plus, on many bikes, a small see-through inline filter in the hose. On fuel injection there is a strainer sock on the in-tank [fuel pump](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_pump) and often a fine filter in the pump unit; the K-Light 125 has a petrol filter in the tank assembly.

## What it does
Fine dirt blocks carburettor jets and sticks injector needles. The filter catches it first. As it fills, flow drops: the engine runs fine at idle but starves at full throttle or up hills. Injection systems also lose fuel pressure.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Engine stops and only runs on reserve or after a rest](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-starvation).
- [Bogs down at full throttle](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bogs-full-throttle) and [sudden power loss](/moto-zero/repair/problems/sudden-power-loss).
- Visible dirt or water in a clear filter: [Water or dirt in the fuel](/moto-zero/repair/problems/water-in-fuel).
- [Fuel pump whines loudly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-pump-noisy).

## When to replace it
At the interval in your bike's manual, and at once after rust or dirt has been found in the tank. The K-Light schedule replaces the petrol filter at 12000 km; its EFI chapter gives two different intervals, so follow the schedule for your bike.

## What to match when buying
- Inline filters: match hose size and use a filter rated for petrol and, on injection, for system pressure. A cheap carburettor filter can burst on an injection line.
- In-tank filters: match the pump unit part number.
- Check the flow arrow. Poor filters have weak plastic and loose paper.

## Fitting notes
- Home job: [Replace the fuel filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-filter-replace).
- Work cold, outside, with no flames. On injection, release pressure as the manual describes before opening any line.
- Fit with the arrow toward the engine, use new clips, and check for leaks with the key on before starting.
- A see-through filter in the line to the carburettor shows rust and dirt. Change it when it fills. It is the cheapest way to protect the carburettor from a rusty tank.
