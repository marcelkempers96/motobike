# Tail and brake light

The tail light is the red lamp at the back. On most bikes one bulb with two filaments does both jobs: a dim filament for the tail light and a bright one for the brake light. Newer bikes use LED units. A small white lamp lights the number plate.

## What it does
The tail light shows your bike to drivers behind at night. The brake light comes on bright when either [brake light switch](/moto-zero/repair/parts/brake_light_switch) closes. Filaments break from vibration; bulb holders rust from water; LED units fail when water gets in. A bulb fitted the wrong way round can light the wrong filament.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Tail light does not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tail-light-out).
- [Brake light does not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-light-dead).
- [Brake light stays on](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-light-stuck-on): this can also flatten the battery.
- Cracked lens, water inside, rusty holder.

## When to replace it
Replace the bulb when a filament is broken or the glass is black. Replace the lens if cracked, and the LED unit if any section is dark.

## What to match when buying
- Match bulb type and wattage. Two-filament bulbs have offset pins so they fit only one way; do not force them.
- Look for an approval mark on the lamp. A red lens must stay red: do not fit a clear lens with a coloured bulb unless it is approved.
- Check the lens gasket is included.

## Fitting notes
- Rider job: [Replace a bulb](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/bulb-replace). Check the lens seal when refitting, as the K-Light manual says.
- Test with someone pressing each brake: [Test and adjust the brake light switches](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-switch-test).
- If power is missing: [Test a lighting circuit](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lighting-circuit-test).
