# Fuel tank

The fuel tank holds the petrol, on top of the frame on most motorcycles or under the floor or seat on scooters. It carries the [fuel cap](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_cap), and either a [fuel tap](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_tap) (carburettor bikes) or an in-tank [fuel pump](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_pump) and [level sender](/moto-zero/repair/parts/fuel_sender) (fuel injection).

## What it does
The tank stores fuel and lets air in through a vent as fuel is used. On newer bikes vapour goes through a rollover valve to a charcoal canister instead of the open air. Steel tanks rust inside when water sits in them, and rust flakes block filters and pumps. Dents and cracks at mounts cause leaks.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Rust or flakes inside the tank](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rusty-tank).
- [Fuel leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-leak) or [fuel smell](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-smell).
- [Engine stops and the cap hisses](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tank-vacuum): blocked vent.
- [Water or dirt in the fuel](/moto-zero/repair/problems/water-in-fuel).

## When to replace it
When it leaks from a seam or crack, or rust has thinned the metal. Light surface rust can be removed: [Remove rust from a fuel tank](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-rust-treat). Never weld or braze a fuel tank at home; fumes in an "empty" tank explode.

## What to match when buying
- Match make, model and year: mounts, pump or tap opening, sender opening and cap ring differ.
- Check the inside of a used tank with a torch for rust and old sealer. Shake it to hear loose flakes.
- Buy new pump and sender seals with the tank.

## Fitting notes
- Work outside or in good air, engine cold, no flame or sparks; disconnect the battery negative first, as the K-Light manual says.
- On fuel injection, residual pressure sprays fuel when the hose is unplugged; cover the connector with a cloth.
- Check rubber mounts and hose routing. Drain old fuel: [Drain old or contaminated fuel](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drain-fuel).
- Steel tanks rust, plastic tanks crack with age and sun, and fiberglass tanks may not cope with ethanol blends. A new tank is the best fix for a rusted one.
- Under-seat tanks need an electric pump. Two-piece tanks have a crossover tube, and the tank must be fully drained before removal. See [Remove and refit a fuel tank safely](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tank-remove).
