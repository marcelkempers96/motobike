# Oil dripping from the exhaust (two-stroke)

A two-stroke burns oil with its fuel. If more oil goes in than burns, it collects in the exhaust as a black, sticky liquid that drips from joints and the outlet. Common causes are too much oil (wrong premix ratio or oil pump set too rich), a lot of slow riding and idling, a rich carburettor setting, or wrong oil. Less often, a worn crankshaft seal on the gearbox side lets gearbox oil into the crankcase and it is burned. MAVCC lists an oily exhaust pipe as a sign the engine needs more than a tune-up.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care. Keep oil off the rear tyre: wipe the silencer and tyre. Fix the cause before long rides.

## Quick checks first
- Premix bike: what ratio did you mix? Did you add oil to the tank twice?
- Oil-injection bike: is the oil tank level going down faster than usual?
- Is the gearbox oil level falling?
- Is the bike ridden mostly slowly in traffic?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the oil coming from a joint (head pipe flange or silencer joint) rather than the outlet?**
- Yes: the joint leaks and lets sludge out. Fit a new gasket with [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check). Then continue, because the oil supply may still be too high.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Premix bike: is the mix stronger than the ratio in your manual?**
- Yes: drain and refill with the correct mix. Never guess the ratio; use the manual or the oil maker's chart for your engine.
- Oil injection: go to step 3.

**3. Check the oil pump with [Check the two-stroke oil pump and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-oil-pump). Is the pump cable set too far open, or is the bike also premixed by mistake?**
- Yes: correct it. Do not premix and inject together unless the manual says so for running-in.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is the gearbox oil level falling with no outside leak?**
- Yes: a crankshaft seal may be leaking gearbox oil into the crankcase. Ask a workshop to check crank seals; see [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment) for the related strip.
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Read the plug with [Read a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-reading). Is it wet and black?**
- Yes: the fuel mixture may also be rich. See [Engine runs rich, smells of fuel and smokes black](/moto-zero/repair/problems/runs-rich).
- No: carbon and sludge may already be built up. Go to step 6.

**6. Workshop: [Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust and port](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-decoke).** After cleaning, a correct oil supply and some regular higher-rev riding keep it clean.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Premix too strong | Mixing record | Drain and mix to the manual ratio |
| Oil pump set too rich | Pump marks, oil use high | [Check the two-stroke oil pump and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-oil-pump) |
| Leaking exhaust joint | Drips at joint | [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check) |
| Carbon and sludge in exhaust | Low power, crust | [Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust and port](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-decoke) |
| Leaking crankshaft seal | Gearbox oil falling | [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment) |
