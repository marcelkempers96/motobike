# Oil pressure or oil level light is on

Some bikes have an oil pressure light: it comes on when the oil pump is not making enough pressure. Others have an oil level light (the K-Light manual mentions an oil sensor alarm for level) or, on two-strokes, a light for the oil tank. All three should light for a moment at key-on as a bulb test and go out once the engine runs. A light that stays on or comes on while riding is a warning to stop. There is no single pressure value for all bikes; testing pressure needs the model manual and a gauge.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. Stop the engine as soon as it is safe. Running without oil pressure can destroy the engine in minutes.

## Quick checks first
- Stop and switch off.
- Check the oil level (four-stroke) or the oil tank (two-stroke).
- Look under the bike for a big oil loss.
- Does the light come on at key-on and go out after starting?
- Was the oil filter changed recently? An element fitted backwards can cut off the oil supply.
- A coarse screen in the drain plug can block with debris and starve the top of the engine.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the oil level below the lower mark, or the two-stroke tank empty?**
- Yes: top up with the correct oil using [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check). Look for a leak. If the light goes out at once after starting and there is no noise, ride gently and find out why the level was low. Two-stroke: go to [Two-stroke oil tank level is not going down](/moto-zero/repair/problems/two-stroke-oil-not-used) if the tank was full but the light was on.
- No, the level is correct: go to step 2.

**2. Is there a new knock, rattle or rumble?**
- Yes: do not restart. Go to [Deep knocking from the bottom of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/deep-knock).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does the light flicker only at hot idle and go out above idle?**
- Yes: thin, old or wrong oil, or a worn pump. Change oil and clean the strainer with [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service). If it continues, workshop.
- No, it stays on at all revs: go to step 4.

**4. Is the wire on the oil pressure or level switch disconnected, broken or rubbing on the frame?**
- A wire touching earth turns the light on: repair it with [Find a wiring fault](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wiring-fault-find).
- Wire good: go to step 5.

**5. Workshop: the oil pressure must be measured with a gauge in place of the switch, following the model manual.**
- Pressure low: blocked strainer or centrifugal filter, worn oil pump, or blocked passage. The K-Light manual lists no or low pressure and a blocked oil passage as causes of seizure. See [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment).
- Pressure correct: the switch is faulty; replace it.

**What happens if you keep riding:** the bearings and cam surfaces lose their oil film and wear or seize within minutes.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low oil level | Dipstick | [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) |
| Old, thin or wrong oil | Flickers at hot idle | [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) |
| Wire to switch shorted to earth | Damaged wire | [Find a wiring fault](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wiring-fault-find) |
| Blocked strainer or filter | Workshop pressure test | [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) |
| Worn oil pump | Low pressure | [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) |
| Two-stroke oil tank empty or pump fault | Tank level | [Check the two-stroke oil pump and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-oil-pump) |
| Oil screen in the drain plug blocked | Screen clogged when removed | [Clean the oil screen in the drain plug or filter chamber](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-screen-clean) |
| Paper element fitted the wrong way | Light came on after a filter change | [Change a spin-on or paper oil filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-filter-change) |
| No oil reaching the cylinder head | Little oil at the head check bolt | [Check that oil is reaching the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-flow-to-head-check) |
