# Power fades as the engine gets hot

The bike is strong for the first part of a ride, then gets weaker as the engine heats up, especially on long climbs, in slow traffic or in hot weather. After a rest it is strong again. Either the engine is overheating (and protecting itself or close to damage), or a part works when cool and fails when hot: an ignition coil, a clutch that slips when its oil is hot, or a brake that drags once it warms.

## Is it safe to ride?

With care. If the engine also knocks, smells hot, smokes, or the temperature warning comes on, **stop and let it cool**: it may be close to seizing.

## Quick checks first

- Is the engine oil level correct, and the oil not old and thin?
- Air-cooled: are the cylinder fins clean, not packed with mud or oil?
- Liquid-cooled: is the coolant level right and does the fan run?
- After a ride, is one wheel hub or brake disc much hotter than the other (test carefully with the back of a finger near, not on, the part)?
- Does the engine pink (rattle) when hot?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the oil level correct and the engine cooling working?**
- Low oil: [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check). Oil also cools an air-cooled engine.
- Fins blocked or oily: [Check air cooling](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-cooling-check).
- Liquid-cooled with low coolant or no fan: [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check) and see [Liquid-cooled engine overheats](/moto-zero/repair/problems/overheating-liquid).
- All fine: go to step 2.

**2. After a ride, is a brake disc, drum or hub much hotter than the other wheel, and does that wheel spin less freely?**
- Yes: a brake is dragging as it heats. [Check for brake drag](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-drag-check) and see [Brake caliper sticks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/caliper-sticking).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. When hot, do the revs rise without the bike speeding up?**
- Yes: the clutch slips when hot. [Adjust clutch free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) and see [Clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-slip).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. When hot, does it misfire or jerk, rather than just feel weak?**
- Yes: a coil or pick-up failing with heat. [Test the ignition coil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ignition-coil-test) and see [Ignition cuts out when hot](/moto-zero/repair/problems/coil-fails-hot).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Read the plug after a hot run. White or blistered? Does it pink?**
- Yes: lean and running hot. [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) and see [Engine runs hot and lean](/moto-zero/repair/problems/runs-lean). The K-Light 125 overheating chart also lists poor fuel grade and long high-speed or overloaded riding.
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Carburettor: is a fuel hose near the hot engine or exhaust?** Fuel can boil in the line. Reroute it and [check the float level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check). Fuel-injected: [read fault codes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-code-read); some ECUs cut power when the engine is too hot.

Use a [road test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/road-test) on the same route to confirm.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low or old oil | Dipstick or sight glass | [Oil level check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) |
| Blocked fins or cooling fault | Visual, coolant level, fan | [Air cooling check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-cooling-check) |
| Brake dragging when hot | Hot disc or hub, wheel slow | [Brake drag check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-drag-check) |
| Clutch slipping when hot | Revs rise without speed | [Clutch free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) |
| Lean mixture | White plug, pinking | [Intake leak test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
| Coil failing hot | Misfire only when hot | [Coil test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ignition-coil-test) |
