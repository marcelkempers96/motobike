# Rear suspension feels harsh

Bumps come straight through the seat with a jolt, as if the rear does not move enough. It can be set too stiff (preload too high for a light rider, tyre pressure too high), or something is stopping it moving: a seized swingarm pivot, seized shock mount bushes, or a damper that has locked up. A rear that sits so low it hits its stops also feels harsh.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care. A rear that does not move loses grip over bumps.

## Quick checks first
- Check rear tyre pressure (K-Light 125: 210 ± 10 kPa cold).
- What preload step are the shocks on? Is it the highest with no load?
- Push down on the seat: does the rear move at all, or very little?
- Does it feel harsh only with a passenger?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the rear tyre over-inflated?**
- Yes: set it with [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Is preload set high while riding alone with no load?**
- Yes: lower it a step (both sides the same) with [Adjust rear spring preload](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-preload-adjust).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does it feel harsh mainly with a load, as a hard knock on bumps?**
- Yes: the rear is bottoming. Go to [Rear sits too low](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-sagging).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. When you push hard on the seat, does the rear move only a little and stiffly?**
- Yes: go to step 5.
- No: check the seat foam and seat mounts; a flattened seat feels harsh too.

**5. Support the bike with the rear wheel off the ground. Is the swingarm pivot or a shock mount seized?**
- To check, follow [Check the swingarm pivot](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/swingarm-check) and [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check). A pivot that does not move smoothly needs cleaning, greasing or new bushes.
- If the pivot moves freely with the shocks disconnected but the shock is very stiff, the damper has seized. Replace the shock (both, on twin shocks).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tyre pressure too high | Gauge reading | [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check) |
| Preload too high | High step, light load | [Adjust rear spring preload](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-preload-adjust) |
| Bottoming under load | Hard knock with passenger | See [Rear sits too low](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-sagging) |
| Seized swingarm pivot | Stiff with shocks off | [Check the swingarm pivot](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/swingarm-check) |
| Seized shock or mount bushes | Shock very stiff | [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check) |
