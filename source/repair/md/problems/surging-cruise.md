# Surges or jerks at steady cruising speed

You hold the throttle still at a steady speed, but the bike surges forward and back, or jerks. Two very different things feel like this. Either the **engine** power rises and falls (a lean mixture at light throttle is the classic cause), or the **drive** has slack that snatches: a loose or tight-spotted chain, worn cush drive rubbers, or a slack throttle cable that moves as the bike bounces.

## Is it safe to ride?

Yes, with care. Check the chain first: a very slack chain can jump the sprockets.

## Quick checks first

- Is the chain slack correct and even all the way round?
- Does the jerk come with a clunk from the rear when you open and close the throttle?
- Is there a lot of free play in the throttle?
- Does it happen only at one light throttle opening?
- Fuel-injected: is the warning light on?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. On a [road test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/road-test), watch the rev counter or listen. Do the engine revs rise and fall during the surge?**
- No, the revs stay steady but the bike snatches: the drive line. Go to step 2.
- Yes, the engine itself surges: go to step 4.

**2. Is the chain slack correct and even? Check at several points around the chain.**
- Too slack or tight in places: [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust) (K-Light 125: 20 to 40 mm, upright). Tight spots: see [Chain is tight in some places](/moto-zero/repair/problems/chain-tight-spots).
- Correct: go to step 3.

**3. Hold the rear brake and rock the rear wheel by hand. Does the sprocket move a lot before the wheel turns?**
- Yes: worn cush drive rubbers. [Check the cush drive rubbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cush-drive-check).
- No: scooters, see [Scooter judders when pulling away](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cvt-judder). Manual bikes, check the throttle cable (step 4).

**4. Is the throttle free play correct, so the grip does not move the throttle when you hit bumps?**
- No: [Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust) (K-Light 125: 3 to 5 mm).
- Yes: go to step 5.

**5. Does it surge only at small, steady throttle openings, with popping or a white plug?**
- Yes: lean at light load. [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test). Carburettor: [check the float](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) and [clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean). See [Engine runs hot and lean](/moto-zero/repair/problems/runs-lean).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Fuel-injected: is a code stored?**
- Yes: [Read engine fault codes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-code-read). Oxygen sensor faults upset the fuel correction at cruise: [Check the oxygen sensor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/o2-sensor-check).
- No: check ignition: [service the spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-service). If it misses rather than surges, see [Misfires when accelerating hard](/moto-zero/repair/problems/misfire-under-load).

**7. Revs rise and fall but the speed does not follow:** this is slip, not surge. See [Clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-slip).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Chain too slack or tight spots | Measure slack round the chain | [Chain adjust](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust) |
| Worn cush drive | Sprocket moves before wheel | [Cush drive check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cush-drive-check) |
| Throttle free play wrong | Grip moves over bumps | [Free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust) |
| Lean at light throttle | Popping, white plug, air leak | [Intake leak test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
| O2 sensor or sensor fault (injection) | Code, live data | [O2 check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/o2-sensor-check) |
