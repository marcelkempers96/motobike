# Rattling from the exhaust or heat shield

A rattle or buzz from the exhaust area at certain revs is usually a loose heat shield screw, a broken shield clip, a loose silencer mount or a perished rubber bush. It can also be a broken baffle inside the silencer, or a nearby part that touches the pipe: the centre stand, the brake pedal, a footpeg bracket or a chain guard. Vibration grows with looseness, so a small rattle can end with a part falling off.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care. Stop if a heat shield or the silencer is hanging or touching the tyre, chain or your leg.

## Quick checks first
- Engine cold: tap each heat shield. Does it buzz?
- Shake the silencer by hand. Does it move at the mount?
- Is the centre stand tucked up and its spring holding it?
- Look for shiny rub marks on the pipe or the parts near it.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the rattle happen with the bike on the stand and the engine revved in neutral?**
- Yes: go to step 2.
- No, only over bumps: check the centre stand, side stand and chain guard. See [Centre stand is hard to use or scrapes](/moto-zero/repair/problems/centre-stand-fault) and [Find and fix rattles](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rattle-fix).

**2. Engine cold: press each heat shield with your hand and rev the engine (helper, outside). Does the rattle stop when you press a shield?**
- Yes: tighten or replace its screws and clips with [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does the silencer move at the rear mount or the joint with the header?**
- Yes: tighten the mount and replace worn rubber bushes with the same repair. If the joint puffs, go to [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Shake and tap the cold silencer. Is there a loose piece inside?**
- Yes: a broken baffle. Replace the silencer. See [Exhaust has become much louder](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-loud).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Is the whole engine moving more than usual?**
- Yes: check [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check). A loose engine shakes the exhaust.
- No: the rattle may be elsewhere. Go to [Rattling from panels, mudguards or the seat](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bodywork-rattle).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Loose heat shield screw or clip | Rattle stops when pressed | [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check) |
| Loose silencer mount or worn bush | Silencer moves | [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check) |
| Stand or bracket touching the pipe | Rub marks, rattle over bumps | [Find and fix rattles](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rattle-fix) |
| Broken internal baffle | Loose piece inside | Replace the silencer |
| Loose engine mounts | Engine moves | [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check) |
