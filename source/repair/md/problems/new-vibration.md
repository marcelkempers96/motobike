# New or increased engine vibration

Every single-cylinder engine shakes a little. Many, including the K-Light 125, have a balancer shaft to reduce it. A new or stronger vibration usually comes from something loose (engine mount bolts, the exhaust, the handlebar), from the engine misfiring, or from the chain and wheels. Less often it comes from inside the engine: a worn crankshaft or a balancer that has slipped out of time after engine work. The first job is to decide whether the vibration follows engine speed or road speed.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care while you check for loose bolts. Stop if the vibration comes with a knock, a rattle from the engine, or if parts are falling off.

## Quick checks first
- Does it change with engine revs in neutral, or only with road speed?
- Shake the exhaust, the handlebar and the footpegs by hand. Anything loose?
- Is the engine misfiring or idling roughly?
- Any recent crash, engine work or new parts?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Bike on the stand in neutral. Rev the engine slowly through the range. Is the vibration there?**
- No, only when riding at certain speeds: go to [Vibration at certain speeds](/moto-zero/repair/problems/speed-vibration). Check the chain with [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust).
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Check the engine mounting bolts with [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check). Was any loose, missing or the bush worn?**
- Yes: fix and retest. Look for frame cracks at the mount: [Crack in the frame or a bracket](/moto-zero/repair/problems/frame-crack).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Check the exhaust and heat shields with [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check), and the handlebar, mirrors and panels with [Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check). Anything loose?**
- Yes: fix it. Buzzing panels often feel like engine vibration.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does the engine run smoothly, or does it misfire or idle roughly?**
- Rough or misfiring: an uneven engine shakes. Go to [Rough, lumpy or uneven idle](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rough-idle) or [Misfires or stutters when accelerating hard or climbing](/moto-zero/repair/problems/misfire-under-load). Start with [Read a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-reading).
- Smooth: go to step 5.

**5. Did the vibration start right after engine work (crankcase split, clutch or balancer removal)?**
- Yes: the balancer shaft may be out of time. Return to the workshop that did the work.
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Is there a knock or rumble too?**
- Yes: go to [Deep knocking from the bottom of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/deep-knock) or [Rumbling or growling from inside the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bearing-rumble).
- No: ask a workshop to check crankshaft and balancer with [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Loose engine mount or worn bush | Bolt turns, engine moves | [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check) |
| Loose exhaust or heat shield | Movement, buzzing | [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check) |
| Loose bars, mirrors, panels | Hand check | [Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check) |
| Misfire | Rough idle, plug condition | [Read a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-reading) |
| Chain tight spots or wheel fault | Follows road speed | [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust) |
| Balancer out of time, crank wear | After engine work, knock | [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) |
