# Exhaust has become much louder

The silencer (muffler) quietens the exhaust with chambers, perforated tubes and sometimes sound-absorbing packing. A sudden or gradual rise in noise usually means a leaking joint, a hole rusted through, a cracked weld, a baffle that has broken loose inside the silencer, or packing that has burned away. Road bikes must meet noise rules; the K-Light was approved to UNECE Regulation 41, and a loud exhaust can fail a roadside or annual check.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care for a short time. Stop if the pipe is hanging loose, touching the tyre or chain, or if you smell exhaust fumes at a standstill.

## Quick checks first
- Look along the pipe for holes, cracks, soot streaks and loose clamps.
- Shake the cold silencer: does something rattle inside?
- Has the bike been dropped or hit a kerb?
- Has anyone removed the baffle or end cap?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Engine cold. Look at the whole exhaust from the head to the outlet. Is there a visible hole, crack or broken weld?**
- Yes: the pipe or silencer needs welding or replacement. A temporary exhaust bandage can get you home. Do not patch the catalytic converter section.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Start the engine outside. Is the extra noise a sharp tick or puff at a joint?**
- Yes: go to [Ticking or puffing from an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-leak) and [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check).
- No, it is a general roar or a deep blare: go to step 3.

**3. Shake the cold silencer and tap it. Is there a rattle inside?**
- Yes: an internal baffle has broken. The silencer usually cannot be repaired by hand; replace it with one approved for your bike.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does your silencer have a removable end cap or baffle? Is it missing or loose?**
- Missing or loose: refit or replace it. Riding without it is often illegal and can upset the mixture.
- Present: go to step 5.

**5. Is the silencer a packed type (common on two-strokes and sport bikes), and does a puff of fibre or a sharp exhaust note come from it?**
- Yes: the packing has burned away. A workshop repacks it; two-strokes often need [Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust and port](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-decoke) at the same time.
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Check the mounts with [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check).** A loose silencer can open its joint under vibration.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Leaking joint or gasket | Ticking, soot | [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check) |
| Rust hole or cracked weld | Visible hole | Replace or weld the pipe |
| Broken internal baffle | Rattle inside silencer | Replace the silencer |
| Missing end cap or baffle | Look at outlet | Refit or replace |
| Burned packing | Fibre, harsh note | [Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust and port](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-decoke) |
| Loose mount | Silencer moves | [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check) |
