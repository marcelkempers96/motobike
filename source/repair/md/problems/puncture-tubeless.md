# Flat tyre on a tubeless wheel

The tyre is flat or going down fast, and the wheel is tubeless (cast alloy wheels, and some spoked wheels; the tyre says TUBELESS or TL). Tubeless tyres usually lose air slowly around a nail, which gives you time to stop safely. Some small holes can be plugged as a temporary fix; many cannot.

## Is it safe to ride?
No, not on a flat tyre. Riding flat damages the inside of the tyre and can bend the rim; a flat front can cause a crash. Stop safely. Ride only after a successful temporary plug, gently, to a tyre shop.

## Quick checks first
- Stop off the road on firm ground; bike on its stand.
- Find the object: look round the tread, or spray soapy water. Do not pull it out yet.
- Note where it is: tread centre, shoulder or sidewall.
- Was the tyre ridden flat for any distance?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is there an object or hole in the tread?**
- Yes: go to step 2.
- No: go to step 5.

**2. Is the hole in the central part of the tread (not the shoulder or sidewall) and small (nail sized)?**
- Yes: go to step 3.
- No: do not plug it. The tyre needs a shop and probably replacing ([Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace)). Arrange transport.

**3. Was the tyre ridden flat or nearly flat?**
- Yes: the inside may be damaged where you cannot see it. Do not plug and ride. Have a shop remove and inspect the tyre.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Do you have a tubeless plug kit and a way to inflate?**
- Yes: [Plug a tubeless tyre puncture](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tubeless-plug). Then ride gently, no passenger, to a tyre shop for a plug-patch from inside or a new tyre.
- No: do not fit an inner tube into a tubeless tyre as a fix. Arrange transport or a mobile repairer.

**5. No object found. Spray soapy water on the valve and along both rims. Where are the bubbles?**
- At the valve: [Air leaking from the tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/problems/valve-leak).
- Where tyre meets rim: [Air leaking where the tyre meets the rim](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak).
- From a crack in the rim: [Rim is dented or cracked](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rim-damage). Do not ride.
- From a cut or crack in the sidewall: [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace).

**6. After any repair, does the tyre hold pressure overnight?**
- Yes: keep checking for a few days ([Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check)).
- No: go to [Tyre slowly loses air](/moto-zero/repair/problems/slow-puncture).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nail, screw or thorn in the tread | Object visible, bubbles | [Plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tubeless-plug), then shop repair or new tyre |
| Valve leak | Bubbles at valve | [Check and replace a tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-check) |
| Bead not sealing | Bubbles at rim line | [Bead leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak) |
| Cut, sidewall or shoulder damage | Damage outside tread centre | [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace) |
| Cracked rim after a pothole | Crack, bubbles from rim | [Rim damage](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rim-damage) |
