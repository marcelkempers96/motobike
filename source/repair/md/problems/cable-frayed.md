# A control cable is frayed or stiff

A cable has broken strands showing (usually next to the nipple at the lever), or it feels stiff, gritty or slow to return. Frayed cables break without warning. Stiff cables make levers heavy and can stop a throttle or brake from returning. The fix is usually oil or a new cable, plus a better route.

## Is it safe to ride?
Do not ride with a frayed or sticking **throttle** or **brake** cable. A frayed **clutch** cable can snap at any time and leave you without a clutch; ride only to a repair point, with care.

## Quick checks first
- Which cable: throttle, clutch, front or rear drum brake, choke, speedometer?
- Look at both ends, especially where the inner leaves the outer at the lever.
- Squeeze the lever: smooth, or gritty and heavy?
- Follow the cable: sharp bends, kinks, crushed outer, melted by the exhaust?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Are any strands broken anywhere on the inner cable?**
- Yes: replace the cable before riding (or before riding further than a repair point for a clutch cable). Throttle: [Lubricate or replace the throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-service). Clutch: [Replace the clutch cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-replace). Brake and others: replace with the correct part.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Disconnect the cable at the lever. Is the lever itself stiff without the cable?**
- Yes: the lever pivot is dry or worn. [Service brake and clutch lever pivots](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Pull the inner cable by hand. Is it stiff or gritty?**
- Yes: go to step 4.
- No: the stiffness is at the other end (clutch arm, brake cam, carburettor). Check that part.

**4. Oil the cable ([Lubricate control cables](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cable-lube)). Is it now smooth?**
- Yes: refit, set free play ([clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust), [drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust), [throttle](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust)).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Is the outer cable kinked, crushed, cracked, or routed with a tight bend or near the exhaust?**
- Yes: replace the cable and route it along the original path in its guides. Route the new one beside the old one before removing the old.
- No: the inner is rusted or the lining is damaged. Replace the cable.

**6. After replacement, turn the bars lock to lock while working each control. Any change in feel or free play?**
- Yes: the route is wrong. Correct it.
- No: done.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dry cable | Stiff, smooth after oiling | [Lubricate control cables](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cable-lube) |
| Frayed at the nipple | Broken strands at lever end | Replace cable |
| Dry lever pivot | Lever stiff without cable | [Lever pivots](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service) |
| Kinked or crushed outer | Visible damage, stays stiff | Replace and reroute |
| Tight bend or wrong route | Feel changes at full lock | Reroute in guides |
