# Fuel is leaking or dripping

You see fuel dripping, a wet patch under the bike, a wet tank, hose or carburettor, or a rainbow stain on the ground. Petrol leaking near a hot engine and exhaust is the biggest fire risk on a motorcycle. Find the source before you ride again. Most leaks come from a hose, a clip, the fuel tap, the carburettor float bowl or overflow, or the tank itself.

## Is it safe to ride?

No. **Stop now**, switch off the engine and turn the fuel tap OFF. Move the bike away from people, flames and hot parts. Do not ride until the leak is fixed.

## Quick checks first

- Turn the tap OFF (carburettor bikes). Does the drip stop within a minute?
- Is the tank overfilled, or the bike parked steeply on its side stand?
- Is fuel coming from the small overflow pipe under the carburettor?
- Is the fuel cap closed and its seal in place?
- Fuel-injected: is the wet spot near the pump on the tank, the hose connector or the injector?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Engine cold, bike upright on level ground. Dry everything with a rag. Where does fuel appear first? Use a strip of clean paper to find the exact spot.**
- Carburettor overflow pipe: go to [Fuel pours out of the carburettor overflow pipe](/moto-zero/repair/problems/carb-overflowing).
- Hose, clip, filter or tap joint: go to step 2.
- Carburettor float bowl joint or drain screw: go to step 3.
- Tank seam, tank bottom or around the tap: go to step 4.
- Around the filler cap: go to step 5.
- Fuel-injected pump, hose connector or injector: go to step 6.

**2. Is a hose cracked, swollen, hard or loose on its spigot?**
- Yes: replace the hose with fuel-rated hose of the same size and fit good clips. A weeping tap body or O-ring: drain the tank and refit the tap with a new seal. [Check the fuel tap, lines and flow](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check). A leaking filter: [replace it](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-filter-replace).

**3. Is the drain screw loose, or the bowl gasket flat or cracked?**
- Tighten the drain screw gently. If the bowl joint weeps, fit a new gasket: [Check the carburettor float](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) shows how to remove the bowl.

**4. Is there a pinhole, rust bubble or wet seam on the tank?**
- Yes: drain the tank at once: [Drain old or contaminated fuel](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drain-fuel). A rusted or holed tank is replaced or repaired by a specialist. **Never weld or solder a fuel tank yourself.** See [Remove rust from a fuel tank](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-rust-treat).

**5. Does fuel weep from the cap after filling, or when the bike leans?**
- Do not fill above the bottom of the filler neck. Check the cap seal and vent: [Clean and lubricate the fuel cap](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-cap-service) and [check the tank vent](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-vent-check).

**6. Fuel-injected: is the leak at the pump seal, the hose connector or the injector?**
- Do not open the system: it is under pressure. Turn the key OFF and take the bike to a workshop. The K-Light 125 manual requires a new pump seal ring and a leak check before running after any pump or injector work. See [Check the fuel pump](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-pump-check) and [Check the fuel injector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check).

After any fix, turn the fuel ON (or key ON to prime the pump) and watch every joint for five minutes before starting.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cracked or loose fuel hose, weak clip | Wet joint, hard or split hose | [Tap and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check) |
| Stuck float needle (overflow running) | Fuel from overflow pipe | [Float check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) |
| Fuel tap seal or body leaking | Wet around the tap | [Tap and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check) |
| Overfilled tank or bad cap seal | Wet around the cap | [Fuel cap](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-cap-service) |
| Float bowl gasket or drain screw | Wet at the bowl | [Float check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) |
| Rusted or damaged tank | Pinholes, wet seam | [Tank rust](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-rust-treat) |
| Pump seal, connector or injector O-ring (injection) | Wet near pump or injector | [Injector check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check) |
