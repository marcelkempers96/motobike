# Brake lever pulls back to the handlebar

You squeeze the brake lever and it touches the grip with little or no braking. This is a brake failure. On a hydraulic brake it means fluid has gone, air or vapour is in the system, the master cylinder cannot hold pressure, or the pads have been pushed far back. On a cable drum brake it means the cable is broken or slipped, or the shoes are worn out.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. **Stop now** as safely as you can: close the throttle, use the other brake gently and change down to use engine braking. Push the bike or have it carried to a workshop.

## Quick checks first
- Look at the front reservoir: is there fluid in it at all?
- Look for fluid on the wheel, fork leg, caliper or under the lever.
- Look at the lever pivot: is the lever broken or loose?
- Cable brake: is the cable still attached at both ends?
- Pump the lever several times: does any pressure come back?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the brake hydraulic or cable?**
- Cable or rod: go to step 7.
- Hydraulic: go to step 2.

**2. Is the reservoir empty or nearly empty, or is fluid leaking anywhere?**
- Yes: find the leak with [Check brake hoses and banjo bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-hose-check) and go to [Brake fluid is leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-fluid-leak). Air has entered; the repair must be followed by a full bleed.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does pumping the lever bring back a firm feel?**
- Yes, quickly: the pads were pushed back. This happens after pad work, or when a bent disc or loose wheel bearing knocks the pistons back. Check [Check a brake disc](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-disc-check) and [Check wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-check).
- Yes, but stays soft: air or boiled fluid. Go to [Brake lever or pedal feels spongy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/spongy-brake).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Are the pads still there and above the limit?**
- No: a missing or worn-out pad lets the piston travel too far. [Check brake pad wear](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-check), then [Replace disc brake pads](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-replace).
- Yes: go to step 5.

**5. Did it happen after long or hard braking, and come back after cooling?**
- Yes: the fluid boiled. [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed), then read [Brakes get weaker when hot](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-fade).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. With no leak and good pads, does the lever sink slowly when held?**
- Yes: the master cylinder is bypassing. [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check). Workshop.
- No: have a workshop bleed and check the whole system, including any CBS or ABS unit.

**7. Cable brake: is the cable broken, slipped out of the arm, or very loose?**
- Yes: replace or refit the cable and adjust. See [A control cable is frayed or stiff](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cable-frayed).
- No: the shoes are worn or the cam has gone over centre. [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust); if the adjuster is at its end, [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid leak or empty reservoir | Wet fittings, low level | [Check brake hoses and banjo bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-hose-check), then bleed |
| Air or boiled fluid | Soft, recent work or hot brakes | [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed) |
| Pads worn out or knocked back | Firm after pumping; thin pads | [Replace disc brake pads](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-replace) |
| Broken or slipped cable (drum) | Cable loose or detached | Replace cable and [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust) |
| Worn shoes or cam over centre (drum) | Adjuster at end of thread | [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service) |
| Master cylinder bypassing | Lever sinks with no leak | [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check) |
