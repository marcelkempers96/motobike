# Burning smell from the wiring

You smell hot or burning plastic, see smoke from under the seat, tank or headlight, or find a wire or connector that is melted or very hot. Electrical heat comes from too much current (a short circuit or an overloaded wire) or from a poor connection that resists the current and heats up. Either can start a fire, especially near fuel.

## Is it safe to ride?
**No. Stop now.** Pull over, turn the key off and get off the bike. If there is smoke or flame, move away and call for help; use a fire extinguisher only if it is safe. When there is no smoke, disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal. Do not ride again until the cause is found and repaired.

## Quick checks first
- Is it really electrical? Clutch, brakes, oil on the exhaust and a plastic bag on the exhaust also smell hot. A brake that drags makes the wheel hot: see [Brake drags and the wheel does not spin freely](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).
- Electric motorcycle: if the smell comes from the battery area, follow [Battery pack is hot, hissing or smells](/moto-zero/repair/problems/ev-pack-hot-smell) and keep away.
- Battery bulging, hissing or very hot: [Battery is boiling, swollen or leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-swollen).
- Where is the smell strongest: under the seat (battery, fuses, regulator), at the headlight, at the handlebar switches, at an accessory?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is any fuse blown?** Battery still disconnected. Check every fuse: [Check fuses and relays](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuse-relay-check).
- A fuse blew: there is a short on that circuit. Go to step 3.
- No fuse blown: the heat came from a poor connection or an overload below the fuse rating. Go to step 2.

**2. Which connector or wire is melted or brown?** Look and feel (only once cold) at: the regulator rectifier plug and the generator (stator) plug, the main fuse holder, the battery terminals, the headlight plug, the handlebar switch, and any accessory wiring.
- Regulator or stator plug burnt: very common. Go to [Regulator is very hot or the connector has melted](/moto-zero/repair/problems/regulator-hot) or [Burnt smell from the generator or a dark stator connector](/moto-zero/repair/problems/stator-burnt). The K-Light regulator plug checks are on PDF p. 79.
- Fuse holder or connector burnt: a loose contact was heating. Replace the connector or holder with new terminals: [Clean and seal electrical connectors](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/connector-clean). Then go to step 4.
- Headlight plug or switch burnt: often an over-powered bulb. Check the bulb wattage.
- Accessory wiring burnt: go to step 5.

**3. Where is the short?** Do not fit a new fuse and switch on to "see what happens". Use the meter, power off: [Find a short circuit](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/short-circuit-find). MAVCC reminds us that a blown fuse means a pinched, bare or shorted wire somewhere (PDF p. 313).

**4. Will the repair stay cool?** After repairing, reconnect the battery and run the circuit. Set the multimeter to DC volts (V with a straight line) and measure across the repaired joint while it carries current, one probe on each side of it.
- A reading close to zero: good.
- A clear reading: the joint still has resistance and will heat again: [Do a voltage drop test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/voltage-drop-test).

**5. Was an accessory involved?** Accessories connected without a fuse, with thin wire, or taking power through a light or switch wire are a common cause. Rewire it correctly: [Wire an accessory safely](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/accessory-wiring).

**6. Harness badly damaged?** If insulation has melted along a length of harness, wires inside may be joined. Replacing the harness is workshop work. Ask for the correct harness for your model (the K-Light 125 diagram is on PDF p. 278).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Burnt regulator or stator connector | Brown, melted plug | [Test the regulator rectifier](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/regulator-test) |
| Short circuit (wire rubbed through) | Fuse blown, continuity to frame | [Find a short circuit](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/short-circuit-find) |
| Loose, corroded connector or fuse holder heating | Melted plastic at one joint | [Clean and seal electrical connectors](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/connector-clean) |
| Badly fitted accessory | Burnt accessory wire or tap | [Wire an accessory safely](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/accessory-wiring) |
| Over-powered bulb | Higher wattage than specified | Fit correct bulb |
| Overcharging battery | Battery hot, swollen | [Battery is boiling, swollen or leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-swollen) |
