# Foot pedal or footpeg is stiff, bent or loose

A footpeg twists or wobbles under your foot, a folding peg will not fold or spring back, or the rear brake pedal is stiff, bent, or slow to return. A brake pedal that does not return fully keeps the rear brake dragging and the brake light on. This page covers footpegs and the rear brake pedal; gear and kickstart levers have their own pages.

## Is it safe to ride?
With care for a loose footpeg once tightened. Do not ride if the brake pedal does not return, is bent so it is hard to use, or the peg bracket is cracked.

## Quick checks first
- Press and release the brake pedal: does it return fully to its stop?
- Try to twist each footpeg.
- Look at the brake light while pressing and releasing the pedal.
- Look for cracks at the peg and pedal brackets.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is it the brake pedal, a footpeg, the gear lever or the kickstart?**
- Gear lever: [Gear lever does not spring back](/moto-zero/repair/problems/gear-lever-no-return) or [Gear lever is loose, bent or slipping](/moto-zero/repair/problems/gear-lever-loose).
- Kickstart: [Kickstarter will not move or does not spring back](/moto-zero/repair/problems/kickstart-jammed).
- Brake pedal: go to step 2.
- Footpeg: go to step 5.

**2. Is the brake pedal bent or cracked?**
- Yes: fit a new pedal. Do not straighten it. Then go to step 4.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Disconnect the brake rod or master cylinder pushrod. Does the pedal now move freely and return with its spring?**
- No: the pivot is dry or worn, or the return spring is weak. Clean, grease, and fit a new spring if needed ([Service footpegs and foot pedals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/pedal-peg-service)).
- Yes: the stiffness is in the brake. Drum: a dry cam ([Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service)). Disc: [Brake drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).

**4. Set pedal height and free play. Does the brake light go off when the pedal is released?**
- Set: [Adjust the rear brake pedal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-pedal-adjust) or [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust). **K-Light 125 free play: 20 to 30 mm.**
- Light stays on: [Test and adjust the brake light switches](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-switch-test).

**5. Footpeg: does the whole peg bracket move?**
- Yes: tighten the mounting bolts to the bike's value (**K-Light 125 footrest bolt: 45 to 50 N·m**). If it loosens again, look for a stripped thread or a crack ([Crack in the frame or a bracket](/moto-zero/repair/problems/frame-crack)).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Folding peg stiff or floppy?**
- Yes: clean and grease the hinge pin, fit a new spring or split pin ([Service footpegs and foot pedals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/pedal-peg-service)). Replace a bent peg.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dry or worn pedal pivot | Pedal stiff with rod disconnected | [Pedal service](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/pedal-peg-service) |
| Weak return spring | Pedal returns slowly | New spring |
| Loose peg bolts | Bracket moves | [Fastener check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check) |
| Bent pedal or peg after a fall | Visible bend | Replace part |
| Brake sticking | Pedal free alone, stiff with brake | [Brake drag](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag) |
