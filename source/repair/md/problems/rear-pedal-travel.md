# Rear brake pedal travels too far

You have to push the rear brake pedal a long way down before the brake works, or it reaches the footpeg or its stop. On a drum brake this is usually adjustment or worn shoes. On a hydraulic (disc) rear brake it suggests air, a low fluid level, a worn master cylinder, or the pedal and pushrod set wrongly.

## Is it safe to ride?
No, until the pedal works within its normal travel. This is usually a quick fix.

## Quick checks first
- Measure the free play at the pedal pad (K-Light 125: 20 to 30 mm).
- Drum brake: look at the wear indicator pointer.
- Hydraulic brake: check the rear reservoir (and CBS reservoir) level.
- Has the chain or rear wheel just been adjusted?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the rear brake a drum (rod or cable) or a disc (hydraulic)?**
- Drum: go to step 2.
- Disc: go to step 4.

**2. Drum: does turning the adjuster in bring the free play back to the manual value?**
- Yes: [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust). Check the brake light after adjusting.
- No, the adjuster is at its end: go to step 3.

**3. Is the wear pointer past its mark, or the brake arm past a right angle when applied?**
- Yes: worn shoes or drum. [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service).
- No: the cam or arm may have been refitted on the wrong spline. Check during the service.

**4. Disc: is the fluid level above LOWER in the rear (and CBS) reservoir?**
- No: check pads with [Check brake pad wear](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-check) and look for leaks. See [Brake fluid is leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-fluid-leak).
- Yes: go to step 5.

**5. Is the pedal free play too large because the pushrod or stop bolt is set wrong?**
- Yes: [Adjust the rear brake pedal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-pedal-adjust). On the K-Light 125, the pushrod should leave **0 to 0.2 mm** gap at the piston and the pedal free play should be **20 to 30 mm**.
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Does the pedal feel soft, or sink when held?**
- Soft: air. [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed); on a CBS bike use [Check a combined braking system (CBS)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cbs-check).
- Sinks: [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check), and on CBS bikes the CBS internal leak test.

**7. Is the pedal bent, or its pivot worn so it moves sideways?**
- Yes: see [Foot pedal or footpeg is stiff, bent or loose](/moto-zero/repair/problems/pedal-stiff).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Drum brake needs adjustment | Large free play; adjuster has thread left | [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust) |
| Worn drum shoes | Pointer past mark | [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service) |
| Pedal or pushrod set wrong | Free play outside manual range | [Adjust the rear brake pedal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-pedal-adjust) |
| Air or low fluid (disc) | Soft pedal; low reservoir | [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed) |
| Worn master cylinder or CBS leak | Pedal sinks when held | [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check) |
