# Rim is dented or cracked

A hard hit on a pothole, kerb or speed bump can dent the edge of the rim, bend it, or crack it. Cast alloy wheels can crack between the spokes or at the rim edge; steel spoked rims usually dent or bend. A damaged rim can lose air, unbalance the wheel, and a cracked wheel can break apart.

## Is it safe to ride?
No if there is any crack, or if the dent is large enough to make the tyre lose air or the wheel wobble. A small cosmetic scrape on the outer face is not a dent.

## Quick checks first
- Clean the rim and look closely, inside and outside, with a torch.
- Check the edge where the tyre sits: flat spots or bends.
- Cast wheels: look between spokes and around the hub for fine lines.
- Spin the wheel: wobble or bump?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is there a crack anywhere (a fine line that does not wipe off, often with grey dust or air bubbles)?**
- Yes: do not ride. The wheel or rim must be replaced. Cracked cast wheels must not be welded for road use.
- Not sure: spray soapy water with the tyre inflated; bubbles from a line mean a crack. Ask a wheel shop to dye-test it.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Is there a dent in the rim edge where the tyre bead sits?**
- Yes: go to step 3.
- No: go to step 4.

**3. Does the tyre lose air at the dent, or is the dent deeper than a scrape?**
- Yes: a wheel shop can sometimes dress a small dent on a steel or alloy rim; a large dent means a new rim or wheel. Fit a new tyre if the bead was also cut ([Inspect tyres](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-inspect)).
- No: monitor. Check pressure every ride for a week.

**4. Measure runout ([Check a wheel for runout](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-runout-check)). Over the limit?** (**K-Light 125: 2.0 mm lateral and radial.**)
- No: the rim is serviceable. Check the tyre too ([Tyre has a bulge or cut](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tyre-bulge)).
- Yes, spoked wheel: if the rim is not dented, it may true up with the spokes ([Check and tighten spokes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spoke-tension)). If it is bent, it needs a new rim and respoking.
- Yes, cast wheel: replace the wheel. Do not heat or hammer it straight.

**5. Was the hit hard enough to affect the fork, steering or axle?**
- Yes: see [The bike has been dropped or crashed](/moto-zero/repair/problems/crash-check).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pothole or kerb hit with low pressure | Dent at bead edge | Wheel shop or new rim |
| Bent spoked rim | Runout over limit, tight spokes | New rim, respoke |
| Cracked cast wheel | Fine line, bubbles | New wheel |
| Corrosion at bead seat | White deposits, bead leak | Clean seat ([Bead leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak)) |
