# Struggles to climb hills or carry a passenger

On flat roads the bike seems fine, but on a hill, with a passenger or with cargo, it slows down, needs a lower gear than before, or cannot keep going. A hill or a heavy load asks for the most pulling force. Weak points that hide on the flat show up here: a slipping clutch, a soft rear tyre, a chain problem, or an engine that is already weak.

## Is it safe to ride?

Yes, with care. Do not overload the bike; carry only what it is designed for, and shift down early on hills.

## Quick checks first

- Is the rear tyre pressure correct for the load? Many bikes need more pressure with a passenger.
- Is the load within the bike's limit?
- On a hill, do the revs rise without the bike going faster?
- Has anyone changed the sprockets?
- Is there a rattling sound (pinking) as you climb?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. On a hill in a middle gear, do the revs rise while the speed stays the same or drops?**
- Yes, manual clutch: see [Clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-slip). Check [clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) first (K-Light 125: 10 to 15 mm).
- Yes, scooter: see [Scooter revs but accelerates slowly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cvt-slip).
- Yes, step-through semi-automatic: see [Semi-automatic clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/auto-clutch-fault).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Does the engine misfire, jerk or rattle as you climb?**
- Misfire or jerking: [Misfires when accelerating hard or climbing](/moto-zero/repair/problems/misfire-under-load).
- Light metallic rattle: [Pinging or rattling when accelerating](/moto-zero/repair/problems/pinking). Shift down and stop labouring the engine.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Are tyre pressure, chain slack and wheel spin correct?**
- Tyres: [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check) for the load.
- Chain: [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust). A chain that is too tight wastes power and wears the gearbox bearing.
- Wheels that do not spin freely: [Bike feels held back](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bike-feels-held-back).
- All fine: go to step 4.

**4. Have the sprockets been changed?** Count the teeth on the front and rear sprockets and compare with your manual (K-Light 125 rear: 41 teeth). A bigger front or smaller rear sprocket makes the bike faster on the flat but weaker on hills.
- Changed: refit the standard sizes, with a new chain if worn.
- Standard: go to step 5.

**5. Does the power fade only on long climbs, as the engine gets hot?**
- Yes: see [Power fades as the engine gets hot](/moto-zero/repair/problems/power-fades-hot).
- No, it is weak in general: go to [Engine feels weak and down on power](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-power). Check the [air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service), [spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-service) and [compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test).

Use a [road test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/road-test) on the same hill before and after each fix.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Overload or low rear tyre pressure | Load check, gauge | [Tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check) |
| Clutch or CVT slip | Revs rise without speed | [Clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-slip) |
| Labouring in too high a gear | Pinking, engine bogs | Shift down |
| Chain too tight or dragging brake | Wheel spin test | [Chain adjust](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust) |
| Non-standard sprockets | Count teeth | Refit standard sprockets |
| Weak engine | See low power | [Low power](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-power) |
