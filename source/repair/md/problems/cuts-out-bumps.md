# Cuts out when riding over bumps

The engine stops, or stutters, when you hit a pothole or a rough patch, and often restarts straight away. A sudden cut-out on a bump is almost always electrical: a loose battery terminal, fuse, connector, switch or plug cap that loses contact for a moment. Fuel problems rarely react to bumps, except a very low tank sloshing away from the pickup.

## Is it safe to ride?

With care. Until it is fixed, avoid fast roads and heavy traffic: a cut-out removes engine braking and can leave you stopped in a traffic lane.

## Quick checks first

- Wiggle the battery leads with the engine running (on the stand). Does it stutter?
- Is the fuse holder tight and the fuse not loose in its clip?
- Flick the kill switch and the side stand: do they feel loose?
- Pull the plug cap: does it click firmly onto the plug?
- Do the lights or dashboard flicker at the same time?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Do the lights and dashboard also go out or flicker when it cuts out?**
- Yes: the main power is interrupted. Go to step 2.
- No, only the engine stops: go to step 4.

**2. Are the battery terminals tight and clean, and is the battery firmly held?**
- No: [Clean and tighten battery terminals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-terminal-clean). A loose battery can also break its own connections.
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Is the main fuse and its holder tight, and does the ignition switch cut out when you wiggle the key?**
- Loose fuse or corroded holder: [Check fuses and relays](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuse-relay-check) (K-Light 125: 20 A main fuse).
- Wiggling the key cuts out: [Test the ignition switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ignition-switch-test).
- Neither: check the main earth: [Clean and tighten earth connections](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/earth-clean).

**4. With the engine idling on the stand, flick the kill switch and tap the switch housing. Does it cut?**
- Yes: [Test the engine stop switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/kill-switch-test).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Tap the side stand and its switch with the bike on the centre stand, in gear with the clutch pulled (or follow your bike's interlock). Does it cut?**
- Yes: see [Engine dies when the side stand moves](/moto-zero/repair/problems/dies-side-stand).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Wiggle the plug cap, the coil wires and the pick-up connector one at a time with the engine idling (do not touch the plug lead with bare hands while running; use an insulated tool). Does it stutter?**
- Yes: refit the cap firmly and [clean and seal the connector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/connector-clean).
- No: go to step 7.

**7. Still cutting out?** Follow the harness where it bends at the steering head and under the seat: [Find a wiring fault](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wiring-fault-find) and [Electrical faults come and go](/moto-zero/repair/problems/intermittent-electrical). If it only happens on a nearly empty tank, keep more fuel in it.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Loose or corroded battery terminal | Stutters when terminal is wiggled | [Battery terminals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-terminal-clean) |
| Loose fuse or holder | Fuse moves in its clip | [Fuses](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuse-relay-check) |
| Worn kill switch | Cuts when housing is tapped | [Kill switch test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/kill-switch-test) |
| Side stand switch | Cuts when stand is touched | [Interlock test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/interlock-switch-test) |
| Worn ignition switch | Cuts when key is wiggled | [Ignition switch test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ignition-switch-test) |
| Loose plug cap or connector | Stutters when wiggled | [Connector clean](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/connector-clean) |
| Broken wire at steering head | Cuts at full steering lock | [Wiring fault find](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wiring-fault-find) |
