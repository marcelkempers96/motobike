# Blue or grey smoke from the exhaust

Blue or grey smoke means engine oil is burning in the cylinder. On a four-stroke, oil gets in past worn piston rings, past worn valve guides and stem seals, or because the sump is overfilled. The oil level falls and plugs get oily. On a two-stroke, light blue smoke is normal because oil is burned with the fuel; heavy smoke means too much oil or a fault in the oil pump or crankcase seals. The K-Light manual calls this "white" smoke in its chart; it is the same fault.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care. Check the oil level often and carry oil. Stop if the oil level is below the lower mark or the engine loses power.

## Quick checks first
- Oil level: is it above the upper mark?
- When does it smoke: on acceleration, after idling, all the time?
- Is the spark plug wet with oil?
- Two-stroke: has the premix ratio or pump setting been changed?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Confirm it is oil with [Diagnose exhaust smoke by colour](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/smoke-diagnosis). Is the card oily, and does the smoke stay when warm?**
- No, it clears when warm and drips water: condensation, normal.
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Two-stroke: is the smoke heavy and has oil started dripping from the exhaust?**
- Yes: go to [Oil dripping from the exhaust (two-stroke)](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oily-exhaust) and [Check the two-stroke oil pump and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-oil-pump).
- Four-stroke: go to step 3.

**3. Is the oil above the upper mark? Check with [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check).**
- Yes: drain to the correct level. The K-Light chart puts this first. If the level rises again, see [Oil smells of petrol or the level rises](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-smells-fuel).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does it smoke most on opening the throttle and under load, and does smoke also come from the oil filler or breather at high revs?**
- Yes: worn rings, piston or cylinder (blow-by). Check [Check the crankcase breather](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/breather-check), then go to step 6.
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Does it puff smoke after idling or when the throttle is closed (for example going downhill), then clear?**
- Yes: valve stem seals or worn valve guides. The K-Light chart points here when filler smoke is absent. Go to step 6.
- No: go to step 6 anyway.

**6. Do [Test engine compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test) with the wet test.**
- Low and rises when wet: rings and cylinder. Go to [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment).
- Normal compression: valve stem seals or guides are more likely. Same workshop job, head only.

**What happens if you ignore it:** oil use rises, plugs foul, the catalytic converter can be damaged, and running low on oil can seize the engine.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Oil overfilled | Level above upper mark | [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) |
| Worn rings or cylinder | Smoke on load, wet test rises | [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment) |
| Worn valve stem seals or guides | Smoke after idling, normal compression | [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment) |
| Two-stroke oil setting too rich | Heavy smoke, oily exhaust | [Check the two-stroke oil pump and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-oil-pump) |
| Blocked breather | Oil in air box | [Check the crankcase breather](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/breather-check) |
| Rings and bore worn early by dirt | Dust inside the intake, scratched bore | [Engine wears out early after dirt gets past the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/problems/engine-dust-wear) |
