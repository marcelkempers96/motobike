# Rattling from panels, mudguards or the seat

A buzz, rattle or knock comes from the body of the bike: side panels, fairing, mudguards, number plate, seat, carrier or heat shield. It may appear at one engine speed, or only over bumps. Most rattles are a missing grommet, a broken tab or a loose bolt. The aim is to find it, then make sure the noise is not something more serious.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes, as long as nothing is about to fall off. A loose mudguard, number plate or carrier can drop into the wheel: fix those before riding.

## Quick checks first
- Push and pull the mudguards, number plate, carrier, seat and side panels by hand.
- Look for missing screws and rubber grommets.
- Open the seat: is the toolkit or battery loose?
- Is a heat shield or chain guard loose?
- Check lock washers and locknuts on the panel bolts. A flat or worn lock washer lets the bolt work loose.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the rattle happen with the bike standing still and the engine running?**
- Yes: it follows engine speed. Go to step 2.
- No, only when moving: go to step 3.

**2. With the engine at the rattling speed (on the stand, in neutral), touch panels and brackets lightly with a gloved hand one at a time. Does the rattle stop when you touch one?**
- Yes: fix that part ([Find and fix rattles](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rattle-fix)).
- No, and it seems to come from the engine itself: see [Ticking noise from the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick) or [New or increased engine vibration](/moto-zero/repair/problems/new-vibration).

**3. Does it rattle only over bumps?**
- Yes: tap and shake each body part with the engine off. Check the seat ([Seat is loose](/moto-zero/repair/problems/seat-loose)), the number plate, mudguards and the centre stand ([Centre stand is hard to use or scrapes](/moto-zero/repair/problems/centre-stand-fault)). If it is a clunk from the suspension, see [Clunk from the rear suspension](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-clunk) or [Knocking or squeaking from the front forks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fork-noise).
- No, it follows road speed: go to step 4.

**4. Does it come from the rear wheel area and change with chain movement?**
- Yes: the chain is hitting the guard or slider. [Chain rubs on the guard, slider or swingarm](/moto-zero/repair/problems/chain-rubbing).
- No: check the exhaust shields and mounts ([Rattling from the exhaust or heat shield](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-rattle)) and the mirrors ([Mirror is loose](/moto-zero/repair/problems/mirror-loose)).

**5. When you find it: is a tab broken, a grommet missing, or a bolt loose?**
- Fix it: new grommets or well nuts, foam tape at contact points, tighten bolts (**K-Light 125 body cover bolt: 9.0 N·m**). Then [Check chassis fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check) to catch other loose parts.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Missing grommet or loose panel screw | Rattle stops when panel is touched | [Find and fix rattles](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rattle-fix) |
| Broken panel tab | Panel moves, tab missing | Foam tape or new panel |
| Loose mudguard, number plate, carrier | Moves by hand | [Fastener check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fastener-check) |
| Loose heat shield | Buzz at one engine speed | [Exhaust mounts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check) |
| Chain hitting guard | Noise follows chain | [Chain guides](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-guide-check) |
