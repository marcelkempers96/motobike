# Oil is milky or creamy

Oil that looks like milky coffee or has a creamy yellow sludge under the filler cap has water in it. On air-cooled bikes the water usually comes from condensation (short trips that never warm the engine fully, cold damp weather) or from washing or riding through deep water. On liquid-cooled bikes it can also be coolant leaking in through the head gasket, a cracked head or the water pump seal. Water in oil stops it protecting bearings and causes rust.

## Is it safe to ride?
Do not ride until you know the cause. A little cream only under the filler cap after short winter trips is less serious; milky oil on the dipstick is serious.

## Quick checks first
- Is the cream only on the inside of the filler cap, or is the whole oil milky?
- Is the bike used only for short trips?
- Has it been through deep water or a flood, or washed with a pressure jet?
- Liquid-cooled: is the coolant level falling?
- Coolant pushing out of the radiator cap with the engine running points to a leak of combustion pressure into the coolant. Check the head gasket.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is there only a small amount of cream under the filler cap, and the oil on the dipstick looks normal?**
- Yes: condensation from short trips. Take a longer ride (at least 20 to 30 minutes, estimate) to warm the engine fully, then recheck. Change oil sooner than usual.
- No, the oil itself is milky: go to step 2.

**2. Has the bike been in deep water, a flood, or washed with water aimed at the engine?**
- Yes: water entered through the breather or air box. Go to step 4.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the engine liquid-cooled?**
- Yes: check the coolant with [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check). If it has fallen, go to step 5.
- No (air-cooled): water came from condensation or the breather. Go to step 4.

**4. Drain the oil and look at it with [Inspect drained oil for clues](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-inspect).** Change it with [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service). Check the air filter and air box for water. After a short run, check again with [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check).
- Stays clean: done. If it was flooded, change it again after a short distance.
- Turns milky again: go to step 5 (liquid-cooled) or ask a workshop (air-cooled).

**5. Liquid-cooled with coolant loss: find where the coolant enters with [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test).**
- Water pump weep hole wet or seal suspected: workshop pump repair.
- Bubbles in the coolant, white smoke: head gasket or cracked head. Ask for [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test).
- After the repair, the engine needs fresh oil, and bearings should be checked if it ran on milky oil: [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Condensation from short trips | Cream under cap only | Longer rides, earlier oil change |
| Water entry (flood, washing) | Recent water, wet air box | [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) |
| Head gasket leak (liquid-cooled) | Coolant loss, white smoke | [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test) |
| Water pump seal | Weep hole, coolant loss | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
| Cracked head | After overheating | [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) |
