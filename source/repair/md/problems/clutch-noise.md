# Rattle or whir from the clutch that changes when the lever is pulled

A noise from the clutch side of the engine (usually the right-hand cover) that changes when you pull or release the lever. Two patterns matter. A whir, squeal or grind that appears when the lever is pulled usually comes from the release bearing, which only spins while the clutch is released. A rattle at idle in neutral that goes quiet when you pull the lever usually comes from play between the clutch basket and its drive gear, or worn basket slots. A light rattle is normal on some engines; a new or growing noise is the warning.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care and get it checked. A failing release bearing can break up and send pieces round the engine. If you hear grinding or the noise gets loud quickly, stop.

## Quick checks first
- Engine warm, in neutral, at idle: listen at the right-hand engine cover while you pull and release the lever.
- Check the oil level. Low oil makes clutch noise louder.
- Check lever free play. With no free play the release bearing never stops spinning.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the noise change when you pull and release the lever?**
- No: it is probably not the clutch. Try [Ticking noise from the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick), [Rattling noise from the cam chain area](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cam-chain-rattle) or [Rumbling or growling from inside the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bearing-rumble).
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Is the oil level correct and the oil in good condition?**
- No: [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) and correct it, then listen again.
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Is the lever free play correct? (K-Light 125: 10 to 15 mm.)**
- No: [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust). Free play protects the release bearing.
- Yes: go to step 4.

**4. Does the noise appear or get louder when you pull the lever (whir, squeal, grind)?**
- Yes: the release bearing or lifter is worn. Go to [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service).
- No, it is a rattle with the lever out that goes quiet when pulled: go to step 5.

**5. Is the rattle new or getting louder, or is there metal in the oil?**
- Metal flakes or chunks: stop riding. [Inspect drained oil for clues](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-inspect) and see [Metal particles or flakes in the oil](/moto-zero/repair/problems/metal-in-oil).
- New or louder rattle: worn basket slots, worn basket damper springs or play at the drive gear. Workshop: [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service).
- Faint and unchanged for a long time: compare with another bike of the same model; it may be normal.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low or old oil | Level low, oil dark | [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) |
| No free play | Lever pulls cable at once | [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) |
| Worn release bearing or lifter | Whir or squeal with lever pulled | [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service) |
| Basket slot wear or drive gear play | Rattle at idle that stops when lever pulled | [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service) |
