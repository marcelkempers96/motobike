# Strong smell of fuel around the bike

You smell petrol around the bike: when it is parked, after you stop, or while you ride. A faint smell for a moment after filling up is normal. A strong or lasting smell means fuel liquid or vapour is escaping, or the engine is burning too much fuel. Liquid fuel near hot parts is a fire risk, so find the source first.

## Is it safe to ride?

Not until you have found the source. If you see any wet fuel, treat it as a leak and see [Fuel is leaking or dripping](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-leak). A smell only from the exhaust is less urgent but should be fixed soon.

## Quick checks first

- Is anything wet: hoses, tap, carburettor, tank, ground?
- Where is the smell strongest: the tank and cap, the carburettor or throttle body, or the exhaust?
- Does it smell more after the bike has been parked on its side stand?
- Is the choke left on?
- Is the oil level rising, or does the oil smell of petrol?
- A float level a little high makes the mixture rich and a low one makes it lean. A level far too high leaks fuel from the overflow tube.


## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is any fuel visible, wet or dripping?**
- Yes: go to [Fuel is leaking or dripping](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-leak).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Is the smell strongest at the exhaust, with black smoke or a sooty plug?**
- Yes: the engine is running rich. Check the [choke](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/choke-check) and [air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service), then see [Engine runs rich](/moto-zero/repair/problems/runs-rich).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the smell strongest around the carburettor, especially after parking?**
- The float needle may be letting fuel past into the intake or out of the overflow while parked. Look for a damp overflow pipe end and a damp air box. [Check the carburettor float and needle valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check). Turn the fuel tap OFF when parking.
- Check the engine oil: if it smells of petrol, see [Oil smells of petrol](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-smells-fuel).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is the smell strongest around the tank and filler cap?**
- Check the cap seal and the vent: [Clean and lubricate the fuel cap](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-cap-service). Do not fill above the filler neck.
- On bikes with a vapour (EVAP) system, fuel vapour goes from the tank through a rollover valve to a charcoal canister. A cracked hose or a failed valve releases vapour. The K-Light 125 has this system; its manual says a damaged rollover valve is replaced. [Check the fuel tank vent](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-vent-check) and its hoses.
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Fuel-injected: is the smell near the throttle body or the pump on the tank?**
- A slow seep at the injector O-ring or the pump seal may evaporate before it drips. Key OFF and take it to a workshop: [Check the fuel injector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check) and [Check the fuel pump](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-pump-check).

**6. Still smells, nothing found?** With the tap ON, wrap clean paper around each hose joint for ten minutes and check for damp spots: [Check the fuel tap, lines and flow](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Small leak at a hose or tap | Damp paper at a joint | [Tap and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check) |
| Rich running or choke on | Smell at exhaust, black smoke | [Choke check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/choke-check) |
| Float needle passing fuel when parked | Damp overflow, smells after parking | [Float check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) |
| Cap seal, overfilling or vent hose | Smell at the cap | [Fuel cap](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-cap-service) |
| Vapour hose or rollover valve | Cracked hose, failed valve | [Tank vent](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-vent-check) |
| Injector or pump seal seep (injection) | Smell near throttle body or pump | [Injector check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check) |
