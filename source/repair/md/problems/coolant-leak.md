# Coolant is leaking

Coolant is usually green, blue, pink or orange and smells sweet. A leak shows as drips under the bike, wet hoses, crust at joints, steam, or a reserve tank that keeps needing top-ups. Outside leaks come from hoses and clips, the radiator, the water pump seal (it leaks from a small weep hole), the drain bolt, the reserve tank or the radiator cap seal. If the level falls with nothing visible outside, coolant may be leaking inside the engine.

## Is it safe to ride?
Not until the leak is found and the level is correct. A bike that loses coolant overheats, and coolant on the rear tyre is slippery.

## Quick checks first
- Wipe the liquid with white paper: coloured and sweet smelling?
- Engine cold: check the reserve tank level.
- Look at hoses, clips and the radiator bottom.
- Is the rear tyre wet?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the liquid really coolant?**
- Oily brown: [Oil leaking under the bike](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-leak).
- Clear water drips from the exhaust when cold: normal condensation.
- Clear water from the air conditioner of a car parked nearby, or rain: not your bike.
- Coloured and sweet: go to step 2.

**2. Engine cold, check with [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check). Is the level low?**
- Yes: top up to UPPER and go to step 3.
- No: the leak may be from the reserve tank overflow after hot running (a little is normal if overfilled). Recheck after a ride.

**3. Trace it with [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test). Where is it?**
- Hose or clip: tighten or replace the clip or hose. Retest.
- Radiator core or seam: go to [Radiator is damaged, bent or blocked](/moto-zero/repair/problems/radiator-damage).
- Water pump weep hole: the pump seal has failed. Workshop job; ride only after repair, because the seal can also let coolant into the oil.
- Drain bolt: fit a new sealing washer when you [Replace the coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-replace).
- Thermostat housing: workshop, see [Check the thermostat](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/thermostat-check).
- Nothing outside: go to step 4.

**4. Is the oil milky, or is there white smoke?**
- Yes: internal leak. Go to [Oil is milky or creamy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/milky-oil) or [White smoke or steam from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/white-smoke).
- No: ask a workshop for a pressure test and [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test).

**Do not** add leak-stop products unless the maker approves them. They can block narrow radiator tubes.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Loose clip or perished hose | Wet hose end | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
| Radiator damage | Wet core, stone damage | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
| Water pump seal | Weep hole wet | Workshop pump repair |
| Drain bolt washer | Wet at bolt | [Replace the coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-replace) |
| Head gasket (internal) | Milky oil, white smoke | [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test) |
