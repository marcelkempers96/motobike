# Engine runs hot and lean, pops and pinks

A **lean** mixture has too much air for the fuel. It burns slowly and hot. Signs: the engine runs hotter than usual, the exhaust pops when you close the throttle, the idle is high or hunts, the engine hesitates, and under load you may hear **pinking** (a light metallic rattle). The spark plug insulator looks white or pale. Lean running is the cause of many burnt valves and holed or seized pistons, especially on two-strokes.

## Is it safe to ride?

With care, gently and for short distances only. Avoid full throttle and long climbs. **Two-strokes, and any engine that pinks under light load: do not ride** until fixed, as the piston can seize and lock the rear wheel.

## Quick checks first

- Has the air filter, exhaust or carburettor jetting been changed recently?
- Is the intake rubber cracked or a vacuum pipe off?
- Is fuel flow good, or worse on a low tank?
- Is the exhaust leaking at the head joint (black soot, ticking)?
- Fuel-injected: is the warning light on?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Read the spark plug after a short run. Is the insulator white, chalky or blistered?**
- Yes: lean or overheating confirmed. See [Spark plug is white or blistered](/moto-zero/repair/problems/plug-white) and [Read a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-reading). Go to step 2.
- Normal tan or grey: the popping may be an exhaust leak. Go to step 3.

**2. Does the idle change when you spray water on the intake joints and vacuum pipes?**
- Yes: air leak. [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the exhaust leaking at the cylinder head or a joint?**
- Yes: outside air is pulled into the pipe and burns with unburnt fuel, so it pops on the overrun. On injected bikes it also fools the oxygen sensor. [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is fuel flow restricted? (Better with the fuel cap open, worse on a low tank, weak flow from the tap.)**
- Yes: [Check the tank vent](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tank-vent-check), [the tap and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check) and [the fuel filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-filter-replace).
- No: carburettor bikes go to step 5; fuel-injected go to step 6.

**5. Carburettor: are the jets clean and the float level correct?**
- A low float level and partly blocked jets give a lean mixture. [Check the float](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) and [clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean). If the air filter or exhaust has been changed to a freer-flowing type, the carburettor may need different jets; ask a workshop.

**6. Fuel-injected: is a code stored?**
- Yes: [Read engine fault codes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-code-read). K-Light 125 code P0131 means the oxygen sensor has read lean for too long.
- No: a workshop checks fuel pressure and the injector: [Check the fuel pump](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-pump-check), [Check the fuel injector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check), [Check the oxygen sensor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/o2-sensor-check).

**7. Does it still run hot with a correct mixture?** Check cooling and see [Air-cooled engine overheats](/moto-zero/repair/problems/overheating-air). Pinking with a correct mixture: see [Pinging or rattling when accelerating](/moto-zero/repair/problems/pinking).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Intake air leak | Water spray changes idle | [Intake leak test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
| Exhaust leak (popping) | Soot at joint, ticking | [Exhaust leak check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check) |
| Fuel restriction | Weak flow, cap test | [Tap and lines](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fuel-tap-line-check) |
| Low float or blocked jets | Carburettor inspection | [Clean carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean) |
| Free-flow filter or exhaust fitted | Parts changed | Refit standard parts or re-jet |
| Low fuel pressure or blocked injector | Workshop pressure test | [Injector check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check) |
