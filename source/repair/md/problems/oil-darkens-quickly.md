# Oil turns black very soon after a change

Modern oil carries soot and dirt in suspension so they drain out at the next change. Some darkening within a few hundred kilometres is normal, especially on an older engine. Oil that is black within a day or two, gritty, or sludgy points to blow-by (combustion gas leaking past rings), dust getting in through a damaged air filter or intake, a dirty engine that was not flushed, or a centrifugal filter that is packed full. Old sludge mixed into new oil also makes it dark fast.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes. Plan the checks below and change oil more often until the cause is found.

## Quick checks first
- Was the strainer cleaned at the last change?
- Is the air filter in good condition and sealed in its box?
- Any blue smoke or smoke from the breather?
- Is the oil black and smooth, or gritty?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Look at the oil and strainer with [Inspect drained oil for clues](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-inspect). Is it gritty or sandy to the touch?**
- Yes: dust is getting into the engine. Go to step 2.
- No, smooth and black: go to step 3.
- Metal flakes: go to [Metal particles or flakes in the oil](/moto-zero/repair/problems/metal-in-oil).

**2. Check the air filter and intake with [Clean or replace the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service) and [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test). Is the filter torn, badly fitted or the intake rubber split?**
- Yes: fix it now. Dust entering the engine wears rings and the cylinder quickly.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Was the engine full of old sludge when you started changing oil more often?**
- Yes: change the oil and clean the strainer with [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) twice at a short interval. Many engines also have a centrifugal filter that a workshop can clean.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is there smoke from the breather or oil filler at idle? Check with [Check the crankcase breather](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/breather-check).**
- Yes: blow-by. Go to step 5.
- No: some darkening is normal. Keep to the service interval in the manual (K-Light 125: every 3000 km) or shorter in dusty use.

**5. Do [Test engine compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test). Low, or rising with the wet test?**
- Yes: worn rings. See [Low compression](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-compression).
- No: rings may be partly stuck. Shorter oil intervals often help.

**Also check:** a rich mixture (black smoke) adds soot to the oil. See [Black smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/black-smoke).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Old sludge in a neglected engine | Black from the first day | [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) |
| Dust entry (filter or intake) | Gritty oil | [Clean or replace the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service) |
| Blow-by from worn rings | Breather smoke, low compression | [Test engine compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test) |
| Rich running | Black smoke, sooty plug | [Black smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/black-smoke) |
