# Black smoke from the exhaust

Black smoke is unburned fuel. The engine is getting too much fuel for the air it breathes (a rich mixture). Common causes are a blocked air filter, a choke left on or stuck, a carburettor float that lets fuel overflow, or on fuel-injected bikes a leaking injector or a faulty sensor. A rich engine wastes fuel, fouls spark plugs, smells of petrol, and over time can thin the engine oil and damage a catalytic converter.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes with care for a short distance. Stop if fuel is dripping, the oil level is rising, or the engine warning light is flashing.

## Quick checks first
- Is the choke lever or knob fully off?
- When was the air filter last cleaned or changed?
- Is fuel dripping from the carburettor overflow pipe?
- Is the engine warning light (MIL) on, on a fuel-injected bike?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Confirm the colour with [Diagnose exhaust smoke by colour](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/smoke-diagnosis). Does a white card held near the outlet get dry black soot?**
- Yes, dry soot: rich mixture. Go to step 2.
- Oily wet spots: this is oil. Go to [Blue or grey smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke).

**2. Is the choke fully off, and does the engine still smoke when warm?**
- Choke on or stuck: free it with [Check the choke or starter valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/choke-check). See [Choke is stuck on or will not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/choke-stuck).
- Choke off: go to step 3.

**3. Remove and look at the air filter with [Clean or replace the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service). Is it dirty, oily or wet?**
- Yes: clean or replace it. Never ride with the filter removed. Retest.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Carburettor bike: is fuel overflowing from the overflow pipe, or does the engine flood when parked?**
- Yes: go to [Fuel pours out of the carburettor overflow pipe](/moto-zero/repair/problems/carb-overflowing) and [Check the carburettor float and needle valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check).
- No: the jets or air passages may be worn or wrong. Do [Clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean). Do not guess jet sizes.

**5. Fuel-injected bike: is the warning light on, or are there stored codes? Read them with [Read engine fault codes (fuel injection)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-code-read).**
- Code for oxygen sensor, temperature sensor or MAP sensor: go to [Check the engine sensors (TPS, MAP, temperature)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-sensor-check).
- No code: the K-Light manual notes that an oxygen sensor reading high when fully warm points to a leaking injector. Go to [Check the fuel injector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check).

**6. Read the plug with [Read a spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spark-plug-reading). Black and dry confirms rich running.** A weak spark can also leave fuel unburned; see [Spark plug is black and sooty](/moto-zero/repair/problems/plug-black-sooty).

**7. Check the oil for a petrol smell.** If it smells of fuel, see [Oil smells of petrol or the level rises](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-smells-fuel) and change the oil after the fault is fixed.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Choke left on or stuck | Lever position, smoke when warm | [Check the choke or starter valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/choke-check) |
| Blocked air filter | Dirty element | [Clean or replace the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service) |
| Carburettor float or needle valve | Overflow, flooding | [Check the carburettor float and needle valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carb-float-check) |
| Dirty or worn carburettor | Black plug, no overflow | [Clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean) |
| EFI sensor fault | Fault code | [Check the engine sensors (TPS, MAP, temperature)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/efi-sensor-check) |
| Leaking injector | Rich with no code | [Check the fuel injector](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/injector-check) |
