## Before you work on a bike

- Work on a stable, level surface with the engine off and cool, unless a step says otherwise.
- Keep fuel away from sparks and flames. Battery acid, brake fluid and coolant harm skin, eyes and paint.
- Brakes, steering, tyres and electric high-voltage systems are safety-critical. If you are unsure, use a workshop.
- Values marked K-Light 125 are for that reference bike. Use your own bike's manual for its values.
