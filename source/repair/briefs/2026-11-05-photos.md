# Brief: choose repair-course photos for existing Repair pages

Repo: /home/user/motobike. The owner of dansmc.com has given permission to use his photos.

## Goal
Produce a mapping from existing Motopedia Repair pages to the best photos in `source/dansmc/`.

## Inputs
- `source/repair/briefs/existing-ids.md`: all repair, problem and part ids with titles.
- `source/dansmc/_index/manifest_all.csv`: one row per photo: folder, lesson_no, page_title,
  page_url, photo_file, caption, text_before, text_after. Files sit at
  `source/dansmc/<section>/<lesson_no>/<photo_file>`; find each with `ls` or `find`.
- Only files that exist on disk count (lessons 001 to 025 and 086 to 151 were uploaded).

## Rules
- Parts: for every part id, pick 0 to 3 photos that clearly show THAT part (real part, close and
  clear). Prefer photos showing the part on its own or fitted. Skip a part when nothing fits.
- Repairs: for every repair id, pick 0 to 4 photos that show a step of THAT job (tool in use, the
  measurement, the adjustment). Order them in job order. Skip when nothing fits.
- Never use: logos, buttons, banners, "home index", "click here", donation or e-mail graphics,
  people's faces as the subject, screenshots of web pages, cartoons, jokes. Most `.gif` files are
  site buttons: skip a gif unless the caption or text shows it is a real diagram.
- Judge from the file name, caption, page title and text_before/text_after. Open an image with the
  Read tool when unsure (keep this under 60 image views in total).
- For each photo write a short plain caption (at most 12 words, site style, no first person),
  for example "Measuring valve clearance with a feeler gauge".

## Output
Write `source/repair/photo-map.json`:
```
{"parts": {"<part_id>": [{"file": "source/dansmc/.../x.jpg", "caption": "...", "page": "<page_url>"}]},
 "repairs": {"<repair_id>": [ ...same shape... ]}}
```
and a report `source/repair/photo-map-notes.md` (at most 40 lines): counts, parts and repairs that got
no photo, anything doubtful.

## Do not
Edit any other file. Commit or push. Budget: 150 tool calls.
Reply in at most 10 lines with the report path.
