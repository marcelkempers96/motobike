- Aim for the most useful 8 to 15 new repair pages and 0 to 5 new problem pages for your section.

- Language: the current site style. Plain, short, calm, impersonal. No first person, no anecdotes,

  rises on a wet test points to rings"). Do NOT copy model-specific specs from the source; say

  the 1 to 3 source image file paths that would illustrate it best.
