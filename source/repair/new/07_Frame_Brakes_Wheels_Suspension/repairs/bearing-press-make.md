# Make a simple press to fit wheel bearings

A new wheel bearing must go in straight and be pushed only on its outer ring. Hammering often tilts it or presses on the wrong part. A press made from a threaded rod pulls the bearing in slowly and evenly. It can be made from items sold in most hardware shops.

## Safety
- Wear eye protection.
- Press only on the outer ring of the bearing. Pressure on the inner ring or the balls ruins the bearing.
- Support the wheel flat on the bench so it cannot fall.
- Do not use this on a hub you have heated with a flame.

## You need
- A threaded rod longer than the hub plus the bearing, and four nuts.
- Two large flat washers or plates, one bigger than the bearing bore. These spread the load.
- A socket or a short piece of pipe whose edge is the same size as the bearing's outer ring.
- Bearing grease for the outer ring and seal lips.

## Steps
1. Clean the hub bore. Check it for scoring or damage. A damaged bore needs a new hub or a machine shop.
2. Lay the new bearing on the bore, square. Make sure the spacer between the bearings is in place before the second bearing goes in.
3. Pass the rod through the hub, through the bearing, and place the socket or pipe on the bearing outer ring. Put a large washer and a nut on each end of the rod.
4. Hold one nut with a spanner and turn the other. The bearing is drawn in slowly. Stop if it goes in at an angle and start again.
5. Stop when the bearing sits flat against its seat. It should stop moving.
6. Fit the second bearing from the opposite side the same way.
7. Fit new seals with a little grease on the lips.

## Check your work
- The bearings turn smoothly by hand with no roughness.
- The wheel spins freely and the axle slides through the spacers.
- There is no play when you hold the axle and rock the hub.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Bearing is rough or loose: [Check wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-check).
- Bearing outer ring will not come out of the hub: [Replace wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-replace).
