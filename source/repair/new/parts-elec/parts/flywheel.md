# Flywheel (magnet rotor)

On many small bikes the flywheel is the outer rotor on the end of the crankshaft. It holds strong magnets and turns around the [stator](/moto-zero/repair/parts/stator) coils. It also adds weight to smooth the engine, and may carry the timing marks, a trigger magnet, a starter-clutch gear or the cooling fan.

## What it does
As the magnets sweep past the stator coils, they make the electricity for ignition, lights and charging. In a CDI system a small trigger magnet on the rim tells the ignition when to fire. On a points magneto the flywheel also hides the points, which are timed to marks on its rim.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- No spark or weak charge with a good stator: damaged or weakened magnets. See [Check an alternator rotor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/alternator-rotor-check) and [No spark at the spark plug](/moto-zero/repair/problems/no-spark).
- Metal chips or magnetic debris stuck inside the rotor.
- Rub marks on the magnets or stator from a worn crankshaft bearing.
- Loose or damaged key and keyway: timing that jumps and a bike that loses spark after a kickback. See [Kickstarter kicks back against my foot](/moto-zero/repair/problems/kickstart-kickback).
- Cracked rotor or damaged taper.

## When to replace it
Replace a flywheel with a cracked rim, damaged taper, damaged keyway or weak magnets. Clean off metal debris first and check the stator for damage. The flywheel is normally replaced as a complete part.

## What to match when buying
- The exact model, since flywheels differ in magnet layout, trigger magnet position, weight and taper.
- Whether it has a starter clutch or a cooling fan.
- Use a pre-owned part only if its magnets and keyway are in good condition.

## Fitting notes
- Remove it only with the correct puller, never with a hammer on the rim. See [Remove and refit a flywheel](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/flywheel-remove).
- Keep the key in place and tighten the centre nut to the value in your bike's manual.
- Keep magnets and metal bits away from the stator and trigger.
- Reset the timing after refitting. See [Set points timing on a flywheel magneto](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/magneto-points-timing).
