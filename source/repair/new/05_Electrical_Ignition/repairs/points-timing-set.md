# Set points gap and ignition timing (battery and coil ignition)

On a bike with contact breaker points and a battery, the spark fires at the moment the points open. This must happen at the right piston position, shown by a mark on the flywheel or rotor and a fixed mark on the crankcase. The test lamp shows the exact moment the points open. Wrong timing costs power, makes the engine hot and can cause pinking.

## Safety
- Key off while turning the engine by hand. Remove the spark plug so the engine turns easily.
- Disconnect the battery negative (-) for adjusting. Reconnect it only for the lamp test.
- A kickstart or a loose rotor can snap back. Keep fingers clear of the cam and the points arm.

## You need
- Your bike's manual: marks, rotation direction and points gap.
- A test lamp.
- New or clean points: [Inspect, clean and replace contact breaker points](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/contact-points-replace).

## Steps
1. Find the timing marks. Often there is an F or a line on the flywheel that must meet a fixed mark on the crankcase. Other makers use different letters or symbols, so check the manual.
2. Turn the engine until the cam lifts the points heel to its highest point. Check the gap with the feeler gauge and set it to the manual's value. Tighten the screws and check again.
3. Connect the lamp: one lead to the engine (earth), the other to the points wire at the movable contact or spring. Reconnect the battery. Switch the ignition on.
4. Turn the engine slowly backwards until the points close and the lamp goes out, then keep going until the flywheel mark has gone well past the crankcase mark. This takes up any play in the advance unit.
5. Turn the engine forward, slowly and in its normal direction, to the mark. The lamp must light just as the two marks line up.
6. If it does not, loosen the screws holding the points plate and turn the plate a little. Tighten and repeat step 4 and 5. The plate may move when you tighten it, so check again after each tightening.
7. If timing moved forward when you tightened, loosen it again and set slightly late, so it ends up right when tight. Do the opposite if it moves late.
8. Points gap changes the timing slightly, and the time the points stay closed (dwell) charges the coil. Do not use the gap to correct timing beyond the manual's range.
9. Twin cylinders with two sets of points: time each set separately. Set the first with the plate, then adjust the second with its own gap or its own sub-plate, then check the second gap is still in the range.
10. Clean the points, grease the cam, refit the cover.

## Check your work
- The lamp lights at the mark on both cylinders.
- The gap is inside the manual's range.
- The engine starts easily and does not ping.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Engine pinks or runs hot: back to the manual's timing. Do not advance beyond the mark. See [Pinging or rattling sound when accelerating](/moto-zero/repair/problems/pinking).
- No lamp signal: check feed with the battery connected and the key on. See [Test the ignition switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/ignition-switch-test).
