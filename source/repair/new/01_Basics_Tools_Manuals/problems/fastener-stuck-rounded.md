# Screw or bolt will not come out, or its head is damaged

A screw spins without moving, the tool slips, the head is round, or a bolt snaps off. This happens most with steel cross-head screws in aluminium engine cases, and with old bolts in rusty parts. The right tool and a gentle first approach save the part.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride only if the fastener holds nothing important. A loose or damaged fastener on a wheel, brake, steering or engine mount: do not ride until it is fixed.

## Quick checks first
- Is the tool the right size and fully seated, with the recess clean?
- Is it a screw into an engine case, a bolt through a part, or a bolt that holds a brake or wheel?
- Is the head rounded, or still sharp?
- Has the bolt rusted into place, or was it over-tightened?
- Do you have penetrating oil, an impact driver and a drill?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the head still square or has a clean recess?**
- Yes: soak with penetrating oil. Try the correct size 6-point socket or bit: [Choose and use spanners and sockets without rounding fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spanner-socket-use). Go to step 2 if it still does not move.
- No, rounded: go to step 3.

**2. Is it a cross-head or hex screw into an aluminium case?**
- Yes: use an impact driver with the right bit: [Loosen a tight screw with an impact driver](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/impact-driver-use). Support the part behind it.
- No (a bolt through a bracket or frame): heat and oil may help; use a longer handle and a steady pull.

**3. Can you grip the head?**
- Yes: try grinding paste in the recess, a slightly smaller socket tapped on, or locking pliers.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Has the head snapped off, with the thread inside?**
- Yes: [Remove a rounded, seized or broken fastener](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stuck-fastener-removal): drill and extract.
- If an extractor breaks, stop. Take it to a workshop.

**5. Is the fastener on a brake, wheel, steering or engine mount?**
- Yes: stop. Take it to a workshop. See [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check).

What happens if you force it: the head rounds more, a cast part cracks, or a hardened extractor snaps and cannot be drilled out.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Ill-fitting tool | Tool wobbles on head | [Choose and use spanners and sockets without rounding fasteners](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/spanner-socket-use) |
| Steel screw seized in aluminium | Cross-head screw in a case, tool slips | [Loosen a tight screw with an impact driver](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/impact-driver-use) |
| Rounded head | Head corners worn smooth | [Remove a rounded, seized or broken fastener](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stuck-fastener-removal) |
| Broken bolt | Head sheared, stump in the hole | [Remove a rounded, seized or broken fastener](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stuck-fastener-removal) |
| Missing basic tools | No impact driver or right-size socket | [Build a basic motorcycle tool kit](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/basic-tool-kit) |
