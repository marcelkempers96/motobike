## Course
- Spark test: a plug with the ground electrode bent out, grounded, should jump a wide gap in open air; if not, it fails under compression (011).
- Leak path test: air in at TDC; sound at intake = intake valve, exhaust = exhaust valve, breather = rings (013).
- Kickback: ignition fires before TDC; retarded start timing and centrifugal advance reduce it (021_magneto1).
- Magneto points carry only a low voltage, so heel wear matters more than contact wear (021_magneto1).
- Fact types for diagnosis: repeatable, eye witness, probability, history (015).
- Fuel in a hot exhaust can explode, so use only a small amount in the fuel shot test (013).
