# Oil filter wrench

A tool that grips a spin-on oil filter so it can be removed and fitted. Types include a cup or T-handle wrench that fits over the end of the filter, and a strap wrench.

## What it does
It turns the filter without crushing it. A cup wrench is the best fit for the job. A small strap wrench works but can dent the side of the filter during fitting.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- The cup slips or is rounded inside.
- The strap is frayed or no longer grips.

## When to replace it
Replace it when it slips or damages filters.

## What to match when buying
- The size of the end of the filter on the engine. Check the filter part number or measure it.
- A small strap wrench for tight places.
- Some engines have the filter inside the oil pan, behind a cover. These need the right tool through the cover.

## Fitting notes
A filter that is dented slightly is usually fine. A badly crushed filter must be replaced. Screw the new filter on by hand until the rubber ring touches the base, then turn it by the amount in the manual. See [Change a spin-on or paper oil filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-filter-change).
