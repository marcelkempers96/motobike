# Two-stroke is hard to start or weak at low revs (reed valve fault)

A reed valve lets fuel mixture into a two-stroke crankcase and stops it flowing back out of the carburettor when the piston goes down. If a reed is broken or stuck closed, the bike is very hard to start, or may not start at all. A reed that does not seal can also spit mixture back through the carburettor and leave the engine weak at low revs. Reed valves are normally reliable.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care, short distances only. A broken petal piece can enter the engine, so stop and have it checked if you find a missing piece.

## Quick checks first
- Is there fuel at the carburettor, and is the spark plug good?
- Does fuel mist or wetness show at the carburettor mouth after kicking?
- Did the problem start suddenly, perhaps with a bang, or build up slowly?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the engine have a spark, fuel and good compression?**
- Not sure: do the basic checks in [Engine turns over but will not start](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cranks-no-start) first.
- Yes to all: go to step 2.

**2. Is there fuel mist or wetness coming back out of the carburettor when you kick or crank?**
- Yes: the reed may not be sealing. Go to step 4.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Are there signs of a leak after the carburettor (cracked rubber, loose bolts)?**
- Yes: [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Remove the carburettor and look at the reeds.**
- Petals bent, cracked, chipped or stuck open or closed, or the stop plate loose: [Check a reed valve and its petals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/reed-valve-check).
- All fine: have the crankcase seals and compression checked: [Test compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Broken or chipped petal | Light shows through, missing piece | [Reed valve check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/reed-valve-check) |
| Petal stuck closed with gum or rust | Petal does not move or spring back | [Reed valve check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/reed-valve-check) |
| Loose or bent stop plate | Petal bends too far or flaps | [Reed valve check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/reed-valve-check) |
| Air leak at the intake | Idle changes with spray | [Intake leak test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
