# Stumbles or dies when the throttle is snapped open (CV carburettor)

The engine stumbles, coughs or nearly stalls when the throttle is opened quickly from idle. A CV (constant velocity) carburettor should not do this. Its slide is lifted by engine suction, not by the cable, so a fast throttle gives a smooth rise in power. When it does stumble, the slide is slow to lift, sticks, or the suction is leaking away.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care. Open the throttle gently when pulling out into traffic until it is fixed.

## Quick checks first
- Is the engine warm and the choke fully off?
- Is the air filter clean?
- Does the throttle cable move freely, with a small amount of free play?
- Does the bike stand for long periods with fuel in the carburettor?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the engine only stumble when the throttle is snapped open, but run well when opened slowly?**
- Yes: go to step 2.
- No, it also stumbles at steady throttle: see [Flat spot](/moto-zero/repair/problems/flat-spot) or [Hesitates when opening the throttle](/moto-zero/repair/problems/hesitation).

**2. Is the throttle cable stiff or are the free play and the plate action wrong?**
- Yes: [Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does the idle change when you spray water or carburettor cleaner on the intake rubber joints?**
- Yes: [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. With the air filter off, does the slide lift smoothly with a finger and fall when released?**
- Stiff or slow to rise: [Check a CV carburettor slide and diaphragm](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cv-carb-check).
- Smooth: go to step 5.

**5. Is the diaphragm torn, pinholed or hard, or are the vacuum or air holes blocked?**
- Yes: replace the diaphragm and clean the holes. See [Check a CV carburettor slide and diaphragm](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cv-carb-check).
- No: [Clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean).

On a multi-carburettor engine, uneven adjustment between carburettors can also give a stumble. A correctly adjusted set rises smoothly from idle to full throttle however hard the throttle is opened.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Sticking slide | Slide does not drop or rise freely by hand | [CV carburettor check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cv-carb-check) |
| Torn or hard diaphragm | Pinholes seen against light | [CV carburettor check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cv-carb-check) |
| Blocked vacuum or air holes | No flow when blown through | [Clean the carburettor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/carburettor-clean) |
| Intake air leak | Idle changes with spray | [Intake leak test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
| Carburettors out of balance (multi) | Rough rise, uneven pull | Workshop balance of the set |
