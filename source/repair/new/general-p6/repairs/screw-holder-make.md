# Hold a screw in a hard-to-reach place

Starting a screw in a tight spot is hard if it falls out of the screwdriver. A holder keeps it in place.

## Options
- **Bought screw holder.** A sleeve or clip that slides on the screwdriver blade and grips the head.
- **Paperclip holder.** Straighten a paperclip, bend one end into a loop or a V and slide it along the screwdriver blade so that it holds the screw head against the bit.
- **A rounded head.** If a bit slips, put a little valve-grinding paste in the head. Make your own with coarse grinding powder in oil. The paste lets the bit bite. See [Remove a rounded, seized or broken fastener](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stuck-fastener-removal).

## Steps
1. Choose a bit that fits the screw head: [Choose the right Phillips bit for Japanese bike screws](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/jis-screwdriver-fit).
2. Slide the holder over the blade and set the screw on the tip.
3. Place the screw in its hole and turn it a few turns.
4. Slide the holder away and finish with the screwdriver.
5. Pull the holder out of the way before tightening.

## Check your work
- The screw is started square and not cross-threaded: it turns freely for the first turns by hand.
