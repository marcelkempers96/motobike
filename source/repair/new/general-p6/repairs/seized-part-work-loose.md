# Free a rusted or glued part by working it back and forth

Cylinders, heads, shafts and axles can be rusted on, or stuck with old gasket cement. Force breaks parts. Oil, small movements and time free them.

## Safety
- Support the bike and the part. A freed part can fall.
- Wear eye protection.

## You need
- Penetrating oil, a dead-blow hammer (a hammer filled with shot) or a rawhide or plastic mallet, and patience.

## Steps
1. Spray penetrating oil all round the joint. Let it soak. It gives best results after a few hours, and it may need renewing after a day or two.
2. Move the part a little in one direction with a gentle blow or a twist.
3. Spray again. Move it back the other way.
4. Repeat. Each cycle should gain a little. Go gently, go slowly, but keep going.
5. When the part moves freely, lift it off. Keep it square so it does not bind.
6. Clean the faces and coat any shaft with oil or anti-seize before refitting. Replace the oil that the penetrating spray washed away: it does not last.

## Tips
- Never lever on cylinder cooling fins or thin aluminium edges: see [Tap a stuck cylinder loose without breaking the fins](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cylinder-fin-safe-hit).
- Never use a hard steel hammer directly on a part. Use a soft-faced hammer or put a block of wood or brass between.
- Mild household acids such as vinegar or lemon juice can also dissolve rust, but they need time.

## Check your work
- The part moved freely and was not damaged.

## If it did not fix the problem
- A stuck axle: [Drive a stuck axle out with a nut and a brass rod](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/seized-axle-drive-out).
- A stuck fastener: [Remove a rounded, seized or broken fastener](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/stuck-fastener-removal).
