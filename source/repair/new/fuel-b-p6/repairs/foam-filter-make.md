# Make a replacement foam air filter from foam sheet

A bike must never run without an air filter, and a filter for an old model can be hard to find. A copy of the original can be cut from foam made for air filters and glued together. This is a stop-gap or a low-cost option, not a perfect match for the original.

## Before you start
- Use foam sold as air filter foam, in sheets. Other foam may break up or let dirt through.
- Keep the old filter or its frame. It is the pattern.
- Read [Clean and re-oil a foam air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-foam-service) first, because the new element needs the same oil.

## Steps
1. Lay the old filter or frame on the foam and trace each piece that is needed.
2. Cut the pieces out with a sharp knife or scissors. Keep the edges clean and square.
3. Join the pieces with hot glue so that there are no gaps. Gaps let dirt past the filter.
4. Let the glue cool, then work foam filter oil into the foam until it is evenly wet. Squeeze out the extra.
5. Fit the element in the airbox or on the carburettor. It must seal around its edges and its frame.
6. Check the mixture. A change of filter type can make the bike run rich or lean, so follow [Check the mixture after changing the air filter or airbox](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/airbox-change-check).

## Check your work
- No light shows through a joint when the element is held up.
- The engine idles and picks up as before, and the plug colour is normal.

## If it did not fix the problem
- [Runs rich or lean after an air filter or airbox change](/moto-zero/repair/problems/air-change-runs-wrong).
- [Clean or replace the air filter](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/air-filter-service).
