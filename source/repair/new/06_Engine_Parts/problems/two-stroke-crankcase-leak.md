# Two-stroke is hard to start, runs lean or smokes (crankcase leak suspected)

A two-stroke needs a sealed crankcase to pull in mixture and push it up to the cylinder. If a crankshaft seal or a case joint leaks, air is drawn in and the mixture goes weak, or gearbox oil gets in. The signs are hard starting, a poor idle, a weak and hot engine, or heavy smoke.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care and only short distances. A lean engine can overheat and seize the piston quickly. Stop if the engine loses power and gets hot.

## Quick checks first
- Does it start easily when warm but not after standing overnight? Old seals can harden when cold and seal again when warm.
- Is there heavy smoke with a gearbox oil smell, and does the gearbox oil level rise?
- Are the carburettor mounting and inlet rubbers tight and undamaged?
- Is the spark plug light grey or white, a sign of lean running?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the carburettor or inlet joint sealed?**
- Check for cracks and loose clamps. [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test).
- Sound: go to step 2.

**2. Is the compression normal?**
- No: the fault is in the piston, rings or cylinder. [Test engine compression](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test).
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Is there oil on the outside of the case near the crankshaft ends?**
- Yes: a seal may be weeping. Fit new seals: [Remove and fit an engine oil seal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-seal-fit).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does a pressure and vacuum test hold?**
- No: find the leak with soapy water and cure it. [Pressure and vacuum test a two-stroke crankcase](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-crankcase-test).
- Yes: the crankcase is sealed. Look at the fuel and ignition instead: [Hard to start when the engine is cold](/moto-zero/repair/problems/hard-cold-start).

If the engine overheats or smells of burnt oil, stop and let it cool. A seized piston is the usual result.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Inlet or carburettor joint leak | Air leak found around the inlet | [Find an intake air leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/intake-leak-test) |
| Worn generator-side crankshaft seal | Pressure test drops; engine runs weak and lean | [Fit new seals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-seal-fit) |
| Worn clutch-side seal | Gearbox oil in the engine; smoke | [Fit new seals](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-seal-fit) |
| Case joint or base gasket leak | Soap bubbles at the joint | Gasket or sealant as the manual calls for |
| Cracked case | Visible crack or hole | Replace the case |
