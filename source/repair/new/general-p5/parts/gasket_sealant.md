# Gasket sealant and liquid gasket

Gasket sealant is a paste or spray used on engine joints. A liquid gasket replaces a paper gasket on faces made to be sealed without one, such as crankcase halves. A non-hardening cement helps a good old gasket seal. Gasket remover softens a stuck gasket so it scrapes off.

## What it does
It fills small gaps and gouges in the joint faces and stops oil from seeping out.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Oil seeping along a cover or crankcase joint: [Oil leak trace](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-leak-trace).
- A hard, cracked or squashed gasket.

## When to replace it
Replace the gasket if it is damaged. Sealant is a help for a gasket that is old and hard but still whole. It should not replace a gasket that should be renewed.

## What to match when buying
- Use the type the manual names for the joint: liquid gasket where no gasket is fitted, non-hardening cement for paper gaskets.
- Check it is oil-resistant and suitable for the temperature.
- Gasket remover comes as a spray or a brush-on liquid. The spray works better on old gaskets.

## Fitting notes
- Clean and degrease both faces. Remove all old gasket and sealant.
- Apply a thin even coat only. Too much squeezes into oil passages.
- To remove a stuck gasket, spray remover, wait an hour or two, then scrape. For very hard gaskets spray again and leave overnight.
- See [Replace an engine cover gasket](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cover-gasket-replace).
