# Identify bolt strength, thread and locking types before you reuse them

A bolt works like a spring. As it tightens it stretches, and the stretch is what holds the parts together. Different bolts are made of different steel, and the right one matters. A weak bolt on the cylinder head or cam sprocket can destroy an engine. A fender bolt does not need that strength.

## Safety
- Never put a weaker bolt where a stronger one is called for.
- Do not reuse a bolt that stretched or gave way during tightening. If you feel a bolt yield, replace it.

## Steps
1. Keep every bolt, nut and washer from the job in a labelled tray. Replacing a full set can cost more than the job.
2. Read the head. Marks show the strength grade. Many bolts have no mark, or only a maker's mark, so keep bolts that sit in different places apart.
3. Match the thread. A thread screwed into a casting is normally coarse pitch. A bolt or stud that takes a nut is normally fine pitch. Measure with a thread gauge and check the manual.
4. Look at the shaft. A waisted bolt (a thinner section in the middle) is made for high stress, for example the cylinder head. Replace like with like.
5. Check for left-hand threads. If a nut will not turn the usual way and is not rusted, try the other way. The manual lists them.
6. Look at the locking method. Lock washers can be spring, wave, toothed or tabbed. Nylon-collar and metal-collar locknuts can usually be reused several times. Tab washers and key nuts must be renewed after they are bent and removed. Some joints use thread locker or safety wire.
7. Rolled threads are stronger than cut threads. Do not cut extra thread on a bolt unless you must.
8. Tighten to the torque in your manual with a torque wrench: [Use a torque wrench](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/torque-wrench-use).

## Thread standards
- Metric (ISO, JIS) and American unified threads use a 60 degree thread. Older British Whitworth, BSF and BA threads have a different angle and rounded tops. They do not mix, even when the size looks the same.
- Before buying a bolt, tap or thread insert, measure the size and pitch: [Identify a thread standard and pitch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/thread-standard-identify).
- A pulled-out thread in a casting: [A bolt hole thread is stripped](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bolt-hole-stripped).

## Check your work
- The bolt is the right size, grade and thread, and has its washer or lock.
- It stays tight after the first ride and a recheck.
