# Choose the right workshop chemicals and sprays

A small shelf of the right chemicals makes many jobs quicker. Any good brand will do. Read the label and check that it suits the part.

## Safety
- Most are flammable or irritants. Use with good airflow, away from flames, with eye protection and gloves.
- Starting fluid: a very small amount only. Do not keep pouring it in. If the engine does not start, find out why.

## Cleaning and loosening
- **Penetrating oil:** spray on rusted parts, wait, then work the part back and forth. It evaporates in a day or two, so oil the part afterwards. Vinegar and lemon juice can also dissolve rust.
- **Light general oil:** apply after penetrating oil. It stays where you put it.
- **Contact cleaner:** removes corrosion and moisture from electrical contacts and leaves no residue. Spray it, work the switch, spray again.
- **Solvent or acetone:** acetone leaves metal free of grease. See [Clean engine parts safely with solvent](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/parts-solvent-clean).
- **Brake cleaner:** removes oil from brake pads and shoes.
- **Carburettor cleaner:** spray into a glass jar to soak small parts. Spray through jets and passages with eye protection. If you see the spray come out the other side, the passage is clear.
- **Engine degreaser:** makes oily dirt wash off with water.

## Lubricants and sealers
- **Chain oil:** use one made for sealed (O-ring) chains.
- **Graphite lubricant:** flows into old cables with cracked outers, then the carrier dries, leaving the graphite.
- **Anti-seize:** put on exposed shafts, wheel nuts and spark plug threads. It makes later removal easy.
- **Thread locker:** use the standard type on bolts that loosen.
- **Gasket remover:** spray, wait an hour or two, then scrape. For bad gaskets wait overnight.
- **Non-hardening gasket cement and liquid gasket:** for gasketless faces, or to help a good old gasket seal. A new gasket is better.

## Other
- **Hand cleaner:** mixes with oil so it washes off with water. In an emergency rub liquid soap on dry hands first.

## Check your work
- Each chemical is labelled, closed, and stored away from heat.
