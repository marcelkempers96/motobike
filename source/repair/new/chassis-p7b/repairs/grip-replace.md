# Remove and fit handlebar grips

Grips wear, tear and go slippery. The left grip sits on the bar. The right grip sits on the plastic or metal throttle tube, which must still turn freely after the new grip is fitted.

## Safety
- A loose grip can twist in your hand. Do not ride until it is firm.
- On the right side, check the throttle snaps shut after the job.
- Remove bar-end weights first if fitted, and refit them tight.

## You need
- New grips of the right inside size: the left grip fits the bar, the right grip fits the throttle tube.

## Steps: remove
1. Remove the bar-end weight or end cap if fitted.
2. Slide a thin screwdriver between the grip and the bar. Squirt penetrating oil (or soapy water) along the blade.
3. Pull the screwdriver out and twist the grip back and forth until it frees, then slide it off. This saves the grip only some of the time.
4. If it will not move, cut it off along its length with a knife. Do not cut into a plastic throttle tube.
5. Clean off old glue and oil. The bar and tube must be clean and dry.

## Steps: fit
1. Choose glue or tape. Glued grips do not move, but must be cut off next time. A grip fitted over masking tape can be removed again without damage.
2. Tape method: wrap the bar (or throttle tube) with masking tape along the length of the grip. Try the grip. If it feels loose, add one more layer. It should be hard to push on.
3. Glue method: use grip glue and follow its instructions. Keep glue off the part of the throttle tube inside the housing.
4. Push the grip fully home. On the right side leave a small gap to the switch housing and the bar end so the tube turns freely.
5. Refit the bar-end weight.

## Check your work
- The grip does not turn on the bar or the tube when twisted hard.
- The throttle snaps shut from full open with the bars straight and at each lock.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Throttle tube rubs or sticks: [Strip, clean and lubricate a twist grip](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-grip-strip).
