# Solder a new end (nipple) onto a control cable

Factory cable ends are steel, pressed (swaged) onto the wire by a machine. At home the end is soldered on instead. Repair ends are brass and take solder well. A soldered end is a get-home or custom repair. For a throttle or front brake cable, a new factory cable is the safer choice.

## Safety
- A throttle or brake cable whose end pulls off can cause a crash. Test the joint hard before you ride.
- Hot solder and flux spit. Wear eye protection and work on a clear bench.

## You need
- A brass end that matches the old one in shape and in hole size for the wire.
- Flux, solder and enough heat to melt the solder through the whole end.

## Steps
1. Heat the solder pot (or iron) fully before you start.
2. Compare the old cable with the new end. Check the end will fit the lever or grip in the same way.
3. Measure the exposed inner wire on the old cable: the distance from the end of the outer to the end of the fitting. Write it down.
4. Cut the frayed end off the wire cleanly. Slide the new end on.
5. Cut the wire so a very short tail sticks out past the end. The cable hints sheet gives about 1/16 inch (about 1.5 mm).
6. Tap the tail with a small hammer so it spreads (peen it) over the end of the fitting. This locks the wire and is the key to a strong joint.
7. Dip the fitting in flux until it is covered.
8. Dip it in the solder for about 10 seconds so the heat goes right through. Dip only just past the fitting: solder that runs up the wire makes it stiff and it breaks where it should flex.
9. Let it cool without moving it. Wipe off the flux.

## Check your work
- Pull hard on the end with pliers while holding the wire in a vice. It must not move.
- The wire just above the end still bends freely.
- Fit the cable and set free play: [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) or [Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust).

## If it did not fix the problem
- Outer cracked or inner stiff all along: fit a new cable ([Replace the clutch cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-replace), [Lubricate or replace the throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-service)).
