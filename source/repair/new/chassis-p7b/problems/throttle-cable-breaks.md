# Throttle cable keeps fraying or breaking at the grip end

Most throttle cables fail at the twist grip end. Each time the grip turns, the wire bends where it leaves the grip, and broken strands start there. A cable that breaks again soon after replacement points to a cause at the grip: a sharp bend, a dry or dirty grip, or a cable that is too tight.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. A frayed throttle cable can catch and hold the throttle open, or snap and leave you with no power. Replace it before riding.

## Quick checks first
- Open the grip housing and look at the cable end for broken strands.
- Does the cable leave the housing in a gentle curve or a sharp bend?
- Turn the bars lock to lock: does the cable pull tight or kink at either side?
- Is it the right cable for the bike (length, ends, single or twin)?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Are there broken strands near the cable end at the grip?**
- Yes: replace the cable ([Lubricate or replace the throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-service), or [Replace a twin throttle cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-twin-cable-replace)). Then go to step 2 to find why.
- No: check the engine end and the outer for cracks ([A control cable is frayed or stiff](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cable-frayed)).

**2. Does the cable enter the grip housing at a sharp angle, or has the housing been turned on the bar?**
- Yes: set the housing to its original position (many have a locating pin or a mark). The cable must enter in a gentle curve.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the grip stiff, dirty or dry with the cable unhooked?**
- Yes: [Strip, clean and lubricate the twist grip](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-grip-strip). A stiff grip makes the wire work harder at the bend.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does the cable pull tight or kink when the bars turn, or is the free play almost zero?**
- Yes: reroute it in its guides and set free play ([Adjust throttle free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust)).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Is the outer cracked, or is the cable a cheap or wrong-length copy?**
- Yes: fit a correct cable and oil it at each service ([Lubricate control cables](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cable-lube)).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dry cable end at the grip | Broken strands, no oil at the end | [Replace](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-service) and lubricate |
| Sharp bend at the grip | Housing turned, cable kinked at entry | Reposition the housing, gentle curve |
| Stiff or dirty grip | Grip hard to turn with cable off | [Strip the grip](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-grip-strip) |
| Cable too tight or misrouted | Idle rises at full lock | [Adjust free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/throttle-cable-adjust) |
| Cracked outer | Water and dirt inside | Replace the cable |
