## Doubts
- Some photos are exploded diagrams (fork, swingarm, brake line, rear drum brake); they are used only where a diagram of the
  part is the best available picture (fork_springs, swingarm, brake_hose, brake_cable).
- 144_speedo_drive_outside_hub and 138_if_these_things_happen_have_corrosion_2 were unused photos; content judged from
  the contact sheet and the lesson caption. 146_changing_tire and 146_tires_tubes show a bare rim with tyre half off.
- fork_springs mentions "a broken spring rattles" as a sign; this is general knowledge, not from the course.
- The course says never patch a motorcycle tube; the site's tube-puncture-repair page patches. I did not add the course
  view, and left the existing "new tube is safer" wording.
- The course gives a dealer's hot-pressure rule and some numbers (bead seating pressure, chain play); not used, no numbers added.
- The lesson 150 url (steering_bearings.htm) is cited for photos only.
