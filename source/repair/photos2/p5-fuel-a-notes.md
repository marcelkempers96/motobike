Conflicts and unverified values
- Lessons give the idle air screw start as 1.25 turns out (064) and 1.5 turns out (067). Pages say
  "use the manual value; many carburettors start near 1 to 1.5 turns out".
- Lesson 064 says wire may clear a jet; part page md/parts/carb.md says never clear jets with wire.
  carb-jet-clear allows one soft strand as a last step and warns it enlarges the jet.
- Throttle range overlaps (idle 0 to 25 percent, slide to 35, needle 15 to 80, main 60 to 100) are
  approximate and given as "about" in jetting-after-modification.
- Alcohol shake test uses ratios only (water about a quarter of fill height; rise about the share of
  the fuel layer). Lesson says approximate.
- Plug test in main-jet-change (full throttle run, cut engine, coast) is standard practice but is not
  in the lessons; the lessons only say run about a mile and read the plug.
