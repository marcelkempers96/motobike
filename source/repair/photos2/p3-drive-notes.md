  cvt_clutch, cvt_belt, cvt-clutch-check, cvt-belt-replace and system cvt have no new photo.
- No photo for neutral-light-fault, cvt-gear-oil-leak, clutch-bleed, scooter-gear-oil (course has none).

  where it matters. 148_bob_lane_sent_me_picture_england (destroyed sprocket) is used for chain-jumps, chain-snapped, sprocket-worn.

  rattle at idle as a sign); no numbers were added. The 10 to 15 minute chain lube settle time was left out of page text.
- jumps-out-of-gear, stuck-in-gear, false-neutral, hard-to-find-neutral, heavy-clutch already covered the course points.
