## Doubts
- Some photos are reused on several pages because the course has few electrical images (circuit tester light,
  multimeter, solenoid, regulator test). Captions say what is shown, but fit is loose on a few lighting pages.
- Several problem photos are generic test photos (circuit tester) used for "the check", not the fault.
- 099_voltage_regulators.jpg and 099_starter_wiring style images are old drawings of mechanical regulators; kept
  only voltage_regulators on regulator and overcharging pages.
- The course gives a spark gap and condenser values for its own bikes; none were copied. Pages say use the manual.
- Not all pass 2 repair pages were checked for photo counts beyond the catalogue.
