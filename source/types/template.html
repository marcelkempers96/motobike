Motorcycle types

From Daimler's wooden Reitwagen of 1885 to the battery-swapping e-boda, motorcycles have been reshaped again and again by roads, money, sport and fuel. Sixteen types, each with a photograph, a labelled plate of the parts that define it and the story of how it came to be. Pick one to open it.
