# Brief: 20-point SEO pass over the whole site

Repo: /home/user/motobike (static site on Vercel; canonical domain for now
`https://motobike-five.vercel.app`, the real domain www.motopedia.org is coming: keep the base URL
in ONE place per builder so it can be switched later). Brand: Motopedia.
Generated pages come from builders, so fix the builder, then rebuild:
`source/repair/build_repair.py`, `source/parts/build_parts.py`, `source/videos/build_videos.py`,
`source/articles/build.py` (cd into source/articles to run). Hand-made pages: the top-level folders
(index.html, overview/, learn/, about/, ...). Course and app pages render in JS (`assets/learn.js`,
`assets/app.js`).

Check and fix each, measure before and after with a script, and write the result to
`source/seo/seo-report.json` (shape below) and `source/seo/seo-report.md` (at most 60 lines):
1 sitemap.xml complete and only real, indexable pages; 2 robots.txt (allow all, sitemap line, block
nothing needed); 3 no stray noindex (only redirect stubs and private pages like account/progress may
have it); 4 canonical on every indexable page, matching its own URL; 5 unique meta title 30 to 70
chars; 6 unique meta description 70 to 165 chars; 7 exactly one H1 per page; 8 heading order (no
H3 before the first H2, no skipped levels in hand-made pages and builder templates); 9 alt text on
every content image (decorative icons may have alt=""; photos and renders need real alt); 10 schema
markup: Organization + WebSite (with SearchAction if a search exists) on the home page, BreadcrumbList,
Article on articles, HowTo or TechArticle on repair pages, Course on learn/, FAQPage where there is a
FAQ, VideoObject on the videos page; validate JSON; 11 internal links: every indexable page reachable
from the nav, the sitemap and at least one other page; add related links where pages are orphaned;
12 fix broken internal links and images (relative paths resolving to nothing); 13 compress images:
report the 30 largest images in assets/ that pages use, convert oversized JPG/PNG photos over 400 KB
to WebP or recompress at max 1600 px (keep the file name and format if many pages reference it;
never touch source/); add `loading="lazy"` and width/height where missing on content images;
14 Core Web Vitals: preconnect/preload the main font, `defer` or `type=module` on scripts, no
render-blocking extras; note what cannot be measured here; 15 mobile layout: check 10 key pages at
390 px with Playwright for horizontal overflow; 16 HTTPS: Vercel serves HTTPS; make sure no http://
asset or link to our own site remains; add a `Strict-Transport-Security` header in vercel.json;
17 URL slugs: report any non-lowercase or underscore slugs (repair part ids use underscores: do NOT
rename, just report); 18 og:image (and twitter card) on every indexable page; 19 Search Console:
prepare `google-site-verification` placeholder support: add a `<meta name="google-site-verification">`
to index.html ONLY if a value is given (leave a comment in the report on how the owner adds it; the
file method `googleXXXX.html` also works); 20 add `/llms.txt` (and `/llms-full.txt` short version is
optional): what Motopedia is, sections with links (course modules, repair, parts, tools, articles,
videos, downloads), licence CC BY-SA 4.0, contact page.

`source/seo/seo-report.json`: `{"date": "...", "checks": [{"n": 1, "name": "...", "status": "ok" |
"fixed" | "todo", "detail": "one line", "owner_action": "what the site owner must do, or empty"}],
"todo": ["short imperative items for the owner, most important first"]}`. This file is shown on the
admin dashboard, so write owner actions in plain words.

Do not edit `account/index.html`, `assets/community-config.js` or `source/promo/` (other work in
progress). Do not change wording of course content. Run all builders at the end and re-run your
checks. Do not commit. Tool-call budget: 180. Reply in at most 10 lines with before/after numbers.
