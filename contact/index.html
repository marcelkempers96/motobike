  • CorrectionsA value, date or explanation that is wrong or unclear. Please name the chapter.
  • TranslationsYou would like to translate a module or the site into another language.
  • Teaching adaptationsYou are adapting the material for a class, workshop or training centre.
  • Collaborations and partnershipsNew models, joint courses or projects that help spread technical education on motorcycles, combustion and electric.
