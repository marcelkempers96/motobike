Motorcycle types through the ages

From Daimler's wooden Reitwagen of 1885 to the battery-swapping e-boda, motorcycles have been reshaped again and again by roads, money, sport and fuel. Each of the sixteen types has a labelled plate of the parts that define it, a photograph, its typical figures and the story of how it came to be.

About this page

Figures are typical ranges for each type, not the specification of any one model. The order follows the story rather than strict dates, and each entry ends with what came next.
