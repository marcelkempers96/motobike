Things to try
  • Turn on the headlight, then make the earth corroded: watch the voltage at the lamp fall and the light dim, the most common electrical fault on old bikes.
  • Add the auxiliary lights and heated grips on a 10 A fuse: the fuse blows. Pick the fuse about 25 % above the normal current.
  • Run everything on 0.5 mm² wire over 4 m: the wire runs hot and the lamps dim. Thicker or shorter wire fixes both.
  • Switch between a charged battery and a running engine: lights are brighter at 14.2 V, because power rises with the square of the voltage.
