Cookies and local storage

Moto Zero sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your progress and preferences on your device, and one cookie only if you choose to translate a page.

01In short

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or social media cookies.
  • One small cookie, googtrans, is set only if you choose a language other than English, so the translation follows you from page to page.
  • Everything else is kept in your browser's local storage, only to remember your progress and settings, and is not sent to us. If you sign in, local storage also keeps you signed in.

03Local storage

Local storage is a small store inside your browser that a website can read and write on your device. It is not sent with requests to the server. The site uses these keys:

mz-progress-v1Learn, Quiz, My progress and the toolsChapters you opened and marked as done (with dates), your answers to in-chapter checks, best and last quiz scores per module and level, and which tools you used.
mz-rep-…Repair job pagesWhich steps of a job you ticked as done (one key per job).

sb-…-auth-tokenAccount (only if you sign in)Keeps you signed in (a session token from Supabase; the middle part is our project name). Removed when you sign out.
mz-roleAccount page (only if you sign in)Whether the account is a member or team account, to show the right menu. Removed when you sign out.

The site also uses one session storage key, mz-next-done, during sign-in to bring you back to the page you came from; the browser deletes it when you close the tab.

If you are signed in, your course progress is also saved to your account, as described in the privacy policy.

04Why there is no cookie banner

Dutch and EU law (article 11.7a of the Telecommunicatiewet, based on the ePrivacy directive) requires consent for cookies and similar storage unless they are strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Everything above only remembers what you did on the site or keeps you signed in, so that the site can show your progress and settings back to you. Translation and video players load only when you ask for them. Nothing is used for tracking, analytics or advertising, so there is nothing to consent to and no banner.

05How to clear it

  • Use Reset progress on the My progress page to clear your learning progress. Before clearing, you can use Export progress to keep a copy and import it later or on another device.
  • Choose English in the language picker to remove the googtrans cookie.
  • Sign out on the account page to remove the sign-in token.
  • To remove every key, clear the site data for this website in your browser settings.
