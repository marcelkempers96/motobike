Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. It uses local storage in your browser to remember your progress and a few preferences, and that information never leaves your device.

On this page
  1. No cookies
  2. What local storage is used for
  3. Why there is no cookie banner
  4. How to clear it
  5. Questions

01No cookies

Moto Zero does not set any cookies, first-party or third-party. There are no analytics, advertising or social media cookies.

02What local storage is used for

Local storage is a small store inside your browser that a website can read and write on your device. The site uses these keys:

KeySet byWhat it holds
mz-progress-v1Learn, Quiz and the toolsChapters you opened and marked as done (with dates), your answers to in-chapter checks, best and last quiz scores per module and level, and which tools you used.
mz-bestQuizYour best score per quiz topic.
mz-soundCockpitWhether the engine sound is on or off.
mz-buildBike builderYour fastest time for each bike and level.

The data is plain text that only pages from this site, in this browser, can read. It is not sent with requests to the server.

03Why there is no cookie banner

Consent banners are needed for storage that tracks people or is not necessary for a service the visitor asked for. The keys above only remember what you did on the site so that it can show your progress and settings back to you, and nothing is shared. There is nothing to opt out of, so there is no banner.

04How to clear it

  • Use Reset progress on the My progress page to clear your learning progress.
  • To remove every key, clear the site data for this website in your browser settings.
  • Before clearing, you can use Export progress to keep a copy and import it later or on another device.
