Privacy policy

Moto Zero collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. What the site remembers about your learning stays in your own browser.

01In short

  • No accounts, no login, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising.
  • Your progress and preferences are stored only in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • A small number of third parties are involved in delivering the site: Google Fonts and the host, Vercel.

02Who is responsible

The site is published by Transitions Lab as part of the Moto Zero open educational programme. For any question about privacy, write to marcel@transitionslab.org.

03What is stored in your browser

To remember your progress without an account, the site uses your browser’s local storage. This data is created and read by the pages on your own device. It is never sent to Transitions Lab or anyone else.

04Third parties

  • Google Fonts. The typefaces load from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address and standard request information, as with any web request.
  • Hosting by Vercel. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web server, it keeps technical logs of requests (such as IP address, time, page requested and browser type) for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.
  • External links. Some links lead to other sites, such as Sketchfab for the 3D models and transitionslab.org. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow the link.

05If you email us

The contact page opens your own email app; the site itself sends and stores nothing. When you email us, Transitions Lab uses your name, address and message only to answer you, and does not share them or add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete the correspondence at any time.

06Deleting your data

To clear your learning progress, use Reset progress on the My progress page. To remove everything the site has stored, clear the site data for this website in your browser settings (often under Privacy, Cookies and site data). Private or incognito windows delete it automatically when closed.

07Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you may ask for a copy of that correspondence or ask us to delete it.

08Changes to this policy

If the site starts to handle data differently, this page will be updated first and the change noted on the Updates page.
