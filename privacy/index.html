Privacy policy

Motopedia has no advertising and no tracking. To see how many people visit, we may count page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, does not identify you and keeps no personal data. You can use every page without an account, and then what the site remembers about your learning stays in your own browser. If you choose to create a free account, sign up for updates or write to us, we store only what is needed for that, as described below.

01In short

  • You can use every page without an account. Then your learning progress stays in your own browser and is never sent to us.
  • No tracking cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, advertising or selling of data. Ever. Visit counting, if switched on, is anonymous and cookieless (Vercel Web Analytics).
  • If you choose to create a free account, sign up for updates, send a message or a brand request, we store only what that needs, for as long as it needs, with the providers listed below.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion at any time, and you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority.

02Who is responsible

The controller for personal data on this site is Transitions Lab, the Netherlands-based publisher of the Motopedia (Motopedia) open educational programme. For anything about privacy or your data, email the team at marcel@transitionslab.org or use the contact form. The postal address is available on request; see the legal notice.

Transitions Lab is a small team and has not appointed a data protection officer; the team answers privacy questions itself.

03Using the site without an account

Reading pages, taking quizzes, using the tools and tracking your progress need no account. What the site remembers about your learning (chapters done, quiz scores, circuits you built, preferences) is kept in your browser's local storage on your device and is not sent to us. The full list is on Cookies and storage.

Like any website, the pages, fonts and some pictures are delivered by servers that see your IP address and basic browser information when you load them. Those are listed under Who processes data for us.

05What other people can see

  • Public: your chosen name, your comments and the photos you post, on the page where you post them. Photos and profile pictures are stored in a public folder: anyone with the link can open them.
  • Not public: your email address, your progress, your messages with the team, your country and the text about you, sign-ups and brand requests. Only you and the Transitions Lab team (as administrators) can see them.
  • Photos are resized in your browser before upload and saved as a new image, which removes location (GPS) and camera data. Please still check that a photo shows nothing private; see the community guidelines.

06Who processes data for us

These providers process data on our behalf (processors) or deliver part of the site:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA): database, sign-in emails and file storage for accounts, comments, photos, progress, messages, sign-ups and brand requests. Privacy policy.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., USA): hosting of the website, with technical request logs. Privacy policy.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): forwards the contact form, sign-up notices, member message notices and brand requests to the team by email. Privacy policy.
  • Our email provider, which stores the emails the team receives and sends.
  • jsDelivr (public content delivery network): delivers the Supabase program library to pages with comments or accounts, and so sees your IP address. Privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google): the typefaces load from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; Google receives your IP address and standard request information. Privacy policy.

Only when you choose them:

  • Google Translate: if you pick a language other than English, the page text and your IP address go to Google to be translated, and Google may set its own cookies. English pages use no translation service.
  • YouTube (Google): video cards show a preview picture from i.ytimg.com. The video loads from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you press play; from then on Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Listen: the Listen button uses your browser's own voices. Some browsers use online voices, which send the text (not your identity from us) to the browser maker.
  • Links to other sites, such as Sketchfab, shops, manufacturers and transitionslab.org, follow their own privacy policies once you open them. Shop links are plain search links: we receive no commission and no data from them.

07Transfers outside the EU

Supabase, Vercel, Google, FormSubmit and jsDelivr are based in, or may process data in, countries outside the European Economic Area, mainly the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under it) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider's data processing terms. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

09Children

Everyone can use the site without an account, including the Kids game. Accounts, comments and photo uploads are for people aged 16 or over. Younger learners may only create an account with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 16 created an account without consent, email the team at marcel@transitionslab.org and we will delete it.

10Security

All traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS). The database uses row-level security, so each member can only read and change their own private data, and only team accounts can moderate. Sign-in works with a one-time link sent to your email, so the site stores no passwords. No system is perfect: if we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will report it to the authority and tell you where the law requires it.

11Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access), and receive account data in a common format (portability);
  • have incorrect data corrected; you can change your name and profile yourself on the account page;
  • have your data deleted; you can delete your own comments at any time, and request deletion of your whole account from the account page or by email;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent (for example for update emails) at any time, without affecting what happened before.

To use any of these rights, email the team at marcel@transitionslab.org or use the contact form. We answer within one month and may ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to your data.

You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in the EU country where you live. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

12Clearing data in your browser

To clear your learning progress, use Reset progress on the My progress page. To remove everything the site has stored, clear the site data for this website in your browser settings (often under Privacy, Cookies and site data). Private or incognito windows delete it automatically when closed.

13Changes to this policy

If the site starts to handle data differently, this page is updated first and the change is noted on the Updates page. If a change needs your consent, we will ask for it.
