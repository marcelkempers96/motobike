# Rattling noise from the cam chain area

On an overhead cam (OHC) engine a chain drives the camshaft in the cylinder head. When the chain stretches or the tensioner stops pushing, the chain slaps against its guides. You hear a fast metallic rattle or slap from the cylinder, often loudest at idle or just after a cold start, and quieter at higher revs. A slack chain wears its guides and sprockets quickly and can skip a tooth, which lets the valves hit the piston. Note: pushrod engines such as the K-Light 125 have no cam chain; the camshaft sits next to the crankshaft. On those engines go to [Ticking noise from the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick).

## Is it safe to ride?
A light rattle at idle: ride gently to a workshop soon. A loud rattle, a rattle with loss of power, or grinding: stop and do not restart.

## Quick checks first
- Does your engine have a cam chain? Check the manual or parts diagram.
- Oil level and age: thin, old or low oil makes hydraulic tensioners noisy.
- Where is the noise? Cylinder side, head cover, or bottom of the engine?
- Did it start after a valve job or a new chain?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is your engine OHC with a cam chain?**
- No (pushrod, like the K-Light): go to [Ticking noise from the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick) or [Knocking from the cylinder when cold that fades when warm](/moto-zero/repair/problems/piston-slap).
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Is the oil level correct and the oil within its change interval?**
- No: correct it with [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) or [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service). If the rattle goes, keep the oil right.
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Listen with a long screwdriver or stethoscope on the cylinder near the tensioner, then near the valve caps. Is it a sharp tick at the caps?**
- Yes: valve clearance is more likely. Check it with [Check and adjust valve clearance](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-clearance).
- No, it is a rattle along the chain side: go to step 4.

**4. Does it rattle most at idle and soften when you raise the revs a little?**
- Yes: typical of cam chain slack. Go to step 5.
- No, it gets louder with revs and load, deep in the engine: go to [Deep knocking from the bottom of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/deep-knock).

**5. Does your engine have a manual or semi-automatic tensioner that can be adjusted?**
- Yes: adjust it following your manual only (there is no common method). If the rattle stays after adjusting, the chain is almost always worn out.
- No (automatic or hydraulic tensioner): go to step 6.

**6. Workshop check: [Check the cam chain and tensioner](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cam-chain-check). Is the tensioner at the end of its travel, or are guides broken?**
- Yes: replace chain, guides and tensioner as the workshop advises, and check valve timing.
- No: the noise may be from the cam, rockers or valve springs. Ask for [Assess the top end (cylinder, piston, valves)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment).

**7. At the next oil change, look for plastic pieces or metal in the oil with [Inspect drained oil for clues](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-inspect).** Pieces of guide in the oil mean the chain needs attention now, and the strainer must be cleaned.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low, old or wrong oil (hydraulic tensioner) | Dipstick, service record | [Change the engine oil and clean the strainer](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) |
| Tensioner stuck or at its limit | Plunger travel, rattle at idle | [Check the cam chain and tensioner](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cam-chain-check) |
| Stretched chain | Rattle stays after adjusting | [Check the cam chain and tensioner](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cam-chain-check) |
| Worn or broken guides | Plastic in oil | [Check the cam chain and tensioner](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cam-chain-check) |
| Valve clearance (not the chain) | Tick at valve caps | [Check and adjust valve clearance](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-clearance) |
