# Oil pump

The oil pump is a small pump inside the engine, usually behind the right side cover, driven by the crankshaft. Most commuter four-strokes use a trochoid pump: an inner rotor turning inside an outer rotor. (The K-Light manual calls it a "fuel pump" by a translation error.) Two-stroke engines use a different part: the [two-stroke oil pump](/moto-zero/repair/parts/two_stroke_oil_pump).

## What it does
The pump draws oil from the sump through the [strainer](/moto-zero/repair/parts/oil_filter) and pushes it through the filter to the crank big end, then up a passage to the rocker arms or camshaft. Other parts get oil by splash. The rotors are very close fitting. Grit in dirty oil wears them, and a blocked strainer starves the pump.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Oil pressure or oil level light on](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-warning-light) with the level correct.
- [Ticking from the top of the engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick) because oil is slow to reach the rockers.
- [Engine overheats](/moto-zero/repair/problems/overheating-air), then [knocks](/moto-zero/repair/problems/deep-knock) or [seizes](/moto-zero/repair/problems/engine-seized).
- Pump failure is rare; check oil level, oil grade and the strainer first.

## When to replace it
When rotor clearances are past the limit, the rotors are scored, or the drive gear is damaged. Follow your bike's manual.

## What to match when buying
- Match engine code. Pumps for 100 cc and 125 cc engines of one family can differ in rotor width.
- Check the drive gear teeth and shaft against the old pump.
- A good pump turns freely by hand with no tight spots and has a clean, flat body face.

## Fitting notes
- Workshop job. Keep every bit of grit out; flush and blow out the parts before fitting.
- Prime the pump with clean oil on assembly so it picks up at once.
- Fix the cause: clean the strainer and use the right oil at the right interval with [Change the engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service). See [Assess bottom end](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment).
