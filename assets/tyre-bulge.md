# Tyre has a bulge or cut

A bulge (a raised lump on the sidewall or tread) means the cords inside the tyre are broken, usually from hitting a pothole or kerb. Air pressure is pushing out through the weak spot. A cut that reaches the cords is just as weak. Either can burst without warning.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. Stop now. A bulged tyre can blow out, and a front blowout often causes a crash. Do not add more air. Push the bike or arrange transport.

## Quick checks first
- Stop safely and look at the whole tyre, both sides.
- Is there a lump, or a cut with threads visible?
- Look at the rim next to the bulge: dented or cracked?
- With a tube tyre, a lump can also be the tube pushing through a cut.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is there a raised lump that you can feel, on the sidewall or tread?**
- Yes: the tyre is finished. Do not ride. [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace). Go to step 4.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Is there a cut? Open it gently with a fingernail. Can you see cords or threads?**
- Yes: replace the tyre. Cuts to the cords cannot be plugged or patched safely.
- No, only a shallow surface cut in the tread: ride gently to have it checked, and check the tyre before each ride ([Inspect tyres for wear and damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-inspect)).

**3. Is the "bulge" really a wavy, uneven bead line next to the rim?**
- Yes: the tyre is not seated on the rim. Deflate and have it reseated ([Air leaking where the tyre meets the rim](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak)).
- No: treat it as a bulge (step 1).

**4. When the wheel is off, check the rim at the place of the impact. Dented, cracked or bent?**
- Yes: go to [Rim is dented or cracked](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rim-damage). A new tyre on a damaged rim can fail too.
- No: check runout before fitting the new tyre ([Check a wheel for runout](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-runout-check)).

**5. Did the impact also hit the fork or the steering?**
- Yes, or unsure: after the new tyre is fitted, check the bars are straight and the bike runs straight ([The bike has been dropped or crashed](/moto-zero/repair/problems/crash-check)).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pothole or kerb impact, broken cords | Lump on sidewall or tread | [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace) |
| Cut through to the cords | Threads visible in cut | [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace) |
| Riding under-inflated, sidewall damage | Low readings, bulge low on sidewall | Replace, keep pressure correct |
| Bead not seated | Uneven bead line, no lump | Reseat bead |
