# Hard to find neutral

On most geared motorcycles neutral sits halfway between first and second gear, so it needs a short, gentle lift of the lever. When it is hard to find, the usual cause is a clutch that does not fully release (drag), so the gears keep turning and the dogs will not line up. Other causes are a lever that does not move its full stroke, a neutral light that is not working (so you are in neutral but do not know it), and less often wear inside the gearbox.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes, but stop with care. If you cannot find neutral at a junction, hold the clutch lever in and the front brake on. Do not let the bike creep into traffic.

## Quick checks first
- Try finding neutral while the bike is still rolling slowly just before you stop. It is often easier.
- Use a short half-lift from first, not a full stroke.
- Turn the engine off and rock the bike back and forth while you lift the lever.
- Check the green N light works with the key on.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. With the engine off, rock the bike and lift the lever gently from first. Does neutral go in easily?**
- Yes: the gearbox is fine; with the engine running the clutch is dragging. Go to step 2.
- No, it is just as hard with the engine off: go to step 4.

**2. Is the clutch lever free play too large, or does the bike creep in gear with the lever pulled?**
- Yes: [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust), then see [Clutch drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-drag) if creeping continues.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the idle speed high?**
- Yes: [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle).
- No: the plates may still drag when warm; follow [Clutch drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-drag) to the end.

**4. Does the gear lever move its full stroke freely and spring back, without touching your boot, the footpeg or the case?**
- No: [Check the gear lever and shift linkage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/gearshift-check).
- Yes: go to step 5.

**5. Does the bike roll freely (it is in neutral) while the N light stays off?**
- Yes: the light or switch is the fault, not the gearbox. [Test the neutral switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/neutral-switch-test).
- No, it really will not go into neutral: go to step 6.

**6. Does neutral still skip or refuse after the clutch and lever are correct?**
- Yes: the stopper arm, its spring or the drum's star plate may be worn. Go to [Assess internal gearbox faults](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/gearbox-assessment).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Clutch drag | Neutral easy with engine off, hard with it running | [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) |
| Technique | Full stroke instead of a short half-lift | Practise a gentle half-lift, rolling slowly |
| Lever or linkage | Lever short of travel, touching, or stiff | [Check the gear lever and shift linkage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/gearshift-check) |
| Neutral light fault | Bike rolls in neutral but light off | [Test the neutral switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/neutral-switch-test) |
| Worn stopper arm or warped plates | Persists after all the above | [Assess internal gearbox faults](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/gearbox-assessment), [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service) |
