# Indicators

Indicators are the four amber lamps, two at the front and two at the back, that flash to show you are turning. Each has a lens, a bulb holder and a mount on a rubber or plastic stalk. They are switched from the left handlebar switch and timed by the [flasher relay](/moto-zero/repair/parts/flasher).

## What it does
When you move the switch, the flasher sends pulses to the front and rear lamp on that side. Each bulb needs a good earth back to the frame. Stalks break in small falls, holders rust, and a broken bulb or earth on one side usually makes the other bulb on that side flash fast or stay on.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Indicators do not work](/moto-zero/repair/problems/indicators-dead).
- [Indicators flash too fast](/moto-zero/repair/problems/indicators-fast): often one bulb blown.
- [Indicators stay on and do not flash](/moto-zero/repair/problems/indicators-steady).
- Broken stalk or lens, water inside.

## When to replace it
Replace the bulb if the filament is broken. Replace the whole indicator if the stalk, lens or holder is broken.

## What to match when buying
- Match bulb type and wattage, for example R10W (12 V 10 W) on the K-Light. A different wattage changes the flash rate with a thermal flasher.
- LED indicators draw much less current; they need an LED-rated flasher or resistors: [Fit LED indicators correctly](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/led-indicator-fit).
- Look for an approval mark on the lens. Amber light is required in most countries.
- Match the mounting thread and stalk length.

## Fitting notes
- Rider job: [Replace a bulb](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/bulb-replace).
- Clean the earth path in the holder, and seal the lens.
- If the fault is on both sides, test the flasher and switch: [Test the indicator flasher relay](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/flasher-test).
