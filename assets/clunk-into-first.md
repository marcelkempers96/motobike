# Loud clunk when selecting first gear

When you select first at a standstill with the engine running, the bike gives a clunk and a small jolt. Some clunk is normal on most motorcycles, especially when cold: the clutch plates still turn the gearbox a little, and the gear engages against that movement. A very loud bang, a lurch forward, or a stall means the clutch is not releasing well, the idle is high, or there is slack somewhere in the drive.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes, usually. If the bike lurches forward when you select first, hold the front brake while you do it until it is fixed.

## Quick checks first
- Is it only loud for the first few minutes on a cold morning?
- Pull the clutch lever, wait two or three seconds, then select first. Is it quieter?
- Does the bike creep with the clutch pulled?
- Is the idle faster than usual?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the clunk get much quieter once the engine is warm, or when you wait a moment after pulling the lever?**
- Yes: this is mostly normal. Cold oil holds the plates together. Use the grade in your manual and wait a moment before selecting first.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Does the bike creep or try to stall when you select first with the lever pulled?**
- Yes: the clutch is dragging. [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) (K-Light 125: 10 to 15 mm); hydraulic clutch: [Bleed a hydraulic clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-bleed). Then follow [Clutch drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-drag).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is the idle speed higher than specified? (K-Light 125: 1500 ± 100 rpm.)**
- Yes: [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is the oil old, low or the wrong grade?**
- Yes: [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check), [Change the engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Is there a lot of slack in the drive: a loose chain, worn cush rubbers, or loose engine mounts?**
- Yes: [Check and adjust chain slack](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust), [Check the cush drive rubbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cush-drive-check), [Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Still a loud clunk with everything above correct?**
- Warped steel plates may be dragging. Go to [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cold oil (normal) | Quieter when warm | Wait a moment; correct oil grade |
| Clutch drag | Creeps with lever pulled | [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) |
| High idle | Revs above specification | [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle) |
| Slack in the drive | Loose chain, cush play, loose mounts | [Check the cush drive rubbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cush-drive-check) |
| Warped clutch plates | Persists after the above | [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service) |
