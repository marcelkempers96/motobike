# Jump start a motorcycle safely

**Rider job (no special tools)** · Time: 10 to 15 min (estimate)


A jump start borrows power from another 12 V battery so you can start a bike with a flat battery and get it home or to a workshop. It does not fix the cause. Afterwards, find out why the battery went flat.

## Safety
- Never jump a battery that is swollen, cracked, leaking, frozen or hot. It can burst. See [Battery is boiling, swollen or leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/battery-swollen).
- Only use another 12 V source. Never 24 V.
- A car battery is much bigger than a motorcycle battery. The old MAVCC course warned that jumping from a car can ruin the motorcycle battery and damage its electrics. If a car is the only source, keep the car engine OFF, so its charging system cannot push high voltage into the bike.
- Never let the two clamps touch each other. Never connect + to −. A reversed connection can destroy the ECU (engine computer), regulator and fuses in a second.
- Batteries give off explosive gas. Make the last connection on the bike frame, away from the battery.
- Lithium starter batteries: follow the battery maker's instructions. Many say not to jump them.

## You need
- Jump leads, or a jump pack made for motorcycles. A motorcycle jump pack is the safest choice.
- A donor bike or a car with its engine OFF.

## Steps
1. Key OFF on both vehicles. Lights and accessories off.
2. Check both batteries are 12 V and that you can see which terminal is + and which is −.
3. Connect the red clamp to + on the flat battery.
4. Connect the other red clamp to + on the donor battery.
5. Connect the black clamp to − on the donor battery.
6. Connect the last black clamp to clean bare metal on the bike engine or frame, away from the battery and fuel.
7. Wait one or two minutes.
8. Turn the bike key ON, check neutral, and press the starter in short bursts only. Rest between tries so the starter and leads do not overheat. If your manual gives a cranking time limit, keep to it.
9. When the engine runs, remove the clamps in reverse order: black from the bike frame, black from the donor, red from the donor, red from the bike.
10. Keep the engine running. Ride with lights on only if needed, and go straight to a place where you can test the battery.

## Check your work
- The engine runs smoothly and does not stop when the clamps come off.
- No clamp has melted or sparked heavily.

## If it did not fix the problem
- The bike still will not crank with a good donor: the fault is not the battery. See [Starter relay clicks but the engine does not turn](/moto-zero/repair/problems/starter-clicks) or [Nothing happens when I press the starter button](/moto-zero/repair/problems/starter-silent).
- Starts on a jump but the battery is flat again next day: [Test and charge a 12 V battery](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-test-charge) and [Test the charging system](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/charging-system-test).
- Many kick start bikes can also be started by kicking, even with a weak battery, on bikes with a magneto ignition. Fuel injected bikes need battery power for the pump and ECU.
