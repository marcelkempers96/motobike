# Exhaust and silencer

The exhaust carries burnt gas from the cylinder head to the back of the bike. It has a header pipe bolted to the head, a silencer (muffler) at the end and, on newer bikes, a catalytic converter and an [oxygen sensor](/moto-zero/repair/parts/o2_sensor) in the header. Two-strokes use a fat expansion chamber that is tuned to the engine.

## What it does
The exhaust lets the engine breathe out, cuts noise, and on newer bikes cleans the gas: the catalytic converter burns leftover fuel and carbon monoxide. The pipe and silencer rust from the inside, mounts crack from vibration, and carbon builds up, especially on two-strokes and engines that burn oil. A melted or blocked catalyst chokes the engine.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- [Exhaust much louder](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-loud) or [rattling](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-rattle).
- [Ticking or puffing from a leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-leak) and [popping on overrun](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-popping).
- [Pipe glows red or catalyst overheats](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-glowing): stop the engine.
- [Low power](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-power) from a blocked silencer or catalyst, or a [blocked two-stroke exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/exhaust-blocked).

## When to replace it
When it is rusted through, cracked, or the catalyst is broken or blocked. Two-stroke pipes can often be cleaned: [Decarbonise a two-stroke exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/two-stroke-decoke).

## What to match when buying
- Match make, model and year. A wrong pipe changes power and fuel use, and on injected bikes can trip the warning light.
- On bikes sold with a catalyst, buy a part with one. The K-Light silencer carries approval marks; many countries require a marked, legal silencer.
- Loud "free-flow" silencers and removed baffles are often illegal and can make the engine run lean and hot.

## Fitting notes
- The exhaust stays hot long after the engine stops; let it cool before touching.
- Always fit a new [exhaust gasket](/moto-zero/repair/parts/exhaust_gasket). Fit all bolts loosely, tighten the header first, then the silencer mount.
- Home job: [Find and fix an exhaust leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-leak-check), [Check exhaust mounts and heat shields](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/exhaust-mount-check).
