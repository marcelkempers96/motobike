# Liquid-cooled engine overheats

A liquid-cooled engine pumps coolant through the engine and a radiator. A thermostat holds the coolant in the engine until it is warm, and an electric fan pulls air through the radiator in slow traffic. Overheating shows as the temperature gauge in the red, a warning light, coolant boiling out of the reserve tank, steam, or a sweet smell. Causes, from simple to serious: low coolant, a fan that does not switch on, a blocked or damaged radiator, a stuck thermostat, a failed water pump, and a leaking head gasket.

## Is it safe to ride?
Stop now, switch off, and let it cool fully. Do not open the radiator cap while hot: boiling coolant can spray out and scald you.

## Quick checks first
- Stop and switch off. Wait until the engine is cold.
- Was the fan running when you stopped in traffic? (You can hear and feel it.)
- Reserve tank level once cold.
- Any wet spots or steam at hoses or the radiator?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Once cold, is the reserve tank below the LOWER mark or empty? Check with [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check).**
- Yes: top up. Then find why it was low: [Coolant is leaking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/coolant-leak). If the radiator itself was empty, a workshop should refill and bleed it.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Did it overheat mainly in slow traffic, and was the fan silent?**
- Yes: go to [Radiator fan does not switch on](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fan-not-working) and [Test the radiator fan and its switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fan-test).
- No, it also overheats when moving: go to step 3.

**3. Is the radiator front blocked with mud, insects, plastic or bent fins?**
- Yes: clean gently with low-pressure water from behind. See [Radiator is damaged, bent or blocked](/moto-zero/repair/problems/radiator-damage).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is the gauge reading high while the radiator and upper hose stay cool?**
- Yes: coolant is not reaching the radiator: thermostat stuck closed, water pump failure or an air lock. Ask for [Check the thermostat](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/thermostat-check). If the coolant was just changed, bleed air with [Replace the coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-replace).
- No, the radiator gets hot too: go to step 5.

**5. Is there white smoke, milky oil or bubbles in the reserve tank while running?**
- Yes: head gasket or cracked head. Go to [White smoke or steam from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/white-smoke). Ask for [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Is the gauge reading high but the engine is not really hot (no boiling, normal fan action)?**
- Yes: the sensor or gauge may be wrong. Go to [Temperature gauge reads high or the warning light is on](/moto-zero/repair/problems/temp-gauge-high).
- No: old coolant can scale up the radiator. Change it with [Replace the coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-replace) and have a workshop check the water pump.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low coolant | Reserve tank level | [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check) |
| Fan not working | Silent in traffic | [Test the radiator fan and its switch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/fan-test) |
| Blocked radiator | Mud, bent fins | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
| Thermostat stuck closed | Hot engine, cool radiator | [Check the thermostat](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/thermostat-check) |
| Air lock after refill | Recent coolant work | [Replace the coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-replace) |
| Head gasket | Bubbles, white smoke | [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test) |
