# Brake caliper sticks or is seized

A caliper piston or slide pin does not move freely, so the pads stay pressed on the disc after you let go of the brake, or one pad hardly works. Signs: the wheel drags, the disc is very hot after a short ride, one pad wears fast, the bike pulls under braking, or a burning smell. The usual cause is dirt and corrosion on the pistons, often from old wet fluid or from riding through water and mud.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. A sticking caliper overheats the fluid and pads, can fade the brake completely or lock the wheel.

## Quick checks first
- Spin the wheel on the stand. Does it stop quickly?
- After a short ride, hold the back of your hand near the disc (do not touch). Very hot?
- Look at pad wear: one pad much thinner?
- Look at the fluid: dark?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the lever or pedal have free play and return fully?**
- No: the problem is at the control, not the caliper. Go to [Brake drags and the wheel does not spin freely](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. With a tube fitted, open the bleed nipple briefly. Does the wheel spin free?**
- Yes: pressure was trapped upstream. Go to [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check).
- No: the caliper is sticking. Go to step 3.

**3. Remove the pads. Do all pistons move out evenly when you pull the lever gently (wood block in place), and push back smoothly?**
- Yes, after cleaning: the job is done with [Clean a brake caliper and free the pistons](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/caliper-clean). Go to step 5.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is a piston seized, pitted, or is fluid leaking past its seal?**
- Yes: [Rebuild a brake caliper](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/caliper-rebuild) or replace the caliper. Workshop.
- No, but a slide pin is rusted or its boot torn: clean or replace the pin and boot, grease with the specified grease.

**5. Are the pads soaked in fluid, glazed, or worn unevenly?**
- Yes: [Replace disc brake pads](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-pad-replace).
- No: refit them the same way.

**6. Is the fluid dark?**
- Yes: [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed). Old wet fluid corrodes pistons from inside and the problem will return.
- No: ride a short distance, then check the wheel spins freely and the disc is only warm.

What happens if you keep riding: the pad wears through, the disc overheats and warps, the fluid boils and you can lose the brake.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dirty or corroded pistons | Pistons move unevenly; dirt on exposed part | [Clean a brake caliper and free the pistons](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/caliper-clean) |
| Seized slide pins | Caliper does not slide; torn boot | Clean or replace pins and boots |
| Pitted piston or damaged seal | Pitting; fluid at piston | [Rebuild a brake caliper](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/caliper-rebuild) |
| Old fluid causing corrosion | Dark fluid | [Replace brake fluid and bleed the brakes](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-bleed) |
| Master cylinder not releasing | Bleed nipple test frees wheel | [Check the brake master cylinder](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/master-cylinder-check) |
