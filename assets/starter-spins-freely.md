# Starter motor whirs but the engine does not turn

You press start and hear the starter motor spin fast, often with a high whirr, but the engine does not turn over. The motor works, but its drive is not reaching the crankshaft. MAVCC calls a slipping starter clutch the most common electric-start fault. The starter clutch (one-way clutch) sits behind the flywheel and is meant to lock when the starter drives and free itself once the engine runs.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes, if the bike starts with the kickstarter. Do not keep pressing the button: the spinning starter can damage the gears and the clutch further.

## Quick checks first
- Does the engine start normally with the kickstarter?
- Did it happen suddenly, after a backfire, or after a cold morning?
- Was the starter recently removed or rewired?
- Is the battery fully charged?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the battery fully charged?**
- No: charge it and try again with [Test and charge a 12 V battery](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-test-charge). The K-Light 125 fault chart lists low battery power for this symptom.
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Was the starter recently removed, replaced or rewired?**
- Yes: check that it turns the right way. A starter running backwards spins without turning the engine (K-Light 125 manual). Check the wiring against the diagram and the part number.
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does the starter sometimes catch and turn the engine, sometimes not?**
- Yes: the starter clutch is slipping. Go to step 5.
- No, it never turns the engine: go to step 4.

**4. Remove the starter. Is its gear, or the first reduction gear you can see, broken or stripped?**
- Yes: replace the damaged gears. A workshop should also check the gear it meshes with. [Remove and inspect the starter motor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/starter-motor-service).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. The starter clutch needs inspecting.** This means removing the side cover and pulling the flywheel with the correct tools. Hand it to a workshop: [Check the starter clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/starter-clutch-check). Meanwhile, use the kickstarter if the bike has one.

What happens if you keep using it: a slipping starter clutch can suddenly grab while the engine is turning, which can break gear teeth or the clutch body.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Flat battery (some designs slip when voltage is low) | Low voltage; works after charging | [Test and charge a 12 V battery](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/battery-test-charge) |
| Worn or sticking starter clutch rollers | Starter spins freely, sometimes catches | [Check the starter clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/starter-clutch-check) |
| Broken starter or idle gear teeth | Visible damage when the starter is removed | [Remove and inspect the starter motor](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/starter-motor-service) |
| Starter wired backwards or wrong part | After recent work; check turning direction | Correct the wiring or fit the right starter |
| Broken starter clutch spring or bolts | Found when the flywheel is removed | [Check the starter clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/starter-clutch-check) |
