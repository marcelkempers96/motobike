# Whining noise from the engine

A whine is a steady high-pitched hum that rises and falls in pitch with speed. Inside a motorcycle engine it usually comes from gears: the primary drive between crankshaft and clutch, the gearbox, or the oil pump drive. It can also come from a water pump bearing, a fuel pump (fuel-injected bikes), or on scooters the drive belt and final gears. Most gear whine is a wear sign rather than an emergency, but a new whine that grows quickly, or comes with metal in the oil, needs attention.

## Is it safe to ride?
Usually yes, gently, while you diagnose it. Stop if it becomes a grind or knock, or if the oil warning light comes on.

## Quick checks first
- Does the whine follow engine speed (rpm) or road speed?
- Is it present in every gear, or only in one?
- Does it change when the clutch lever is pulled?
- Is the oil level and grade correct?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Fuel-injected bike: with the key on and engine off, is there a whine that lasts only a few seconds?**
- Yes: this is the fuel pump priming; normal. If it runs all the time or is very loud, go to [Fuel pump whines loudly or runs all the time](/moto-zero/repair/problems/fuel-pump-noisy).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Engine running, bike still in neutral. Is the whine there?**
- No, only when moving: it follows road speed or gears. Go to step 5.
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Pull the clutch lever in and out. Does the whine change?**
- Yes: primary drive gears or clutch. See [Rattle or whir from the clutch that changes when the lever is pulled](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-noise).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Liquid-cooled engine: is the whine loudest at the water pump, or is there coolant at the pump weep hole?**
- Yes: worn water pump bearing. See [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test).
- No, or air-cooled: check the oil level with [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check). Low oil makes gears and the oil pump noisy. If correct, ask for [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) to check the oil pump drive and primary gears.

**5. Riding: does the whine appear only in one or two gears?**
- Yes: worn gearbox gears or bearings. Go to [Whining noise in one or more gears](/moto-zero/repair/problems/gearbox-whine).
- In all gears, changing with road speed: chain, sprockets or wheel bearing. Scooter: drive belt or final gears, see [Noise from the scooter drive case](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cvt-noise).

**6. At the next oil change, look for metal with [Inspect drained oil for clues](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-inspect).** Chips or flakes mean gear or bearing damage: go to [Metal particles or flakes in the oil](/moto-zero/repair/problems/metal-in-oil).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel pump priming (normal) | Only at key on, a few seconds | None needed |
| Low or worn-out oil | Dipstick, oil age | [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check) |
| Gearbox gear or bearing wear | One gear only | [Assess internal gearbox faults](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/gearbox-assessment) |
| Primary drive or clutch | Changes with clutch lever | [Assess bottom end and internal engine damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment) |
| Water pump bearing | Weep hole wet, noise at pump | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
