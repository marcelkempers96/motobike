# Tyre slowly loses air

The tyre is fine after you pump it, but it is soft again after a day or a few days. Air is escaping through a small hole, the valve, the joint between tyre and rim (tubeless), or a crack in the rim. A low tyre wears fast, makes steering heavy, uses more fuel and can overheat and fail. This check finds where the air goes.

## Is it safe to ride?
Only short distances with the pressure set correctly, and check it before every ride. If it goes soft within an hour, treat it as a flat and do not ride.

## Quick checks first
- Measure both tyres cold with a good gauge. Is one really lower, or is the gauge wrong?
- Look at the valve cap: is it there?
- Look for a nail, screw or thorn in the tread.
- Is the tyre tubeless (TUBELESS or TL on the sidewall, valve fixed in the rim) or tube type?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Set the correct pressure, then measure again after 24 hours (cold). Has it dropped noticeably?**
- No: the tyre is holding. Small changes come from temperature. Check weekly with [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check).
- Yes: go to step 2.

**2. Put soapy water on the valve tip. Does it bubble?**
- Yes: the valve core leaks. Go to [Air leaking from the tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/problems/valve-leak).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Put soapy water round the valve base and bend the valve gently. Bubbles or cracks?**
- Yes: the valve stem is perished or badly seated. [Check and replace a tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-check).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Lift the wheel. Spray soapy water over the whole tread and both sidewalls, turning slowly. Do bubbles appear on the tread?**
- Yes, from an object or small hole in the tread centre: tubeless, see [Plug a tubeless tyre puncture](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tubeless-plug); tube type, see [Repair a puncture in an inner tube](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tube-puncture-repair).
- Yes, from a sidewall cut or crack: the tyre cannot be repaired. [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Tubeless only: spray the line where the tyre meets the rim, both sides. Bubbles?**
- Yes: go to [Air leaking where the tyre meets the rim](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak).
- No, or tube type: go to step 6.

**6. Look at the rim closely: dents on the edge, cracks near spokes or at the valve hole?**
- Yes: go to [Rim is dented or cracked](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rim-damage).
- No: go to step 7.

**7. Tube type: remove the tube and test it in water.**
- Small hole found: patch or replace the tube, and find what caused it (spoke end, rim tape, object).
- No hole and no other leak: the tyre may be old and porous, or the gauge is wrong. Check with a second gauge. [Inspect tyres for wear and damage](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-inspect).

What happens if you keep pumping it up and riding: a low tyre flexes and heats. A tube can tear at the valve, and a tubeless tyre can come off its bead in a corner.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Small object in the tread | Bubbles at a nail or thorn | [Tubeless plug](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tubeless-plug) or [tube patch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tube-puncture-repair) |
| Leaking valve core | Bubbles at the valve tip | [Check and replace a tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-check) |
| Perished rubber valve | Cracks or bubbles at the valve base | [Check and replace a tyre valve](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/valve-check) |
| Bead leak (tubeless) | Bubbles at the rim line | [Bead leak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bead-leak) |
| Rim crack or dent | Visible damage, bubbles at the rim | [Rim damage](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rim-damage) |
| Old porous tyre | Slow loss, no single leak found | [Replace a tyre](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-replace) |
