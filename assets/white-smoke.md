# White smoke or steam from the exhaust

Thin white vapour when the engine is cold is normal. Burning fuel makes water, and it condenses in a cold pipe and drips from the outlet. It should clear within a few minutes as the pipe warms. Thick white smoke that stays when the engine is warm, and smells sweet, means coolant is burning in the cylinder on a liquid-cooled engine, usually through a failed head gasket or a cracked head. Some manuals, including the K-Light, call oil smoke "white"; if the smoke is bluish and smells of burnt oil, it is oil.

## Is it safe to ride?
Cold-start vapour that clears: yes. Thick white smoke when warm on a liquid-cooled bike: do not ride; the engine can overheat and coolant can damage the bearings.

## Quick checks first
- Does it clear after 5 minutes of riding?
- Is your bike air-cooled or liquid-cooled?
- Liquid-cooled: is the coolant level falling?
- Smell: sweet (coolant), burnt oil, or petrol?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Does the vapour clear once the engine and pipe are warm, with only water drips at the outlet?**
- Yes: normal condensation, especially in cool or humid weather and on short trips. No repair needed. Short trips also put water in the oil; see [Oil is milky or creamy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/milky-oil) if the oil looks creamy.
- No, it stays when warm: go to step 2.

**2. Is your engine air-cooled?**
- Yes: there is no coolant to burn. Persistent white or grey smoke is oil. Go to [Blue or grey smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke).
- No, liquid-cooled: go to step 3.

**3. Engine cold: check the reserve tank with [Check and top up coolant](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/coolant-check). Has the level fallen with no outside leak?**
- Yes: coolant is likely going into the engine. Go to step 4.
- No: confirm the smoke colour with [Diagnose exhaust smoke by colour](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/smoke-diagnosis). If it is oil, go to [Blue or grey smoke from the exhaust](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke).

**4. Check the engine oil with [Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check). Is it milky or above the mark?**
- Yes: coolant is mixing with the oil. Stop riding. Go to [Oil is milky or creamy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/milky-oil).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. With the engine running, are there bubbles in the reserve tank, or does it smell of exhaust?**
- Yes: combustion gas is entering the coolant: head gasket or cracked head.
- No: still possible. Go to step 6.

**6. Workshop: [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) with a pressure test, and [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test) for bubbles in the radiator.** A head gasket job also needs the head checked for flatness, because overheating warps heads.

**What happens if you ignore it:** the engine loses coolant, overheats and can warp the head; coolant in the oil destroys bearings.

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Normal condensation | Clears when warm | None needed |
| Oil smoke called "white" | Bluish, burnt oil smell | [Diagnose exhaust smoke by colour](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/smoke-diagnosis) |
| Head gasket leak (liquid-cooled) | Coolant loss, bubbles | [Do a leak-down test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/leak-down-test) |
| Cracked head or cylinder | Coolant loss after overheating | [Find a coolant leak](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cooling-leak-test) |
