# Side stand and spring

The side stand is the leg on the left side that holds the bike leaning when parked. It swings on a pivot bolt under the frame, and one or two strong springs hold it up when riding and down when parked. Many bikes have a switch that stops the engine if you put it in gear with the stand down.

## What it does
The springs pull the stand past a centre point so it stays either fully up or fully down. If a spring is weak or missing, the stand can drop while riding and dig into the road in a left turn, which can throw the rider off. A worn pivot lets the bike lean too far and fall over.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Stand does not snap up, flops, or the bike leans too far: [Side stand spring is weak or the stand is loose](/moto-zero/repair/problems/side-stand-weak).
- Engine dies when the stand moves or when selecting gear: [Engine dies when the side stand moves](/moto-zero/repair/problems/dies-side-stand). This is the stand switch.
- Will not start with stand up: [Will not start on the side stand](/moto-zero/repair/problems/start-blocked-interlock).

## When to replace it
Replace a weak, stretched or broken spring at once. Do not ride with a stand that does not stay up. Replace the pivot bolt or bushes if worn, and the stand if bent or cracked.

## What to match when buying
- Springs: make, model and year; length, wire thickness and hook shape. Some bikes use two springs, one inside the other.
- Stand: length and foot shape; a longer stand makes the bike stand too upright and fall over the other way.
- The stand switch is a separate part: [Side stand, clutch and neutral switches](/moto-zero/repair/parts/interlock_switches).

## Fitting notes
- Springs are very strong. Use a spring puller or a strap; keep fingers clear. Wear eye protection.
- Grease the pivot bolt, tighten it so the stand moves freely, then lock it.
- See [Check the side stand](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/side-stand-check).
