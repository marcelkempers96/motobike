# Wheel locks too easily

The wheel stops turning and skids with only light pressure on the lever or pedal, or the brake grabs suddenly instead of building smoothly. A locked front wheel usually means a fall; a locked rear makes the bike slide sideways. Causes include a brake that is already partly on (drag), a grabbing drum shoe, contaminated or wrong pads, a CBS that is out of balance, worn tyres or low grip, and poor rear suspension that lets the wheel bounce off the road. This page is about the **brakes** locking the wheel. If the wheel is locked while you push the bike, or on an electric bike with the motor, see the related pages.

## Is it safe to ride?
No. Find the cause before riding again.

## Quick checks first
- Spin both wheels on the stand. Any drag?
- Check tyre pressure and tread.
- Is it only in the wet, or on gravel or sand? Locking is easier there for any bike.
- Electric bike: is the wheel locked even with the brakes released? Go to [Rear wheel is locked or very hard to push](/moto-zero/repair/problems/ev-wheel-locked).
- Does the bike have ABS, and is the ABS light on?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the wheel hard to turn by hand with the brake released?**
- Yes: the brake is dragging, so a small extra push locks it. Go to [Brake drags and the wheel does not spin freely](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. ABS bike with the ABS light on?**
- Yes: the ABS has switched off. Go to [ABS warning light is on](/moto-zero/repair/problems/abs-light).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Is it the rear wheel on a CBS bike?**
- Yes: check the rear pushrod setting and pedal free play with [Adjust the rear brake pedal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-pedal-adjust). If still wrong, [Check a combined braking system (CBS)](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cbs-check).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is it a drum brake that grabs?**
- Yes: worn cam, broken return spring, or oil-soaked or glazed linings make shoes grab. [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Disc brake: are the pads new, the wrong type, or contaminated, or is a piston sticking?**
- Yes: fit pads listed for your bike and clean the caliper with [Clean a brake caliper and free the pistons](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/caliper-clean).
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Are the tyres worn, hard, or at the wrong pressure, or does the rear bounce over bumps?**
- Yes: [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check) and [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check).
- No: practise progressive braking: squeeze, do not grab, and use both brakes.

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Brake partly on (drag) | Wheel hard to spin | [Check for brake drag](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-drag-check) |
| Low grip tyres or wrong pressure | Worn tread, low pressure | [Check tyre pressure](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-pressure-check) |
| Grabbing drum shoes | Drum brake, uneven bite | [Service a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-service) |
| CBS out of adjustment | Rear locks on CBS bike | [Adjust the rear brake pedal](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-pedal-adjust) |
| Wrong or contaminated pads | Pads not listed for bike; oily | New correct pads |
| ABS fault | ABS light on | [Diagnose an ABS warning light](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/abs-check) |
