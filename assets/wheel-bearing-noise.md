# Rumbling or grinding from a wheel

A rumble, growl or grinding noise comes from the front or rear and rises with road speed, not engine speed. It continues when you pull the clutch in and coast. A worn wheel bearing is a common cause, but dragging brakes, a cupped tyre, a dry chain or a speedo drive can sound the same. This check separates them.

## Is it safe to ride?
With care for a short distance if there is no play in the wheel. If the noise is grinding metal, or the wheel has play, do not ride.

## Quick checks first
- Coast with the clutch in: does the noise stay? Then it is from the wheels or chain, not the engine.
- Does the noise change when you lean left and right? Bearings often change tone when loaded.
- Touch the hub after a ride (carefully): is one much hotter than the other?
- Look at the tyre: cupped tread hums too.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Lift the wheel and rock it top and bottom. Any play?**
- Yes: go to [Wheel moves sideways on the axle](/moto-zero/repair/problems/wheel-bearing-play).
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Spin the wheel by hand. Does it slow down quickly, or do you hear a regular swish or scrape once per turn?**
- Yes: a brake is dragging. [Check for brake drag](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-drag-check), see [Brake drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/brake-drag).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Rear: take the chain off the sprocket (or slacken it fully) and spin again. Has the noise gone?**
- Yes: the noise is the chain or sprocket. See [Chain is noisy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/chain-noise).
- No, or front wheel: go to step 4.

**4. Spin the wheel with your fingers on the fork leg or swingarm next to the hub. Do you feel a rough, gritty vibration?**
- Yes: the bearing is worn. [Check wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-check), then [Replace wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-replace).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Front with a mechanical speedo: disconnect the speedo cable and spin. Quieter?**
- Yes: the speedo drive or cable is dry or damaged. [Replace the speedometer cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/speedo-cable-replace); grease the hub drive when the wheel is out.
- No: go to step 6.

**6. Is the tyre cupped or worn unevenly?**
- Yes: the hum may be the tread. See [Tyre worn into a scalloped or cupped pattern](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tyre-cupping).
- No: remove the wheel and check the bearings off the bike. On the rear, also check the sprocket carrier and cush drive ([Check the cush drive rubbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cush-drive-check)).

**7. Electric hub motor?** Noise from the motor itself: [Grinding, clicking or growling from the motor](/moto-zero/repair/problems/ev-motor-noise).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Worn wheel bearing | Rough feel when spun, no drag | [Replace wheel bearings](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/wheel-bearing-replace) |
| Dragging brake | Wheel slows fast, swish per turn | [Brake drag check](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-drag-check) |
| Chain or sprocket | Noise gone with chain off | [Chain slack and lube](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/chain-adjust) |
| Cupped tyre | Stepped tread blocks | [Inspect tyres](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/tyre-inspect) |
| Dry speedo drive | Quieter with cable off | [Speedo cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/speedo-cable-replace) |
