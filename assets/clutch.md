# Clutch assembly

The clutch sits on the gearbox input shaft, behind the right engine cover on most commuter bikes. It joins and separates the engine and the gearbox so you can pull away, change gear and stop without stalling.

## What it does
A stack of friction plates and steel plates is squeezed together by springs. When the plates are clamped, engine power passes to the gearbox. When you pull the lever, the cable pushes a lifter that takes the spring force off, the plates separate and the drive stops. In a wet clutch (most motorcycles) the plates run in engine oil, which cools them. Step-through bikes often have a semi-automatic or centrifugal clutch that works by engine speed and the gear pedal instead of a lever.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Revs rise but the bike does not speed up: [Clutch slips](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-slip).
- Bike creeps or lurches with the lever pulled: [Clutch drags](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-drag), often with [Hard to find neutral](/moto-zero/repair/problems/hard-to-find-neutral).
- Snatching when pulling away: [Clutch judder](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-judder).
- Lever very hard to pull: [Heavy clutch](/moto-zero/repair/problems/heavy-clutch).
- Rattle that changes with the lever: [Clutch noise](/moto-zero/repair/problems/clutch-noise).
- Step-through clutch slips or bites hard: [Semi-automatic clutch fault](/moto-zero/repair/problems/auto-clutch-fault).

## When to replace it
You almost never replace the whole assembly. Check the cable and lever free play first, then the oil. If the fault remains, the workshop measures the plates and springs and replaces worn parts as a set. Replace the basket or centre only if the slots are deeply notched.

## What to match when buying
- People usually buy [Clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/parts/clutch_plates), a [Clutch cable](/moto-zero/repair/parts/clutch_cable), a cover [gasket](/moto-zero/repair/parts/gaskets) and the correct [engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/parts/engine_oil).
- Match make, model, year and engine code. The engine number is stamped on the crankcase (K-Light 125 example: QJ157FMI).
- A wet clutch needs motorcycle oil suited to wet clutches. Some car oils contain friction modifiers that make plates slip.

## Fitting notes
- Adjust free play first: [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust).
- Opening the clutch is a workshop job: special holding tools and the locknut torque are needed. See [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service).
- Step-through clutches: [Check a semi-automatic or centrifugal clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/auto-clutch-check).
