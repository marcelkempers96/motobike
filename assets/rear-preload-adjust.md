# Adjust rear spring preload

**Rider job (no special tools)** · Time: 5 to 15 min (editorial estimate) (estimate)


Spring preload is how much the shock spring is already squeezed before you sit on the bike. More preload holds the rear higher under a heavy load (passenger, goods); less preload gives a softer feel with a light load. Preload does not make a worn damper work again. Many commuter shocks have a stepped collar at the bottom of the spring with several positions; for example the Hero Honda Splendor Plus rear shocks have a 5-step adjuster.

## Safety
- Put the bike on its centre stand. Take weight off the rear wheel if the collar is hard to turn.
- On twin shocks, **always set both sides to the same step**. Different settings make the bike twist and handle badly.
- Keep fingers clear of the spring coils while turning the collar.

## You need
- The C-spanner from the tool kit. A screwdriver used as a lever slips and cuts hands.

## Steps
1. Find the adjuster: usually a stepped ring under the spring, with notches or numbered steps. Some shocks have a threaded collar with a locknut instead.
2. Note the current step on each shock.
3. Decide: higher step (more preload) for a passenger or load, or if the rear sits low or bottoms. Lower step for riding alone with no load, if the rear feels harsh and sits high.
4. Fit the C-spanner into the slot on the collar and turn it so the collar climbs to the next step. Move one step at a time.
5. Do the same on the other shock to exactly the same step.
6. Threaded collars: loosen the locknut, turn the collar the same number of turns on both sides, retighten the locknut.
7. Sit on the bike and bounce. Ride a short distance and judge the feel.
8. Write the setting down.

## Check your work
- Both shocks on the same step.
- The rear does not hit its stops over normal bumps with your usual load.
- The headlight beam is not pointing too high with a load on the back. See [Aim the headlight](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/headlight-aim).

## If it did not fix the problem
- Still bottoms at the highest step, or bounces: the spring is tired or the damper is worn. [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check).
- To set preload by measurement: [Measure suspension sag](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/sag-measure).
