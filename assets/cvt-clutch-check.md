# Check the scooter clutch and bell

**Workshop job (assessment and hand-off)** · Time: 20 min (look inside the case); 1 to 2 h workshop assessment (estimate)


The scooter clutch sits inside the rear pulley. It has shoes with friction lining held in by small springs. As the engine speeds up, the shoes swing out and grip the inside of a steel drum called the bell, which drives the rear wheel through the gear case. Worn or glazed shoes, a grooved bell, oil on the lining or tired springs cause judder when pulling away, slipping, rattling and creeping at idle. This page is an **assessment and hand-off**: you can look, but the clutch is under strong spring tension and needs holding and compressing tools.

## Safety
- Let the engine and exhaust cool.
- The rear pulley spring is strong. Released without a compressor, parts can fly out and injure you.
- Do not ride a scooter that creeps strongly at idle in traffic. Hold the brakes at junctions.

## What you can check (home)
1. Check idle speed first. A high idle makes the shoes grip at idle: [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle).
2. Open the drive case (see [Inspect and replace the scooter drive belt](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/cvt-belt-replace)). Look at the bell from the outside and, if visible, the inner surface.
3. Look for oil or grease in the case. Oil on the bell or shoes makes them grab and slip; find the leak first ([Oil leaking from the scooter rear gear case](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cvt-gear-oil-leak)).
4. Look at the amount of dust. Fine dust is normal; lumps of lining or metal mean the clutch needs opening.
5. With the engine off, try to turn the bell by hand while holding the pulley: note any roughness.

## What the workshop will do
1. Hold the bell and remove its nut, then the bell. Check the inner surface for grooves, steps, glazing and blue heat marks, and measure the inside diameter against the model limit.
2. Remove the rear pulley assembly with a spring compressor.
3. Measure shoe lining thickness and look for uneven wear and oil soaking. A generic fault chart in the K-Light manual says: clean off oil, replace the complete set of shoes if worn, and repair or replace if less than 70% of the shoe face is touching.
4. Check the small shoe springs. Stretched springs let the shoes rattle and grip too early. Replace springs with the shoes.
5. Check the main (torque) spring, the rear pulley faces, the sliding guide pins and needle bearings. Clean and re-grease the bearings without getting grease on the belt faces.
6. Fit new circlips and tighten the nuts to the model torque.

## What to ask for
- Shoes and springs replaced as a set; bell replaced if grooved or beyond its limit.
- Shown the old parts and the measurements.
- Confirmation that any oil leak into the case was fixed.

## Do not attempt at home
- Do not remove the clutch nut by jamming the bell or using pipe grips; use the correct holding tool.
- Do not release the rear pulley spring without a compressor.

## If it did not fix the problem
- Still slips: [Scooter revs but accelerates slowly](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cvt-slip).
- Judder remains with a good clutch: check engine mounts and the rear shock bushes ([Check engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-mount-check), [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check)).
