# Clutch drags: the bike creeps or lurches when in gear with the lever pulled

You pull the clutch lever fully, select first gear and the bike jerks forward, tries to creep or stalls the engine. Neutral is hard to find and gears clunk. The clutch is not separating fully, so some drive still reaches the gearbox. Usually the cause is too much free play or a stretched cable; sometimes it is cold, thick oil, a high idle or warped steel plates.

## Is it safe to ride?
Ride with care. The bike may lurch into the junction when you select first, or stall when you stop. Hold the front brake when selecting a gear at a standstill.

## Quick checks first
- Pull the lever: does it come almost to the bar before the clutch releases? Too much free play is the most common cause.
- Does it happen only for the first few minutes on a cold morning?
- Is the idle speed higher than usual?
- Is there a fluid reservoir on the clutch lever (hydraulic clutch)? Check its level.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the lever free play larger than specified? (K-Light 125: 10 to 15 mm.)**
- Yes: [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust), then test again.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Pull the lever fully and watch the release arm on the engine (cable bikes). Does it move its full travel?**
- No, the cable stretches or the lever is bent or worn: [Replace the clutch cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-replace); for a damaged lever see [Brake or clutch lever is loose, bent or broken](/moto-zero/repair/problems/lever-damaged).
- Hydraulic clutch with a soft lever: [Bleed a hydraulic clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-bleed).
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Is the idle speed too high? (K-Light 125: 1500 ± 100 rpm.)**
- Yes: a high idle makes the plates spin harder and drag more. Go to [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle).
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Does the drag happen only when cold and disappear after a few minutes of riding?**
- Yes: the plates are held together by cold, thick oil. Check the oil grade and age ([Check the engine oil level](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/oil-level-check), [Change the engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service)). A bike that stood for weeks may have stuck plates; ride gently until they free.
- No, it drags even when warm: go to step 5.

**5. With free play, cable, idle and oil correct, does it still drag?**
- Yes: likely warped steel plates, uneven springs, a notched basket or hub, or a worn pushrod or lifter. Go to [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service).
- No: the fault is fixed. Recheck free play in a week.

**What happens if you ignore it:** the gearbox dogs take a hammering on every gear change, gear selection gets harder, and the engine may stall at junctions ([Engine stalls at idle or at junctions](/moto-zero/repair/problems/stalls-at-idle)).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too much free play | Lever nearly touches the bar before release | [Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust) |
| Stretched cable or worn lever | Release arm does not move fully | [Replace the clutch cable](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-replace) |
| Air in a hydraulic clutch | Soft lever, low reservoir | [Bleed a hydraulic clutch](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-bleed) |
| High idle | Revs above specification | [Idle speed is too high](/moto-zero/repair/problems/high-idle) |
| Thick, old or wrong oil | Drag only when cold | [Change the engine oil](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-oil-service) |
| Warped steels, notched basket, worn lifter | Persists after all the above | [Inspect and replace clutch plates and springs](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-plate-service) |
