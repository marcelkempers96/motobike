# Centre stand is hard to use or scrapes

Getting the bike onto the centre stand (main stand) takes a lot of effort, the bike rocks on the stand, or the folded stand scrapes the road when cornering. Often the cause is technique, a dry pivot, weak springs or a sagging rear suspension.

## Is it safe to ride?
Yes, if the stand stays folded up. If it scrapes the road in corners or hangs down, fix it before riding: a stand that digs in can lift the rear wheel.

## Quick checks first
- Are you using your foot on the stand tab and lifting by the frame or grab rail, not the seat?
- Fold the stand up: does it snap tight against its stop?
- Is the rubber stop pad there?
- Does the rear sit lower than normal, especially with a load?

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is it hard to lift the bike onto the stand?**
- Yes: go to step 2.
- No: go to step 4.

**2. Are you pressing down on the stand tab with your full weight on that foot, and pulling up and back by a solid frame part?**
- No: use the technique on [Check the centre stand](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/centre-stand-check). Most effort problems disappear.
- Yes: go to step 3.

**3. Is the stand pivot dry, rusty or tight?**
- Yes: remove, clean and grease the pivot ([Check the centre stand](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/centre-stand-check)).
- No: check for bent legs or a rear tyre much larger than standard, which make lifting harder.

**4. Does the bike rock or lean when on the stand?**
- Yes: the legs are bent or the feet worn. Replace the stand. Check the brackets for cracks.
- No: go to step 5.

**5. Does the folded stand scrape the road in corners or over bumps?**
- Yes: go to step 6.
- No: go to step 7.

**6. Does the stand fold fully up and is the spring strong?**
- No: fit new springs and a stop pad.
- Yes: the rear may be sitting too low. See [Rear sits too low](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-sagging), [Adjust rear spring preload](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-preload-adjust), [Check the rear shock absorbers](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/rear-shock-check).

**7. Does the stand rattle when riding?**
- Yes: worn stop pad, weak spring or loose pivot. Fix as in step 6, and see [Rattling from panels, mudguards or the seat](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bodywork-rattle).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong technique | Easy when done correctly | Use foot on tab, lift by frame |
| Dry or rusty pivot | Stiff movement | [Clean and grease](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/centre-stand-check) |
| Weak springs, missing stop pad | Stand not tight when folded | New springs and pad |
| Bent legs | Bike rocks on stand | New stand |
| Rear suspension sagging | Scrapes with load | [Rear sits too low](/moto-zero/repair/problems/rear-sagging) |
