# Brake or clutch lever is loose, bent or broken

A hand lever is bent, snapped, wobbles on its pivot, or the whole lever assembly turns on the handlebar. This usually follows a drop or crash, or long wear on the pivot. A broken front brake lever leaves you with less braking; a broken clutch lever makes gear changes and stopping in gear hard.

## Is it safe to ride?
Not with a broken, cracked or badly bent brake or clutch lever, or a brake lever assembly that moves on the bar. Replace or fix it first.

## Quick checks first
- Which lever: front brake (right) or clutch (left; on scooters the left lever is the rear brake)?
- Is it bent, cracked, snapped, or just loose?
- Does the whole perch or master cylinder turn on the bar?
- After a fall: check the brake fluid reservoir for cracks and leaks.

## Diagnose it step by step

**1. Is the lever cracked or broken, or bent so it is hard to reach or touches the throttle or grip?**
- Yes: fit a new lever. Do not straighten a cast lever; it can snap later. Follow [Service brake and clutch lever pivots](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service). Go to step 5 after fitting.
- No: go to step 2.

**2. Does the whole lever assembly (perch or master cylinder) turn on the handlebar when you pull or push it?**
- Yes: tighten the clamp bolts to the bike's value (**K-Light 125 left lever assembly bolt: 10 to 12 N·m**). If the bar is bent from a fall, see [The bike has been dropped or crashed](/moto-zero/repair/problems/crash-check).
- No: go to step 3.

**3. Does the lever wobble up and down or sideways on its pivot?**
- Yes: remove the pivot bolt, check for an oval hole or worn bolt, grease and refit with the locknut ([Service brake and clutch lever pivots](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service)). Replace worn parts.
- No: go to step 4.

**4. Is the lever stiff or slow to return?**
- Yes: grease the pivot; check the cable ([A control cable is frayed or stiff](/moto-zero/repair/problems/cable-frayed)).
- No: go to step 5.

**5. After any lever work: does the brake work, and does the brake light come on with the lever and go off when released?**
- No brake light, or it stays on: [Test and adjust the brake light switches](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-switch-test). **K-Light 125: light on at about 15 mm lever travel.**
- Brake lever soft: check fluid level and for leaks ([Check brake fluid level and condition](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/brake-fluid-check)); see [Brake lever or pedal feels spongy](/moto-zero/repair/problems/spongy-brake).
- Clutch: set free play ([Adjust clutch lever free play](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/clutch-cable-adjust)). Drum brake lever: [Adjust a drum brake](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/drum-brake-adjust).

## Causes, most likely first

| Cause | How to confirm | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Bent or broken lever from a fall | Visible damage | New lever, [lever service](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service) |
| Clamp bolts loose | Assembly turns on bar | Tighten clamp |
| Worn or dry pivot | Lever wobbles or sticks | [Lever service](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/lever-service) |
| Bent handlebar | Lever angle wrong after fall | [Crash check](/moto-zero/repair/problems/crash-check) |

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
