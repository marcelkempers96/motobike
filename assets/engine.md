# Engine (complete)

The engine is the block of metal between your feet. On most commuter bikes it holds the cylinder, the crankshaft, the clutch and the gearbox in one set of cases, so one oil does all the work.

## What it does
The engine burns a mix of fuel and air above the piston. The pressure pushes the piston down, and the crankshaft turns that push into rotation. A four-stroke needs two turns of the crank for each power stroke and uses valves; a two-stroke fires every turn and uses ports in the cylinder wall. Every engine needs four things at the right time: fuel, air, a spark and good compression. Wear in rings, valves and bearings slowly takes away compression and oil pressure.

## Signs it is worn or failing
- Hard to start and weak: [Low compression](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-compression), [Engine feels weak](/moto-zero/repair/problems/low-power).
- Smoke: [Blue or grey smoke](/moto-zero/repair/problems/blue-smoke), [Oil level drops](/moto-zero/repair/problems/oil-level-falling).
- Noise: [Deep knocking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/deep-knock), [Rumbling](/moto-zero/repair/problems/bearing-rumble), [Ticking](/moto-zero/repair/problems/tappet-tick).
- [Metal in the oil](/moto-zero/repair/problems/metal-in-oil) or a [seized engine](/moto-zero/repair/problems/engine-seized): stop riding.

## When to replace it
Almost never as a whole. Test first with a [compression test](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/compression-test), then get a [top end assessment](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/top-end-assessment) or a [bottom end assessment](/moto-zero/repair/repairs/engine-internal-assessment). Replace the whole engine only if the cases or crank are broken and parts cost more than a used engine.

## What to match when buying
- People usually buy parts, not engines: [gaskets](/moto-zero/repair/parts/gaskets), [piston](/moto-zero/repair/parts/piston) and [rings](/moto-zero/repair/parts/piston_rings), [valves](/moto-zero/repair/parts/valves), [oil seals](/moto-zero/repair/parts/oil_seals), [cam chain](/moto-zero/repair/parts/cam_chain) or [pushrods](/moto-zero/repair/parts/pushrods).
- For a whole engine, match the engine code stamped on the crankcase, not just "125 cc". The K-Light engine number starts with QJ157FMI. Check carburettor or fuel injection, kick or electric start, gearbox type and mounting points.
- A used engine should come with a compression reading and no metal in the oil. Check that the engine number is not filed or re-stamped; that can mean a stolen engine and trouble at registration.

## Fitting notes
- Engine removal is a workshop job: the bike must be supported and the engine lowered on a jack.
- After fitting, the K-Light manual has you check oil level, chain slack, rear brake pedal play, clutch play and throttle play before riding.
- Use new throttle body or intake O-rings and tighten the [engine mounting bolts](/moto-zero/repair/parts/engine_mounts) to the manual values.
