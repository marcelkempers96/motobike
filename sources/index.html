Where the material comes from

Curriculum · 1988 · 877 pages

Motorcycle Mechanic: Teacher Edition

Baugus, M. & Fulkerson, D. (Ed.). Mid-America Vocational Curriculum Consortium, Stillwater, Oklahoma. ERIC document ED 319 915.

A 19-unit, competency-based course in entry-level motorcycle mechanics: safety, tools, metric measurement, fasteners, engines, battery and electrical service, carburation, wheels and tyres, steering and suspension, brakes, clutches, starting, tune-ups, pre-delivery and one-cylinder top-end service. The backbone for the workshop and job-sheet approach of Moto Zero.

View on ERIC →
Service manual · KS Motorcycles / Qianjiang · 278 pages

Maintenance Manual: K-Light 125

KS Motorcycles. Maintenance Manual of Two-wheeled Motorcycle (K-LIGHT125).

The factory manual for a current fuel-injected 125 cc single: specifications, fault diagnosis, inspection intervals and procedures for the battery and charging, ignition, starter, lighting, brakes and CBS, wheels and suspension, chain drive, fuel system, lubrication and cylinder head. Every reference value and service limit in Moto Zero maps onto this machine.

KS Motorcycles →
Technician starter kit · 50 pages

Unit 2: The Motorcycle

Hero Honda technician starter kit, Unit 2: The Motorcycle (IGNOU course material).

A compact general description of the motorcycle and its systems (four-stroke petrol engine, lubrication, constant-mesh gearbox, clutch, chassis and electrics), written for trainee technicians working on 100 cc commuter machines.

Exhibition catalogue · Guggenheim Museum · 1998

The Art of the Motorcycle

Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, New York, 1998.

The catalogue of the landmark exhibition: a century of motorcycle design from the steam velocipede to the superbike, with essays on technology, culture and form. The source for the history and design-lineage sections of Module 1.
