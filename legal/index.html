06Removal of content and takedown

If you believe that something on the site infringes your copyright or other rights, shows you or your personal data without permission, or is unlawful, email the team at marcel@transitionslab.org with the subject “Removal request”, or use the contact form. Please include:

  • the address (URL) of the page and a description of the content;
  • who you are and why the content should be removed (for example, that you own the rights);
  • how we can reach you.

We take content down quickly while we look into it when a request is well founded, and we tell the person who posted it where appropriate. For comments and photos, see also the community guidelines.
