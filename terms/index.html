07Workshop procedures and high voltage

Procedures, job sheets and reference values are general guidance. Specifications differ between machines, so always follow the manufacturer’s service manual for the motorcycle in front of you, including its torque settings, service limits and safety instructions. The traction systems of electric motorcycles operate at voltages that can kill: work on high-voltage components only if you are trained and authorised for it. The full text is in the safety disclaimer.
