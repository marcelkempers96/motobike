01Educational purpose

Moto Zero is an open educational programme of Transitions Lab. It explains how motorcycles work and how they are inspected, diagnosed and repaired. It is a learning resource, not a service manual for any particular machine and not professional advice.

03Workshop procedures

Procedures, job sheets and reference values are general guidance. Specifications differ between machines, so always follow the manufacturer’s service manual for the motorcycle in front of you, including its torque settings, service limits and safety instructions. Use suitable protective equipment and tools.

04High-voltage systems

The traction systems of electric motorcycles operate at voltages that can kill. Work on high-voltage components must only be carried out by people trained and authorised for it, following the manufacturer’s isolation procedure. Module 05 explains the principles; it does not qualify anyone to do the work.

05Interactive tools

The values in the Overview, Cockpit, Wiring Lab and other interactive tools are teaching approximations. They show how systems behave, not the exact figures of a particular motorcycle, and must not be used for design, setup or diagnosis of a real machine.
