How often should I replace the brake disc / rotor on a BMW R 1300 GS / GS Adventure?

As a guide, Every 2-3 sets of pads or below min thickness. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.
