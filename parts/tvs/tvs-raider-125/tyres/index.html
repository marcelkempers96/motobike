Questions

How often should I replace the tyres and inner tubes on a TVS Raider 125?

As a guide, 10,000-25,000 km or 5 years age. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a TVS Raider 125 tyres and inner tubes cost?

Typically US$ 20 to 250, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.

Can I replace the tyres and inner tubes myself?

The job is rated 3 of 5 for difficulty. It is safety-critical: if you are not sure, have a mechanic check your work before you ride.
