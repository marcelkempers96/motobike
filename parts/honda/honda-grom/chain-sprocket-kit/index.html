Questions

How often should I replace the chain and sprocket kit on a Honda Grom?

As a guide, 15,000-25,000 km (replace as a set). The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a Honda Grom chain and sprocket kit cost?

Typically US$ 15 to 150, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.

Can I replace the chain and sprocket kit myself?

The job is rated 3 of 5 for difficulty. It is safety-critical: if you are not sure, have a mechanic check your work before you ride.
