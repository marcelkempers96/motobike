Questions

How often should I replace the brake disc / rotor on a Honda Scoopy?

As a guide, Every 2-3 sets of pads or below min thickness. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a Honda Scoopy brake disc / rotor cost?

Typically US$ 30 to 250, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.

Can I replace the brake disc / rotor myself?

The job is rated 3 of 5 for difficulty. It is safety-critical: if you are not sure, have a mechanic check your work before you ride.
