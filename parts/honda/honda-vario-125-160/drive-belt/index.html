As a guide, Inspect 8,000-10,000 km; replace ~20,000-25,000 km. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

Typically US$ 8 to 40, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.
