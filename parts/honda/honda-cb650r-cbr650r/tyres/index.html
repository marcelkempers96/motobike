Questions

How often should I replace the tyres and inner tubes on a Honda CB650R / CBR650R?

As a guide, 10,000-25,000 km or 5 years age. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a Honda CB650R / CBR650R tyres and inner tubes cost?

Typically US$ 20 to 250, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.
