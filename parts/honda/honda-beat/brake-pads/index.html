Questions

How often should I replace the brake pads on a Honda BeAT?

As a guide, 10,000-20,000 km (min 2 mm). The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a Honda BeAT brake pads cost?

Typically US$ 8 to 60, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.

Can I replace the brake pads myself?

The job is rated 2 of 5 for difficulty. It is safety-critical: if you are not sure, have a mechanic check your work before you ride.
