Questions

How often should I replace the front fork oil seals on a Honda CB125F?

As a guide, 20,000-40,000 km or when leaking. The owner's manual for your model wins, and riding on dusty roads, with a passenger or with heavy loads shortens it.

How much does a Honda CB125F front fork oil seals cost?

Typically US$ 8 to 40, depending on genuine or aftermarket and the shop. Prices are indicative.

Can I replace the front fork oil seals myself?

The job is rated 4 of 5 for difficulty. It is safety-critical: if you are not sure, have a mechanic check your work before you ride.
