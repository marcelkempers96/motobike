How a motorcycle works

Turn a four-stroke, a two-stroke or an electric bike in 3D. Tap any part to see it on its own, look inside it and watch it work.

  • Highlight a whole system: powertrain, chassis, electrics
  • See hidden parts through a see-through shell
  • Run the engine, brake, chain, carburettor, ignition and motor, with live graphs

Build a bike from the ground up

Start with the frame and fit every part in workshop order, then change the specification and see what it does.

Learn all about wiring

In the workshop and on the road

Made for the people who keep machines running

The course

What you learn

Five modules, written to explain the machine well enough to describe it, specify it and repair it. Every chapter has learning goals, worked numbers from a real 125 cc machine, workshop job sheets and questions with answers.

01

Foundations

History, types, anatomy and controls, the physics of riding, and safe workshop practice.
02

The engine

Four-stroke and two-stroke cycles, the top and bottom end, lubrication, cooling and diagnosis.
03

Fuel, ignition, electrics

Mixture, carburettors and injection, ignition, charging, lighting and electrical fault-finding.
04

Drivetrain and chassis

Clutch, gearbox, final drive, geometry, suspension, wheels, tyres, brakes and servicing.
05

Electric motorcycles

Motors, controllers, regeneration, cells, packs and the BMS, charging and high-voltage safety.

Electrical components training

Motors, controllers and battery systems, for working mechanics

Many skilled mechanics learned their trade on engines and gearboxes, by hand. Electric motorcycles bring motors, controllers, battery packs and electronics that few of them have had the chance to train on. We are building practical training modules on these components for informal mechanics, the fundis who keep the fleets moving.

In the meantime, start with the electrics you can already explore.
