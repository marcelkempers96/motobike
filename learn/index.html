How motorcycles work, from the first combustion stroke to the BMS of an electric bike.

Six modules, re-told for the screen: short explanations, live figures you can drive, and a quick check at the end of every chapter. Reference values come from a fuel-injected 125 cc single, the K-Light 125.

Modules

  1. Module 1: Foundations: the machine, its anatomy and the workshop
  2. Module 2: The engine: four-stroke and two-stroke
  3. Module 3: Fuel, air and exhaust
  4. Module 4: Electrical systems: ignition, battery, charging and lighting
  5. Module 5: Drivetrain and chassis
  6. Module 6: Electric motorcycles
